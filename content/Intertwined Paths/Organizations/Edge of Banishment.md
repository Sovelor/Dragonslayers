# Edge of Banishment

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Η Αιχμή της Εξορίας]]

**Role:** Secret elite group within the [[Order of Freedom]] · Founded by [[Sovelor]] · Led by [[Loroves]] since Sovelor's disappearance · Base: the Tower of Exile

## Summary
An utterly secret elite group within the [[Order of Freedom]], founded by [[Sovelor]], the son of Asmodeus, after the attack of the Dragons. It is made up of the Order's most capable agents, works entirely outside the official structures and takes on missions no one else can: it hunts down and banishes evil outsiders, and exists to face threats that neither the Order nor the gods can touch. The Order's own document calls it the *Exile's Edge*.

## Base
The Edge kept the old Tower of Freedom as its base and renamed it the **Tower of Exile**. Very few know that the Tower still operates.

## Leadership
After Sovelor's disappearance, [[Loroves]], his chosen one, took over the leadership. She is utterly devoted to Sovelor, knows the secrets of the blood of Asmodeus, has access to rituals no one else knows, and is the only one who can keep the Edge united.

## Secrecy
Its existence is unknown to all but a few: [[Mahaya (Lord of Time)|Mahaya]], Velatha and some of the old Dragonslayers. Most Branches of the Order don't know it exists, only the Branch Masters have faint suspicions, and only Mahaya, Velatha and Loroves know the truth. The Branches act as protectors, scouts and early-warning systems that feed the Edge with information.

> The Edge of Banishment is the Order's surgical blade. The Branches are its nervous system.
