# World Chronology

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Χρονολόγιο του Κόσμου]]

When each story of **Threads of Fate** takes place. **C** marks a [[Campaigns|campaign]], **S** a [[Side Stories|side story]]. Campaigns that share a number (C2.1, C2.2, …) happen in the same period.

## The age of the Dragonslayers
- **C1 · [[Fate-Touched]]:** the first campaign, the story of the Dragonslayers, told in the manuscript [[The Dragonslayers Saga - Story Timeline|The Dragonslayers Saga]].

## Ten years later
Four campaigns that run side by side, ten years after the finale of *Fate-Touched*, in the same world:
- **C2.1 · [[Old Shadows]]**
- **C2.2 · [[Ancient Blood]]**
- **C2.3 · [[Inner Fire]]**
- **C2.4 · [[A New Beginning]]**

*None yet.* Each [[Side Stories|side story]] is added to the era it belongs to.
