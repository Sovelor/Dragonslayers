# Threads of Fate

Welcome to the wiki of **Threads of Fate**, our D&D project. It spans five campaigns in the same world: *Fate-Touched*, the story of the Dragonslayers, and four campaigns set ten years after its finale.

## The campaigns
- **C1** [[Fate-Touched]]: the original campaign, on which the manuscript "The Dragonslayers Saga" is based
- **C2.1** [[Old Shadows]] · **C2.2** [[Ancient Blood]] · **C2.3** [[Inner Fire]] · **C2.4** [[A New Beginning]]: ten years later, side by side
- [[Campaigns]]: all five at a glance

## Beyond the campaigns

- [[Intertwined Paths]]: characters, places, organizations and lore that span campaigns
