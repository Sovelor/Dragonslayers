# Shadow Altar

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Σκιώδης Βωμός]]

**Role:** Altar behind the door of Tunnel No. 5, holding the Sword of Garagos

## Summary
An altar found behind the door of [[Tunnel No. 5]], holding the [[Sword of Garagos]] (the Shadow Bastardsword, one of the six Shadow Swords of Garagos). While the Six were trying to work out what it was, a gold coin rolled down the stairs behind them and [[Hecate the Prophet]], [[Thalri]], [[Bjorn Brottdottir]] and [[Sol' Goleth]] walked in. Here Hecate stunned the party and drew the shadow out of [[Akis]], getting her last component.

After Hecate left (Session 30), the shadow covering the altar began to steam and hum to life, and the 49 cursed dwarves of Tunnel No. 5 rose around it. [[Aya]], with the help of [[Thoriel]] and [[Petros]], cleansed the altar by the power of Allalme, and the dwarves were purified. %% src:OS-S030 d11 %%
