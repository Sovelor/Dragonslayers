## Summary
An altar found behind the door of [[Tunnel No. 5]], holding the [[Sword of Garagos]]. While the Six were trying to work out what it was, a gold coin rolled down the stairs behind them and [[Hecate the Prophet]], [[Thalri]], [[Bjorn Brottdottir]] and [[Sol' Goleth]] walked in. Here Hecate stunned the party and drew the shadow out of [[Akis]], getting her last component.
