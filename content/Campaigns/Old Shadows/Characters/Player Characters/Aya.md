# Aya Darkspear

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Άγια]]

![[Characters - Aya.jpg|300]]

**Role:** Member of the Incredible Six in *Old Shadows* (she/her) · cleric/paladin of [[Allalme Dragonborn|Allalme]]
**Race:** Human · **Class:** Cleric/Paladin of Allalme (level 9) · **Pronouns:** she/her · **Age:** 25 · **Alignment:** lawful good · **Home:** Mithral Hall · **Languages:** Common, Dwarvish, Celestial

## Summary
A human cleric/paladin raised by a dwarven family in Mithral Hall, devoted to the goddess Allalme. In Session 29 her goddess could not reach her in the tunnels: she prayed at the door of Tunnel 5 and nothing answered. With [[Thoriel]] she blocked the way until [[Petros]] explained what they were doing there with no plan. Facing the Prophet she demanded the truth (*"Enough! No more half-truths, riddles and fake mercy!"*), and [[Hecate the Prophet|Hecate]] showed her in a grim vision who she really is and what she wants. According to [[Vincent Vermilion]]'s profile, she received his sword and feels obligated to him.

## Appearance
White skin, blue eyes and long black hair. She wears mithral full plate and wields a spellstoring great hammer and the Exodus Bastard Sword. Her gear includes a cloak of Charisma, boots of elvenkin, a ring of protection +1, a tiara of wisdom +2 and a bracelet of the sun.

## Personality
Taciturn and decisive; serious when she must be, and the heart of the party when she gets drunk. Her voice is calm and steady.

## Background
She grew up under the roof of the dwarves of Mithral Hall, playing with her brother Aradun and their friends in its corridors. She was taught to fight with dwarven weapons and heavy armour, and with time, far more than the other children, she mastered the techniques: slowly and stubbornly she learned to fight in heavy plate with the great dwarven hammer.
In her teens she felt a calling and, believing it came from Moradin, decided to become his cleric and do his will. Out in the world she felt more at ease in nature and in the forests, watching the animals, and sensed that something did not fit between Moradin and her. In her prayers she often asked him for guidance and saw no result, until she met Thoriel and learned about druids, and felt that nature was close to her and calming.
At the druid circle in Brindinford she learned of Allalme and felt her call strongly. That same night she asked Moradin's forgiveness and devoted herself to Allalme, whom she needed to learn more about. Even then she felt that something was not right...

## Goals & fears
To learn about herself.
