# Aya

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Άγια]]

**Role:** Member of the Amazing Six in *Old Shadows* (she/her) · devoted to a goddess

## Summary
In Session 30 her goddess could not reach her in the tunnels: she prayed at the door of Tunnel 5 and nothing answered. With [[Thoriel]] she blocked the way until [[Petros]] explained what they were doing there with no plan. Facing the Prophet she demanded the truth (*"Enough! No more half-truths, riddles and fake mercy!"*), and [[Hecate the Prophet|Hecate]] showed her in a grim vision who she really is and what she wants. According to [[Vincent Vermilion]]'s profile, she received his sword and feels obligated to him.
