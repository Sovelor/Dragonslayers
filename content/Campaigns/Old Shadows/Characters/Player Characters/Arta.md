# Arta

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Άρτα]]

**Role:** Member of the Amazing Six in *Old Shadows* (she/her) · daughter of [[Bjorn Brottdottir]] · godchild of High Priest [[Drugag]]

## Summary
Grew up in the Undercity of Splendarrmornn, believing her parents were bakers; when she visited Bjorn's Bakery in Session 30 it had been closed for a long time. In the Underdark cave of glowing mushrooms she felt she had been there before: her mother used to bring her to this sacred place of prayer when she was very young. At the altar her father begged her: *"Stand at your father's side. Accept your destiny."*

## Appearances
- [[Session 030 - The Descent into the Shadow]]: visits her closed childhood home; remembers the sacred cave; her father asks her to join him
