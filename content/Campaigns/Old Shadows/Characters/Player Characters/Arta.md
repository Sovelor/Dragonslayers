# Arta Brottdottir

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Άρτα]]

![[Characters - Arta.png|300]]

**Role:** Member of the Incredible Six in *Old Shadows* (she/her) · daughter of [[Bjorn Brottdottir]] · godchild of High Priest [[Drugag]]
**Race:** Dwarf · **Class:** Ranger, Wild Plains Outrider (level 9) · **Pronouns:** she/her · **Age:** 52 · **Alignment:** neutral good · **Home:** the Undercity (also called the Lower City) of [[Splendarrmornn]] · **Languages:** Common, Dwarven

## Summary
A dwarven ranger from the Undercity of Splendarrmornn, raised in the shadow of the enigmatic **Keepers of the Secrets**. Haunted by fragmented memories and the unexplained absence of her mother, she seeks the truth of her past and wants to find out whether her destiny is truly her own.
Grew up in the Undercity of Splendarrmornn, believing her parents were bakers; when she visited Bjorn's Bakery in Session 29 it had been closed for a long time. In the Underdark cave of glowing mushrooms she felt she had been there before: her mother used to bring her to this sacred place of prayer when she was very young. At the altar her father begged her: *"Stand at your father's side. Accept your destiny."*

When she joined the party, and for a long time after, she believed her parents were bakers; she knows the truth about her father, [[Bjorn Brottdottir]], only after later revelations (DM). She was not aboard *The Dragon Turtle*: the others met her in Brindinford, where she saved [[Petros]]'s life from a ratman's attack and joined them during their quest to find out what was going on in the city (DM).

The Undercity is also called the Lower City; it is the same place, named differently by many (DM).

## Appearance
A sturdy, broad-shouldered dwarf with a compact, muscular build, shaped by years of hunting monsters in the Deep Roads. Her face is strong-featured and weathered, with a firm jaw, prominent cheekbones and an expression that rarely betrays more than she intends. Her copper-red hair is worn long, with a carefully braided beard; her dark green eyes are sharp and watchful, often distant and contemplative. Her skin is fair and slightly ruddy.
She usually wears a chain shirt under a distinctive purple cloak and carries an oversized dwarven urgrosh. There is something guarded about her look, a quiet severity that sometimes softens into unexpected warmth.
Her voice is low and slightly husky, with the strong, earthy accent of Splendarrmornn's Lower City. She speaks in short, deliberate sentences, bluntly and practically, with a dry, understated sarcasm; when angry her words turn sharp, when uncomfortable she goes quiet, and when she speaks of her mother her voice softens.

## Personality
Pragmatic, stubborn, fiercely independent, with a dry, occasionally biting sense of humour. A survivor of the Deep Roads, she trusts her instincts, her urgrosh and Rusk more readily than the words of others. Reserved and guarded, she struggles with trust and vulnerability, shaped by fragmented childhood memories and the cold discipline of the Keepers, yet beneath the hard surface she is deeply loyal and compassionate. She values deeds over words and loyalty over authority, and increasingly questions the truths she was taught never to question.
She is observant and economical in speech and movement. In any unfamiliar room she checks the exits, shadows and possible threats, and keeps her back to solid stone. She folds her arms when listening, strokes or adjusts her braided beard when thinking, and checks the edge of her urgrosh even when it needs no attention. When troubled she goes silent rather than explain, and when asked about her past she sometimes hesitates, as though searching for a memory that should be there. Among trusted friends she softens; small kindnesses come more easily than grand declarations.
- **Ideal:** Freedom. No oath, bloodline or ancient secret should decide who a person must become; loyalty must be earned, not commanded.
- **Bond:** Rusk, her faithful rothe, her oldest and most trusted companion.
- **Flaw:** Distrust. Raised among the Keepers, she buries her doubts and hides her feelings, and sometimes mistakes vulnerability for weakness.

## Background
Arta was born in the Lower City of Splendarrmornn, among miners, labourers and craftsmen who keep the mountain's less welcoming inhabitants where they belong. She knew the Deep Roads beneath it as a living landscape of sounds, air and hidden passages, but never had the same certainty about her own past. Much of her childhood is indistinct: memories come in fragments, places feel familiar without explanation, and she cannot remember things she feels certain she should. At the centre of it is her mother: her face and voice are hard to recall and her disappearance is entirely unexplained, yet the fragments that survive hold warmth and unconditional tenderness, unlike almost anything else from those years. Her father, Björn, never gave a satisfactory explanation, and in his world questions were not always welcome.
Through Björn her life became entangled with the **Keepers of the Secrets**, a secretive organisation active in the hidden affairs of Splendarrmornn. They taught discipline, discretion, obedience and duty, and that not every question deserves an answer. On her left shoulder they set a magical tattoo that forbade her to speak of her past and of the Keepers themselves, so that the line between what she could not remember and what she was forbidden to say blurred. Whether her missing past was deliberately concealed, magically altered or simply lost, she cannot tell.
She became an exterminator of the Lower City, keeping the Deep Roads beyond it safe from wandering monsters, vermin and other subterranean threats, and became an accomplished ranger with an oversized dwarven urgrosh. She found the work comforting: monsters are at least straightforward about their intentions. Her steadiest companion in these years was **Rusk**, a sturdy and stubborn rothe.
Then the Keepers sent her to find and bring to justice a dwarf named Rockgar Pickaxeson, whom the records of Splendarrmornn say died in the catastrophe of [[Tunnel No. 5]]. They told her he had caused it and had vanished into the world above under another name: [[Petros]] Axinakis. She took the task, with Rusk and her purple cloak, and followed his trail into the company of the five strangers who became the Incredible Six. On meeting him she saved him from a wererat assassin instead. In Khudrukhar [[Aya]] removed the tattoo that bound her to silence.

> [!info] Rumours and open mysteries
> **Rumours:** she once worked as an exterminator beneath the Lower City, and the monsters were generally more frightened of her than she of them; her rothe has better judgement than most dwarves; she was sent from Splendarrmornn to bring a murderer to justice; she once bore a strange tattoo on her left shoulder that would not let her speak certain truths, gone now, though some wonder whether the silence went with it; she belongs to an ancient order of dwarves who guard terrible secrets beneath the mountain; her father is a powerful man in Splendarrmornn whom nobody wants to discuss; she trusts her rothe more than the gods.
> **Open mysteries:** who her mother was and what happened to her; why she remembers so little of her childhood, and whether her memories were taken; what Björn knows about his wife's disappearance and why he will not speak of her; whether Björn ever truly loved her or she was always an instrument of Shar and the Keepers; the true purpose of the Keepers and the forbidden knowledge beneath Splendarrmornn; whether other influences of the Keepers remain in her though the tattoo is gone; and how much of her life, and how many of her choices, were shaped by them.

## Goals & fears
- To uncover the truth of her forgotten childhood, recover her lost memories and learn what happened to her mother.
- To confront Björn: why he hid so much, what part the Keepers played in her life, and whether anything of the father she once knew remains under his devotion to Shar.
- To understand the Keepers' true purpose and free herself of their influence: the tattoo is gone, but she must still overcome the obedience and distrust instilled since childhood.
- To protect Rusk and the Incredible Six, who show her a loyalty freely given rather than commanded, and ultimately to discover who Arta truly is.
- **Fears:** learning what became of her mother, and that her father knows exactly what happened and his silence hides something unforgivable; becoming like Björn, so devoted to duty that she cannot tell loyalty from servitude; and losing Rusk and the Six. Thraldour's fate has shown her there are losses worse than death.

## Relationships
- **[[Bjorn Brottdottir]]:** her father, a stern patriarch and high priest of Shar, once an unquestioned authority and now a figure of betrayal and conflicted loyalty. He sees her as capable, strong-willed and increasingly troublesome; whether any paternal affection survives is uncertain. Enemy?
- **Her mother (name unknown):** absent, her fate a mystery; the fragments Arta remembers carry warmth and tenderness.
- **Rusk:** her faithful rothe, trusted friend and constant travelling partner. He considers her a satisfactory dwarf, if occasionally unreasonable about which tunnels to enter.
- **[[Petros]] (Rockgar Pickaxeson):** originally the target of her assignment; she saved him instead from a wererat assassin and he became a trusted friend, bound to her by their shared homeland and troubled pasts. He sees in her a reflection of his own, and worries her determination may lead toward the same darkness he unleashed.
- **[[Aya]]:** a trusted friend who freed her from the tattoo. Aya sees a good person whose compassion was constrained by years of enforced obedience.
- **[[Thoriel]]:** her gentleness gives Arta a rare sense of emotional safety, though her openness about feelings can make the guarded dwarf uncomfortable. Thoriel wishes Arta would trust others more readily and values her loyalty and her tenderness towards Rusk.
- **Aradun:** a dependable brother-in-arms whose honour, courage and dwarven traditions she respects; he respects her, though her growing disillusionment with her homeland's authorities troubles him.
- **Thraldour (Thrall):** a dear companion whose mind was shattered in Khudrukhar; his body rests in Brindinford, and she has not abandoned hope for his recovery.
- **[[Freiderikos]]:** the newest of the company, whose carefree manner and peculiar upbringing hint at a more complicated story. He finds her intimidating, competent and hard to impress, and is beginning to appreciate her loyalty.
- **[[Hecate the Prophet|Hecate]]:** an enemy allied with her father, whose actions show the consequences of the darkness Arta was raised to serve.
- **The Keepers of the Secrets:** enemies; once her masters, now the people from whom she seeks answers.
