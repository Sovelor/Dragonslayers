# Petros Axinakis

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Πέτρος]]

![[Characters - Petros.png|300]]

**Role:** Member of the Incredible Six in *Old Shadows* (he/him) · survivor of the Collapse of Tunnel No. 5
**Race:** Dwarf · **Class:** Wizard, Master Specialist in Transmutation (level 9) · **Pronouns:** he/him · **Age:** 60 · **Alignment:** lawful neutral · **Home:** [[Splendarrmornn]] · **Languages:** Common, Dwarven, Goblin, Draconic, Elven, Primal (Terran)

## Summary
A disgraced dwarven transmuter living under an assumed name, seeking to uncover the darkness that destroyed fifty of his kin and to make sure it never happens again. He was born Rockgar Pickaxeson, a former Mining Director of Splendarrmornn, and is now a wandering adventurer and errant wizard.
The Prophet's last component was trapped inside his familiar rat, [[Akis]]: in Session 29 [[Hecate the Prophet|Hecate]] stunned the party, took the rat and drew out the shadow that lived in it. She calls him "Rokgar" and told him: *"This time, Petros... there is no prophecy for you."* In the last tunnel he faced the ghosts of the **49 dwarf miners** who died under his supervision as Tunnel Foreman in the Collapse of Tunnel No. 5, calling each name until the ghosts stepped aside. His sister [[Thalri]] stands with the Prophet.

## Appearance
A short, stocky, broad-shouldered dwarf with a face marked by years of hard work and harder experiences: a prominent nose, expressive brows and warm, lively brown eyes that often betray a weariness his smile tries to hide. His head is completely bald and, most unusually for a dwarf, he is clean-shaven, without even a trace of a beard. His skin is fair and weathered, with the occasional scar and wrinkle.
He wears blue-and-gold wizard's robes, carries a wooden staff and keeps his spellbook fastened to his belt. His rat familiar, [[Akis]], is rarely far from his shoulder. His voice is a warm, expressive baritone with a dwarven accent softened by years among surface dwellers; when he is distressed or angry the playful tone disappears and the deep, measured, authoritative voice of the Mining Director he once was takes over.

## Personality
Eccentric, witty and relentlessly cheerful, Petros hides a deeply troubled soul behind terrible jokes and theatrical charm. He is animated, rarely lets a silence pass without a joke, a pun or some unnecessary display of magic, and speaks to Akis as though the rat were a respected colleague. Much of the cheerfulness is deliberate (he would rather be thought a fool than let his companions carry the weight of his troubles), but it is also genuine. He is fiercely loyal, endlessly curious and a firm believer in Fate. When genuinely troubled his jovial manner disappears and the disciplined Mining Director briefly resurfaces.
- **Ideal:** Fate. Mystra weaves the tapestry of existence, and even tragedy has its place within her design; our duty is to choose what we make of the threads we are given.
- **Bond:** the Fifty, the dwarves who perished in Tunnel Five. He is determined to uncover the truth and ensure their sacrifice was not in vain.
- **Flaw:** guilt. He mistakes suffering for atonement, believing that forgiving himself would mean forgetting those he failed.

## Background
Petros Axinakis was born Rockgar Pickaxeson, son of Rurik (a senior mine overseer) and Marnil (an architectural engineer) Pickaxeson, in the dwarven city of Splendarrmornn in the mountain kingdom of Ammarindar. His fascination with stone led him to the city's College of Transmutation, where, a miner's son with a provincial accent, he found that making people laugh worked better than demanding respect. He became an accomplished transmuter and believed no problem could resist enough knowledge, preparation and a properly applied spell.
By the time he was Mining Overseer he was known among his miners as Rockgar Stoneface, with an almost unreasonable insistence on safety. Two years before his adventures began, a map with no sender's name and a broken seal appeared on his desk, showing a long-forgotten passage. He led a crew of fifty dwarves, among them his closest family friend Ruby Metallson, into what became Tunnel Five. Deep within they found a door that looked dwarven but was subtly wrong, its runes crooked and mirrored. He opened it. Beyond lay a darkness that seemed to consume the very certainty of existence. Ruby shouted for him to collapse the passage and Rockgar obeyed, turning the stone above them to mud and back to rock. Fifty dwarves died; only Rockgar, Ruby and his rat Akis survived. Whether the collapse saved Splendarrmornn from something worse is unknown.
The official report, submitted by Ruby, recorded that Rockgar had died heroically. There was a funeral with an empty coffin. Rockgar could not bring himself to return: he shaved his beard, took the human-sounding name Petros Axinakis (*Petros* means stone, and *Axinakis* keeps something of the pickaxe) and left Splendarrmornn. Where Rockgar had demanded respect, Petros sought laughter; where Rockgar had believed he could control the world, Petros placed his faith in Fate and in Mystra.
Every night he lights four candles (the first crack, the wrong echo, the silence and the names), sets out a silver bowl of cold water, shaves his head and face, and recites the names of the fifty dwarves who died under his command. Akis, once a white rat, began changing after the collapse: his fur darkened from grey to black and his eyes sometimes reflect a faint violet light, though magical examination found nothing.
His travels brought him aboard *The Dragon Turtle*, which was wrecked (his profile says it was bound for Baldur's Gate; the DM has it sailing from Baldur's Gate to Luskan). That night he followed the other castaways, Thraldour, [[Aya]], Aradun, Havi and [[Thoriel]], to their camp and revealed himself as one more survivor of the wreck; later, in Brindinford, [[Arta]] saved his life from a ratman's attack and joined them. Together they became the Incredible Six. Their adventures led through haunted forests, corrupted lighthouses, the lands around Brindinford and the ruins of Khudrukhar, where they lost Thraldour; on the return to Brindinford they gained [[Freiderikos]].

> [!warning] Conflicting accounts
> The profile speaks of "fifty dwarves" who died in Tunnel Five but also of "the forty-nine dwarves who perished"; the session notes and the rest of the profile say fifty. Fifty is used.

> [!info] Rumours and open mysteries
> **Rumours:** he is a dwarven wizard who shaved his beard to pass as a human; his rat is the real wizard and the dwarf only carries the spellbook; he was once someone important but becomes interested in the weather when asked; he lights four candles every night and whispers names into the dark; his familiar used to be white; he is never afraid of dying, only of being the last one alive; when things turn desperate the laughter stops and a different dwarf takes his place; he is not running from his past but waiting for it to catch up.
> **Open mysteries:** who sent the map and why was he chosen; what Hecate will do with the darkness she took from Akis; what has become of Thalri and how deep she has fallen under the Keepers of the Secrets; who in Splendarrmornn knows Rockgar is alive; whether Petros follows Mystra's path or his faith blinds him to darker powers; and, if Rockgar died in Tunnel Five, who exactly is Petros Axinakis?

## Goals & fears
- To uncover the truth behind the catastrophe of Tunnel Five: who sent the map, what lay beyond the false door, and whether the darkness was ever truly contained.
- To protect his companions and prevent others from suffering the consequences of his mistakes.
- To understand Mystra's design and whether Fate has a purpose for him beyond surviving, and to live with the memory of the Fifty without letting their deaths define the rest of his life.
- **Fears:** that the darkness was never contained and he only delayed a greater catastrophe; losing those he loves; facing the family who buried him; that his suffering has been meaningless; and that one day he might forgive himself.

## Relationships
- **Rurik, Marnil, Berrin and [[Thalri]] Pickaxeson:** his father (senior mine overseer), mother (architectural engineer), older brother (a miner of Splendarrmornn) and younger sister (a student of the College of Transmutation). They believe he is dead.
- **Ruby Metallson:** his closest family friend, fellow survivor of Tunnel Five, keeper of his secret and the man who helped him disappear
- **[[Akis]]:** his faithful rat familiar and closest confidant, bearer of a mysterious darkness
- **[[Arta]]:** fellow adventurer and a connection to the homeland he abandoned
- **Thraldour:** a former companion of the Six whose fate weighs on him
- **[[Aya]]:** the party's moral compass, who thinks he made an impossible choice and fears his guilt may drive him to sacrifice himself needlessly
- **Aradun, [[Thoriel]], [[Freiderikos]]:** fellow adventurers; Thoriel finds his secretive streak frustrating and wishes he could see that he deserves the compassion he offers others
