# Petros

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Πέτρος]]

**Role:** Member of the Amazing Six in *Old Shadows* (he/him) · survivor of the Collapse of Tunnel No. 5

## Summary
The Prophet's last component is hidden inside him: in Session 30 [[Hecate the Prophet|Hecate]] stunned the party, took his familiar [[Akis]] and drew out the shadow that lived in the rat. She calls him "Rokgar" and told him: *"This time, Petros... there is no prophecy for you."* In the last tunnel he faced the ghosts of the **50 dwarf miners** who died with him in the Collapse of Tunnel No. 5, calling each name until the ghosts stepped aside. His sister [[Thalri]] stands with the Prophet.
