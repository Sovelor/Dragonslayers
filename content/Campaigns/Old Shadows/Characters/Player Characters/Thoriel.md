# Thoriel Meila

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Θόριελ]]

![[Characters - Thoriel.png|300]]

**Role:** Member of the Amazing Six in *Old Shadows* (she/her) · calls on Sylvanus and the Lady of the Forest
**Race:** Elf · **Class:** Druid (Shape Shifter), level 9, believer of Sylvanus · **Pronouns:** she/her · **Age:** 25 · **Alignment:** Chaotic Good · **Home:** the Reaching Woods · **Languages:** Common, Elvish, Druidic

## Summary
A young elf druid from the Reaching Woods who left her grandmother's tea shop to find her adventurer parents. In the last tunnel of Session 29 she saw the animals she had failed to save and called on the Lady of the Forest in anguish and anger. At the door of Tunnel 5 she argued with [[Petros]] and then, for the first time, felt **Sylvanus** beside her, a warm hand on her shoulder strengthening her magic. According to [[Vincent Vermilion]]'s profile she does not trust him.

## Appearance
Very long green hair reaching beneath her waist, and pale green eyes, the colour of freshly watered grass during the first spring rains. Her skin is paler than a usual elf's, a porcelain complexion. Her pointed, delicate features blend with a fresh and innocent look.

She dresses simply and comfortably, but very colourfully: a light blue dress against a black wooden breastplate. Round her neck hangs her focus, a necklace of bright orange amber with a butterfly still trapped inside; on her forehead is a tiara with magical properties. Her pouch is full of stitched, colourful flowers and carries a longbow and an alchemist's lab.

Her voice is cheerful and young, with an energetic, enthusiastic tone.

## Personality
Outgoing, friendly and always smiling when she meets someone. Her young age makes her innocent but not naïve. She opens up fast to those she considers friends and is an open book, with no secrets or ulterior motives beyond friendship and exploration. She always tries to help and understand someone in need; nature and animals are always a priority. She would follow a friend into the most dangerous places if they need her help and support, and she is a great listener.

She is energetic and curious, easily distracted at times but never lost. She always wants to drink tea, or is making some (jasmine is her favourite). She sometimes reads a small booklet that looks like a diary, or writes letters to her grandmother. Her movements are fluid and graceful, with a joyful walk.

At times her youth and inexperience make her stubborn, and she has outbursts of emotion she cannot hide. When she is angry with someone she gives them the silent treatment, but it never lasts long.

**Ideal:** always tell the truth and be yourself; never stray from nature and Sylvanus' teachings. **Bond:** her grandmother Lahz' Akku, with whom she grew up. **Flaw:** innocence; she believes everyone has good in their heart and always hopes for the best.

## Background
Thoriel grew up in the Reaching Woods, a forest of thick young trees on the banks of the River Chionthar, living in complete isolation with her grandmother **Lahz' Akku**, an elf archdruid. Her grandmother kept a tea shop where they both lived and worked, a small sanctuary for passing travellers and Thoriel's only contact with other people, races and stories. Winters were harder, with fewer visitors and more dangerous creatures roaming free. Lahz' Akku always protected her, but kept many secrets and refused to tell them.

Thoriel's parents, **Oarelion** (father) and **Vaelilan** (mother), left about 15 years ago on a travelling mission and have not returned; their only contact is an occasional postcard from the places they visit. She remembers them: once they all lived somewhere in a city, until they suddenly had to flee an attack by a black dragon. She was too young to remember the city's name. Her grandmother refuses to speak of her parents or their disappearance and changes the subject. One of the few things she has shared is that everyone in the family has the colours of the season they were born in, which explains Thoriel's green hair and eyes (born in spring).

Thoriel never had druidic powers, though her grandmother always made her study and prepare. Apart from Lahz' Akku and some travellers she was lonely, her only company the adventure books of the tea shop; she spent most of her free time in the butterfly conservatory her grandmother built her to make winter less dreary.

One fateful day her druidic nature unlocked and she began to experience the world differently. One of her new abilities let her discover her grandmother's secret room in the tea shop's basement, where she found a mysterious chest and many encrypted letters between her grandmother and someone called "-M". In an emotional outburst with her grandmother her first shape-shifting happened, and she was stuck in animal form. Not knowing what was happening, she ran into the forest and met a young paladin of Tyr named **Belren**, whom she wounded seriously. The shock gave her partial control of her shape-shifting, and she returned to the tea shop with the paladin. Belren stayed for a while and convinced her there is more to the world than the forest, and that she could help people like her parents. That sparked her own adventure of discovery and reunion. After a month of travel away from the Reaching Woods she boarded a ship bound for Baldur's Gate; the ship was sunk and almost everyone died. That is how she met [[Petros]], [[Arta]], [[Aya]], **Aradun** and, later, [[Freiderikos]].

## Goals & fears
She wants, more than anything, to find her parents and go on an adventure with them, and to make her grandmother proud of who she is becoming; to explore the world, see the places she read about in books, and make many friends. She fears disappointing her grandmother and parents, and not being strong enough as a druid and failing to save nature and animals. She also worries about the dispute between her grandmother and the rabbits back at the tea shop.
