# Thoriel

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Θόριελ]]

**Role:** Member of the Amazing Six in *Old Shadows* (she/her) · calls on Sylvanus and the Lady of the Forest

## Summary
In the last tunnel of Session 30 she saw the animals she had failed to save and called on the Lady of the Forest in anguish and anger. At the door of Tunnel 5 she argued with [[Petros]] and then, for the first time, felt **Sylvanus** beside her, a warm hand on her shoulder strengthening her magic. According to [[Vincent Vermilion]]'s profile she does not trust him.

## Appearances
