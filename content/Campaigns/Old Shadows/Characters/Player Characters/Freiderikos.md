# Freiderikos Nerasimis

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Φρειδερίκος]]

![[Characters - Freiderikos.png|300]]

**Role:** Member of the Incredible Six in *Old Shadows* · uses *Regroup*
**Race:** Human · **Class:** Sorcerer, Incantatrix (level 9); a former farmer, now a professional adventurer · **Pronouns:** he/him · **Home:** Haston · **Languages:** Common, Halfling, Draconic

## Summary
A human sorcerer from the idyllic farming village of **Haston**, raised in a remarkably perfect childhood. Blessed with an extraordinary singing voice and a natural affinity for lightning magic, he travels the world seeking adventure, friendship and heroic deeds, and has joined the Incredible Six.
In Session 29 he pulled half the party out of a shadow ooze with *Regroup* before they blasted it apart. His verdict on the whole descent: *"That was... unpleasant."*
In Session 30, when [[Thoriel]] cast *Barkskin* on him, his skin grew **blue dragon scales**, and his staff turned out to be made of dragon bone, from a gold dragon.

## Appearance
A youthful human of average height and lean build, with a warm, sun-kissed complexion and an almost permanently cheerful expression. Short, tousled black hair, a magnificent curled handlebar moustache and bright, expressive dark eyes that always seem on the verge of laughter frame his face; his most distinctive feature is an enormous, infectious grin, worn with the confidence of someone who has never seriously considered that things might go wrong.
He dresses as flamboyantly as he behaves: a bright golden-yellow cloak fastened with blue gemstone brooches and a decorative gold chain, over a loose, open-collared white shirt, with a blue diamond-shaped pendant at his neck. An oversized, floppy, pointed yellow wizard's hat with a dangling blue gemstone completes the look.
His voice is a bright, warm tenor with a melodic cadence and a cheerful rural Haston accent. It is extraordinarily clear and resonant, with a natural singing quality that can captivate an audience without effort; he often slips into song mid-conversation and raises his voice dramatically when excited. Even when frightened or uncertain, his tone keeps a hopeful warmth.

## Personality
Exuberant, charismatic, hopelessly optimistic and blessed with an almost supernatural ability to see the best in everyone. He approaches life as one grand adventure, preferably accompanied by music, laughter and the occasional spectacular thunderclap. He greets strangers like old friends, speaks with animated gestures and punctuates his words with a flourish of his hands or an enthusiastic adjustment of his moustache.
Having grown up in sheltered, accommodating Haston, he is remarkably gullible: he takes people at their word, believes even improbable stories, and has real difficulty recognising deception, sarcasm or malice. He is not unintelligent; beneath his naivety lie a sharp, creative mind and an extraordinary talent for magic. His optimism is neither an act nor a disguise: he simply believes the world is a wonderful place.
- **Ideal (Fortune):** life is a grand adventure, and Lady Luck favours those brave enough to embrace it; every stranger is a potential friend, every misfortune a good story waiting to happen.
- **Bond (Haston):** his beloved hometown, family and the villagers who raised him; he carries their kindness wherever he goes and wants to make them proud.
- **Flaw (Naivety):** having never truly known cruelty or betrayal, he trusts far too easily, mistaking deception for sincerity and recklessness for courage.

## Background
Freiderikos Nerasimis was born in Haston, a small farming village of rolling green hills, plentiful harvests and remarkably pleasant people. His parents adored him, his neighbours encouraged him, and even the village's grumpiest residents could not stay cross in his presence. Unknown to him, an ancient blue dragon watches over Haston: it is his distant ancestor, and the villagers, well aware of it, made it their shared duty to give him an exceptionally happy childhood. They genuinely loved the boy, but the thought of displeasing an ancient dragon added considerable motivation. Freiderikos never suspected a thing.
From an early age he showed two extraordinary talents: a magnificent singing voice and an instinctive mastery of magic. Lightning danced between his fingers and thunder answered him; to him magic was music, and thunder simply the sky applauding. His ancestor hoped this power would make a mighty, ambitious sorcerer worthy of its bloodline, and was considerably less delighted when he used it to entertain children at a village festival. He embraced the teachings of **Tymora**, Lady Luck, believing that fortune favours the bold.
Inspired by heroic ballads, he decided to become a hero himself. When troubling rumours came from Brindinford, of sinister creatures and demons threatening its people, he set off at once, only to find that the town had already been saved: the Incredible Six had defeated a terrible mind flayer and the demonic forces. Learning that they had gone on to the ancient dwarven stronghold of Khudrukhar, he followed them and met them inside Khudrukhar, the day after they had slain **Nightscale**, the Shadow Dragoness, at a terrible price: Thraldour had survived in body, but his mind was shattered. Starstruck and a little shaken, Freiderikos offered them his friendship, his magic and his talents, and joined the Incredible Six, taking Thraldour's place.

> - Some believe his singing voice is a gift from the heavens; others, that it is enchanted.
> - What does the dragon intend for its descendant? Will Freiderikos ever learn why Haston worked so hard to keep him happy, and how will he take it? What will happen at his first real betrayal or loss, and will he embrace, reject or redeem his draconic heritage?

## Goals & fears
He wants to become a true hero like those in the songs of his childhood: to travel the world, defeat terrible monsters, rescue those in need and return to Haston with tales that would make his family proud. He hopes to earn his place among the Incredible Six, the heroes he once admired from afar, and to bring laughter and warmth back into a company that has suffered far more than he can yet comprehend. He wants to master the lightning and thunder that come so naturally to him and, above all, to make the world a happier place.
He has never had much reason to be afraid. His greatest fear is failing those who depend on him; he also fears disappointing the Six, knowing that he has taken the place of Thraldour. More deeply, though he has never put it into words, he fears a world in which kindness is not enough.

## Relationships
- [[Petros]]: a fellow adventurer and wizard whose magic and eccentric personality he greatly admires. Petros finds his boundless enthusiasm both amusing and strangely comforting, and sees in him an innocence he lost long ago.
- [[Arta]]: a formidable dwarven ranger whose practical nature contrasts sharply with his enthusiasm. She cannot quite understand how someone with so little common sense has survived this long, but cannot deny that his heart is in the right place.
- [[Aya]]: a paladin he admires for her courage, compassion and dedication to protecting others; she admires his genuine kindness and willingness to help.
- [[Aradun]]: a dragon shaman whose strength and martial prowess he finds immensely impressive; Aradun is surprisingly supportive of his theatrics and temper.
- [[Thoriel]]: an elven druid whose gentle nature and affinity for the natural world he appreciates; she finds his warm optimism and lack of secrets refreshing.
- **The Nerasimis family**: his loving parents and family in Haston, who have always encouraged his dreams.
- **The villagers of Haston**: his beloved friends and neighbours, who adore him as the kindest, most cheerful young man their village has ever produced.
- **An ancient blue dragon** (name unknown): his distant ancestor and the secret protector of Haston, who wants him to become a powerful, ruthless figure worthy of its bloodline and finds his cheerful kindness an utterly baffling disappointment. Freiderikos knows nothing of it.
- **Enemies:** Shar and her servants; the **Keepers of the Secrets**; [[Hecate the Prophet|Hecate]], whose cruelty threatens the friends he has come to cherish.
