# Akis

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Άκις]]

![[Characters - Akis.png|300]]

**Role:** [[Petros]]'s familiar, a rat
**Race:** Rat (white), magical beast, formerly a vessel of Shar's darkness · **Occupation:** arcane familiar · **Pronouns:** he/him · **Age:** 20 · **Alignment:** lawful neutral · **Home:** [[Splendarrmornn]] · **Languages:** Rat
**Where found:** almost always perched on Petros's shoulder, nestled in his robes or asleep among the pages of his spellbook; when apart from his master, investigating unattended food and dark corners · **Attitude to the party:** friendly

## Summary
A small white rat and the faithful familiar of [[Petros]] (born Rockgar Pickaxeson), once the unwilling vessel of a fragment of Shar's darkness. Behind his unassuming look is a poetic, melancholic soul: the silent witness to his master's triumphs, tragedies and the ghosts that follow him.
Hecate took Akis in Session 29 and drew out the shadow that lived inside him. The rat turned white again, and the Prophet had her last component.

## Appearance
A small, slender rat with soft snow-white fur, delicate pink paws, a long hairless tail and bright, inquisitive eyes. His whiskers twitch constantly and his nose is always busy investigating the world around him. After the catastrophe of Tunnel Five his fur gradually darkened to an unnatural black, sometimes with a faint violet glimmer in his eyes; after Hecate extracted the fragment of Shar's darkness his coat returned to its pristine white. He bears no marks of his former corruption: to a casual observer he is an ordinary rat, if an unusually attentive one.
To most people he speaks only in soft squeaks, nervous chirps and the occasional indignant chitter. To Petros, through their familiar bond, his voice is soft, low and strangely ageless, slow and almost musical, with no regional accent. His speech is poetic and melancholic, rich in images of death, memory, shadow and sorrow, as though every sentence were an epitaph.

## Personality
Quiet, observant and deeply melancholic, with the soul of a poet and the instincts of a rat. He is fiercely loyal, endlessly curious, occasionally mischievous and far more perceptive than he looks. He views the world with a mixture of tenderness and pessimism, finding beauty in fleeting moments and sorrow in their passing, but has never entirely surrendered to despair. Above all he is a witness: he neither judges nor forgives easily, but remembers everything.
He is happiest on Petros's shoulder or tucked into a forgotten pocket, and rarely stays still: whiskers twitching, nose testing the air, paws grooming his fur. He steals crumbs, investigates unattended food, explores places larger creatures cannot follow, and now and then tugs Petros's beardless chin or nibbles his ear for attention. When others speak of sorrow, death or things best left forgotten, he grows perfectly still and watches faces. During Petros's nightly ritual he is utterly silent: he never interrupts the recitation of the Fifty, never scurries away, and never sleeps until the last name has been spoken. When his master is troubled he presses himself against him.
- **Ideal:** Remembrance. All things perish, but so long as one soul remembers, nothing is truly lost.
- **Bond:** Devotion. Petros (Rockgar) is his master, companion and entire world; Akis will remain beside him until the last candle burns out.
- **Flaw:** Melancholy. Having seen so much suffering, he sees the shadow of loss in every happy moment and finds it easier to mourn what may be lost than to rejoice in what remains.

## Background
Akis was summoned by Rockgar Pickaxeson during his first year at the College of Transmutation in [[Splendarrmornn]], as part of his studies in binding a familiar. Through the bond he could sense his master's moods and fears, and he watched Rockgar grow from an uncertain student into a respected wizard. When Rockgar returned to the mines as Mining Overseer, Akis rode on his shoulder and came to know the mountain, its forges and the dwarves who worked under his master.
He went with Rockgar and the fifty dwarves into the forgotten depths of [[Tunnel No. 5]]. He sensed the wrongness of the door before the others did, and when it was opened he saw the darkness come out. As Rockgar tried to seal the passage, a fragment of the darkness escaped the collapsing tunnel and took refuge in Akis, who became its unwilling vessel. Fifty dwarves died; Rockgar and Ruby Metallson survived, and so did Akis. His white fur then turned grey and then black, with a violet light in his eyes now and then, and the fragment of Shar stayed hidden even from his master's magical examinations.
When Rockgar abandoned Splendarrmornn, Akis went with him and was the only companion to see him shave his beard, renounce his name and become the cheerful wanderer Petros Axinakis. Every night he watches the ritual of remembrance: four candles, a silver bowl, cold water, a razor and the fifty names.

> [!info] Rumours and open mysteries
> **Rumours:** the rat watches people with an unsettling attentiveness, as though listening to thoughts they have not yet spoken; he never sleeps when his master lights the four candles, but sits and listens as though the names meant something to him; his eyes once shone violet in the dark; Petros talks to him as to an old philosopher, and the rat's advice is either very poor or often ignored; he was touched by a goddess of darkness and another goddess came to reclaim what was hidden in him; he sometimes sits on an empty chair in Bridingfort beside a warrior who still breathes but no longer answers; whenever Petros laughs, the rat looks at him first, as if to judge whether the laughter is real; and some whisper that the rat remembers things his master has spent years trying to forget.
> **Open mysteries:** why the fragment of Shar's darkness took refuge in Akis (chance, his bond with Rockgar, or something arranged); what he experienced while carrying a piece of a goddess, and whether something of its nature became part of him; whether he saw something in Tunnel Five that even Rockgar never saw; whether Hecate took only the fragment of Shar or something else entwined with him; whether he is truly free of the darkness now his fur is white again; and what Hecate will do with the fragment she reclaimed, and what part Akis unknowingly played in it.

## Goals & fears
- To witness the day when Rockgar laughs again without sorrow hiding beneath it; he asks little of the world: a warm shoulder, a few stolen crumbs, and a master who might learn to live rather than merely endure.
- **Fears:** losing Rockgar above all, and that his master's guilt will one day drive him to a sacrifice from which even Fate cannot deliver him; helplessness, having seen death and suffering with no strength to prevent them and no words to comfort those left behind; being separated from his master; the return of the darkness that once lived in him and what purpose his unwitting service to Shar may yet fulfil; and above all oblivion, the forgetting that follows death: that the names of the Fifty fall silent and Rockgar Pickaxeson disappears beneath the weight of Petros Axinakis.

## Relationships
- **[[Petros]] (Rockgar Pickaxeson):** master, summoner and centre of his world. Petros calls him his oldest friend, closest confidant and last living link to the man he was, and wonders whether the removal of Shar's darkness means he failed the smallest creature in his care.
- **Ruby Metallson:** remembers him as the little white rat who accompanied Rockgar through college, knows he saw the horrors of Tunnel Five, and understands why Rockgar has never parted with him.
- **[[Arta]]:** Akis sees in her the same wounds that haunt his master and regards her with tenderness and quiet kinship; she regards him with quiet affection, though his link to Shar troubles her, and knows he was an innocent vessel.
- **[[Aya]]:** he trusts her to guide Rockgar when even his own faith falters; she sees him as a faithful, innocent companion and his return to white as a blessing, even if Petros struggles to see it.
- **[[Thoriel]]:** he finds comfort in her quiet understanding of nature, life and death; she feels tenderness for him, suspects he understands far more than he can say, and treats him as a companion rather than a pet.
- **Aradun:** his steadiness reminds Akis of the mountain he once called home; Aradun thinks him a surprisingly brave little creature, finds his food-stealing less admirable, and secretly respects his taste.
- **Thraldour (Thrall):** once a towering presence of warmth and courage, his mind shattered in the depths of Khudrukhar; his body now rests in Bridingfort. Akis cannot mourn someone who still draws breath, nor celebrate so cruelly incomplete a survival. Before the end Thrall counted him a tiny but worthy member of the company.
- **[[Freiderikos]]:** the newest voice in the company; his untroubled spirit intrigues Akis, who watches the young sorcerer with curiosity and cautious hope. Freiderikos finds him fascinating and amusing, suspects far more intelligence behind those eyes than he lets on, and wonders which of the two, rat or wizard, is really in charge.
- **[[Hecate the Prophet|Hecate]]:** the hand that stole the night from his fur. She restored his white coat but left his master devastated, and Akis remembers the helplessness of being held in her hands. She saw him not as a living creature but as the missing vessel of her divine darkness, and having reclaimed it considers him insignificant.
- **[[Bjorn Brottdottir|Bjorn]]:** sees Akis as a former instrument of Shar's influence, whose loyalty to Petros interests him only where it might be exploited.
- **Shar:** the unseen tenant of his little soul. For years a fragment of her darkness dwelt in him; the shadow has departed, but its memory remains. She used him as a hiding place too small and innocent to arouse suspicion.
