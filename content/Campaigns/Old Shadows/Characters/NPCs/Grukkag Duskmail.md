# Grukkag Duskmail

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Γκρούκκαγκ Ντασκμέιλ]]

**Role:** High Priest of the Shadow · godfather of [[Arta]]

## Summary
Received the Six at the Church of Shar in the Undercity of Splendarrmornn during a service, and opened the way into the temple's inner sanctum, where the staircase down to Tunnel 5 begins.

His daughter **Kupra** was a powerful sorcerer and, it seems, Arta's childhood friend; she was killed by the Six in the **Shrine of Oblivion**, where Grukkag holds the services of the Keepers' religion. In his lodgings the Six found his personal papers and notes, and his exhibit hall holds specimens of the fauna, flora and sentient beings of the Underdark (Session 31). %% src:OS-S031 d12 %%
