# Thalri Pickaxeson

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Θάλρι]]

![[Characters - Thalri.png|300]]

**Role:** [[Petros]]'s sister · stands with the Prophet in *Old Shadows*

**Race:** Shield Dwarf · **Class:** Wizard / Acolyte of Shadow · **Pronouns:** she/her · **Age:** 55 · **Home:** [[Splendarrmornn]] · **Languages:** Dwarven

**Found:** at the Pickaxeson home, and at the **Church of the Shadow** in the Undercity of Splendarrmornn · **Attitude to the party:** it's complicated

## Summary
One of the four figures who followed the Six into the altar chamber in Session 29, together with [[Hecate the Prophet|Hecate]], [[Bjorn Brottdottir]] and [[Sol' Goleth]]. One of the party's open goals is to bring her back.

The youngest daughter of the Pickaxeson family and sister of Rockgar ([[Petros]]), in her final year at the **School of Magic of Splendarrmornn**. She idolises Rockgar and wants to follow in his footsteps. She is now a follower of [[Hecate the Prophet|Hecate]], the Prophet of the Shadow: the grief of losing Rockgar, and the Prophet's prophecy that he would come back to her, led her to join the **Keepers of the Secrets**. She is now one of Hecate's most faithful followers.

## Appearance
Thalri lacks the rough, warlike look of most of her kin from Splendarrmornn: her appearance reflects a woman who has given her life to the secrets of magic, of knowledge and of ancient rituals. She stands a little taller than the average dwarf woman. Her body is sturdy and compact, as befits her origin, but without a warrior's dense muscle. Her face is round, with strong cheekbones and stern lines around the mouth, and her skin is pale for a dwarf. Her eyes are perhaps her most striking feature: deep, dark and almost black, they seem to swallow the light rather than reflect it. Her hair is dark brown, nearly black, cut to a middling length so that it does not get in her way, with early grey strands from her contact with powerful and dangerous magical forces.

A deep black veil and a heavy hood cover most of her body. The cloth is old, steeped in dust, smoke and years of use in underground shrines and forgotten catacombs. Pouches, small leather bags and little objects of unknown use hang from her belt, each one perhaps the tool of some secret or some curse. She bears the **Holy Symbol of the Eclipse**.

She is always quiet, polite and soft-spoken, with a gentle, measured voice and the heavy accent of the Shield Dwarves of Splendarrmornn. She is hard to forget: not because she tries to stand out, but because of the strong impression she leaves behind.

## Personality
Always smiling and calm, Thalri has a gentleness most would envy. She is measured in her movements and her words, and she will hardly ever raise her voice in a conversation. The wounds of losing Rockgar do not seem to have healed, even now that she sees her brother alive again, and this makes her give herself wholly to the Prophet.

- **Ideal:** she fully embraces the dogma of the Keepers of the Secrets and wants to help the Prophet of the Shadow
- **Bond:** Hecate
- **Purpose:** to free the world from pain

## Background
Thalri idolises Rockgar and wants to follow in his footsteps. She firmly believes that he lives and that one day he will return. The Keepers of the Secrets and the Prophet herself took advantage of this belief, of her refusal to accept anything else, and of the inner wounds left by the loss of her beloved brother. The Prophet offered her a way to drive her pain away, showing her a Vision and the oracle that Rockgar would return to Splendarrmornn, bringing the Shadow with him. Thalri was converted to the Keepers of the Secrets, and she now wants to help Hecate redeem the world from pain.

> [!info] Rumours and open mysteries
> - She was a child prodigy of the School of Magic of Splendarrmornn.
> - Grief and pain have crushed her, and she has lost her mind.
> - She spends a good deal of time in the Undercity.

## Goals & fears
She fears nothing: she is free of hope and of pain, and so she has nothing left to fear.

## Relationships
- **The Pickaxeson family:** third child of Rurik and Marnil, sister of Berin and of Rockgar ([[Petros]]); the youngest daughter, with a particular soft spot for Rockgar.
- **[[Hecate the Prophet|Hecate]]:** her mentor. In Hecate, Thalri sees the saviour who will redeem the world from pain.
