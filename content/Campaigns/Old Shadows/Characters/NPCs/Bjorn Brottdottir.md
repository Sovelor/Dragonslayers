## Summary
In Session 29 he came with [[Hecate the Prophet|Hecate]] into the altar chamber and begged his daughter: *"Stand at your father's side. Accept your destiny. Together we can achieve anything!"* His bakery in the Undercity had been closed for a long time when Arta visited it.
