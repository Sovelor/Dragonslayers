# Lycast

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Λικάστ]]

**Role:** Sea hag, leader of the creatures in the Pearl Lighthouse

## Summary
A sea hag, called "lady of the Marshes" in a letter found on her, who led the creatures that took over the lighthouse, where the keepers were all found dead. She first met the party in the shape of a woman castaway, locked in a cage and pretending to be a prisoner, to learn who the intruders were. At the top of the lighthouse [[Petros]] trapped her magic cauldron, and she lost a great part of her power; **Thraldour** beheaded her.
