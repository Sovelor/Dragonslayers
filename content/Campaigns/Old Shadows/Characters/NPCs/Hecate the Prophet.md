# Hecate the Prophet

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Εκάτη, η Προφήτης]]

**Role:** Main antagonist of *Old Shadows* · the Prophet · also met by name in *A New Beginning*

## Summary
The Prophet who gave the servant of the shadow in *A New Beginning* a sword (the Shadow Longsword) to be filled with 50 souls is the same Hecate the Six face in *Old Shadows*. In Session 29 she walked into the altar chamber with [[Thalri]], [[Bjorn Brottdottir]] and [[Sol' Goleth]], stunned the whole party, took the shadow out of [[Akis]] (her last component) and called [[Petros]] "my dear Rokgar". She left them alive on purpose: *"Death is mercy... First you will watch. Then you will hurt! Then you will break."*
