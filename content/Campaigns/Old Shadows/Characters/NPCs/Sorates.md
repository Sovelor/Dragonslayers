# Sorates

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Σόρατες]]

**Role:** Human Monk of the Balance from the Grey Monastery · member of the [[Circle of the Moon]]

## Summary
A human Monk of the Balance from the Grey Monastery in the mountains of the Grey Peaks, and a member of the Circle of the Moon, which is part of a larger organization, the Silver Hand. He follows the Lady of the Moon, who is now Allalme: Selûne died ten years ago, in the Battle of Destiny. The Keepers of Secrets raided the monastery and took him prisoner; the Six found him tortured beside Ruby Metallson in the Keepers' torture chamber and freed him (Session 31).
