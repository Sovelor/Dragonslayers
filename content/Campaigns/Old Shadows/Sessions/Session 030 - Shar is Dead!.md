# Session 030 - Shar is Dead!

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Συνεδρία 030 - Η Σαρ είναι νεκρή!]]

## Recap
After [[Hecate the Prophet]] took the last piece of shadow from [[Akis]], she and the three who came with her left. The binding spell broke and the Incredible Six could move again, alone in the ritual chamber of [[Tunnel No. 5]], trying to catch their breath. It was only the first of the day's revelations. Behind them the [[Shadow Altar]] began to vibrate darkly: the solid shadow covering it now steamed all around, humming to life. [[Aya]] quickly wrapped everyone in a spell of light to keep the dark at bay.

The **49 dwarves of the Tunnel** appeared around them: twisted, cursed souls whispering [[Petros]]'s name. At the edge of the light one of them asked him angrily who was standing before him. Petros answered "Rockgar Pickaxeson", and the shadow dwarf stepped into the light and grabbed Petros's face, trying to crush his skull and poisoning him with shadow, as the dead moved to take him with them into the Shadow. [[Thoriel]] pulled him back and Aradun held them off with his breath of light. As the dwarves closed in, Aya's light kept shrinking; she tried to turn them, but her goddess seemed out of reach. With the help of Thoriel's and Petros's magic she managed, with difficulty, to cleanse the Shadow Altar by the power of Allalme. The 49 were purified and forgave Rockgar: one by one they bid Petros farewell, calling out their names, until the last asked once more who stood before him. *"Petros Axinakis, wandering wizard and the one who shall keep your memory alive,"* he answered, and the souls were free to return to their paradise.

Paying their respects to Tunnel No. 5, the Six went deeper into the underground complex, looking for the Keepers of the Secrets. In a natural cave full of Underdark mushrooms and plants, [[Arta]] and Thoriel found six new species, and Thoriel took samples. One red mushroom was unknown to everyone; Aradun cut off a piece and tasted it, and became four years younger. They named it the **mushroom of time**.

Further on, a rock stood in the middle of a wide passage where it did not belong, as if it had been moved there by some kind of teleportation, and beyond it lay a well (Thoriel's player also saw a wheel brimming with evil power). Whispers in a language nobody understood rose from it. Thoriel's friend **Hope**, a small wisp, told her the whispers came from creatures of her own plane, steeped in shadow; as the party approached, the wisps joined together into an **Elder Shadow Elemental**. In the tunnels near the underground river the Six won a hard fight against its powerful attacks, with [[Freiderikos]] frying it with new lightning powers. Afterwards Aradun climbed down the well, found a skeleton and took its belongings, since it no longer needed them.

Next they climbed a sheer cave wall full of holes to avoid: a trial for the Keepers' trainees, Arta told them, which she had always won. She climbed it easily and Petros, thanks to his magic boots, reached the top with her; the others followed with difficulty. During the climb an **Elder Xorn** attacked them, but Petros spoke to it in the language of Earth and persuaded it to let them pass without a fight, with no one having to pay. Then came the **Trial of the White Tunnel**, a corridor of white granite whose whole surface was poisoned. Recalling her memories, Arta told them to cross as fast as possible; Thoriel flew through with Akis, and Petros brought the rest across with *Dimension Door*.

They reached the tomb of **Ereg Tal**, patriarch of the Keepers of the Secrets, and his wife **Kora**. On the right stood the statue of a dwarf, on the left that of a dwarf woman, each with an inscription naming them; further back stood a third statue with an aura so dark and otherworldly that everyone's blood froze. Something powerful and full of shadow was there with them. Only Aya recognised it, from the Prophet's vision: *"I recognise her. I have seen her."* Arta said this was not right: a statue of **Shar** used to stand there, and this was something else entirely. Remembering they had come to find Arta's father and the Keepers, the Six left quickly, without taking anything from the sarcophagus, to Freiderikos's dismay.

At a door stood two Keepers of the Secrets, the dwarves **Shaldi** and **Kirkold**, guards of the passage and Arta's companions during her training. They seemed to remember Arta and Petros and greeted Petros the way they greet each other, mocking both for not remembering them and Arta for her choices. Aradun and Arta "persuaded" them with a gold coin from the well, and the Six passed without a fight. Further in, more guards revealed that Arta's father was waiting for her and Petros, and no one else, and that the ceremony had already begun. In the guardhouse of the first level of the Keepers' headquarters the Six fought **Ubar the Black**, Senior Keeper of the First Level and a dwarf wizard necromancer, and his guards. Aradun announced he liked Ubar's rings so much he was thinking of taking them, and his risky tactic caught the guards unprepared: the fight turned in the Six's favour at once, and Ubar died quickly, blasted by Freiderikos's and Petros's spells.

To protect her new friend, Thoriel cast *Barkskin* on Freiderikos before shifting into a jaguar. To her shock, his skin grew **blue dragon scales**. Aradun's dragon axes had already warned him that something was wrong with Freiderikos, and as soon as the fight was over he pinned the unsuspecting Freiderikos down. The Six questioned him about his past. Freiderikos, anxious that he had done something wrong, answered with complete honesty but seemed to have no idea who Tiamat was or why this was a bad omen; his staff turned out to be made of dragon bone, from a gold dragon. Aradun, with his own metallic dragon heritage, swore he would stop Freiderikos himself at the slightest evil intention; Aya was on high alert as the party's paladin; only Petros seemed relaxed.

**Key beats**
1. Hecate leaves; the Six are free again in the ritual chamber of Tunnel No. 5
2. The 49 cursed dwarves rise around the Shadow Altar and try to take Petros into the Shadow
3. Aya, with Thoriel and Petros, cleanses the Shadow Altar; the 49 forgive Rockgar and bid him farewell
4. The mushroom of time: Aradun grows four years younger
5. The Elder Shadow Elemental at the shadow well; Aradun loots a skeleton in the well
6. The Keepers' climbing trial; Petros talks an Elder Xorn into letting them pass
7. The Trial of the White Tunnel, crossed by flight and *Dimension Door*
8. The tomb of Ereg Tal and Kora: the statue of Shar has been replaced by something otherworldly that Aya recognises
9. Shaldi and Kirkold let the Six through for a gold coin; Arta's father waits only for her and Petros; the ceremony has begun
10. Ubar the Black and his guards defeated in the first-level guardhouse
11. *Barkskin* gives Freiderikos blue dragon scales; Aradun pins him down and the Six question him
