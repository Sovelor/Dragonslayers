# Session 002 - The Pearl Lighthouse

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Συνεδρία 002 - Ο Μαργαριταρένιος Φάρος]]

## Recap
[[Petros]] officially became a member of the party. In the morning the group set out to solve the mystery of the inactive lighthouse. At noon they reached the **Pearl Lighthouse** and found seven *locathah* and a *sahuagin* guarding its entrance. They got past them easily, with the resourceful Petros showing off his skill at magic: a flaming bolt from his crossbow landed among the creatures, and his *Pyrotechnics* spell blinded them before they could react.

Inside, they found that the lighthouse's keepers were all dead, and fiendish creatures attacked them. In the antechamber a **grick** ambushed the party and seized **Thraldour**; Petros's *Grease* spell made it fall from the ceiling where it clung, and [[Aya]] finished it with her warhammer. On the first floor they trapped twelve goblins together in one room, smoking them out with fire, and then made short work of them. On the second floor the **barghest Skrom** had his lair with his two bugbear bodyguards; the party won, but Skrom escaped with a *Dimension Door* spell. On the third floor two **redcaps** guarded the cell of a woman, a castaway from the ship. She was not what she seemed: she was [[Lycast]], a sea hag and the leader of them all, who had taken the shape of a captive to find out who the intruders in her lighthouse were and what they wanted.

At the top of the lighthouse the party faced Lycast and her magic cauldron, with Skrom and a redcap bodyguard at her side. It was a hard victory, won by another of Petros's tricks: with *Rope Trick* and *Levitate* he trapped the cauldron in the spell's extradimensional space, there to stay forever. Losing the cauldron cost Lycast a great part of her power. **Thraldour** and [[Aradun]] then pinned her to the ground, and the two dwarves tried to kiss the hideous hag, but in the end could not find the will to do it. Thraldour beheaded her. Skrom died in the same fight, killed by [[Thoriel]].

On Lycast they found a parchment, a letter to her that speaks of **the Speaker** and of the hour of Judgement, and asks that the lighthouse keep working as planned (see below).

Finally the party visited the tunnels beneath the lighthouse and faced undead creatures and a **mimic**. Four sea ghouls (*lacedons*) and the mimic caught them unprepared, and a **lesser demilich** stunned Aradun and Petros and toyed with **Havi**'s mind and memories. Aya used her divine power to turn the undead, reducing three of them to dust, and together with [[Thoriel]] destroyed the mimic. Then Aya channelled positive divine power into the demilich and, with the help of Thoriel's divine energy of nature, they suppressed its necromantic energy. That gave Petros, who had come to his senses, the chance to destroy it with *Magic Missile*. It was an extremely hard victory.
