# Elizium

> [!warning] Spoilers
> Reveals Elizium's death in Part One.

**Role:** High elf (Υψηλό Ξωτικό) from Silverymoon
**Appearance:** White skin, long blond hair, dark leather armour; fights with two short swords

> [!danger] Deceased
> Dies in [[Forest Camp]] protecting [[Badinga Alpenrok]] and [[Garpask Losark]] from a goblin/hyena ambush.

## Summary
Meets Badinga in the [[Abandoned Fortress]] during a snowstorm ([[The Dragonslayers Saga - Story Timeline#Prologue|Prologue]]). Travels with the forming trio through [[Garpask Losark]]'s rescue, then dies defending the group during the night ambush at [[Forest Camp]].

## Character beats
- Calls himself "Elizium the Brave" when he and Badinga charge the wild dogs to save Garpask.
- Tells the others his journey north is "a journey of atonement", but gives no details. They respect his silence.
- Reckons Lonlywood is five to six days' march from where the trio sets out.

## Death sequence
1. Leap-and-fall wounding beat
2. Critical hit taken
3. Killing blow + fatal retaliation

## Burial
Badinga and Garpask keep watch over his body all night. At first light they carry him to the top of a hard-to-reach hill and bury him there, so that he can "always look down from high and smile".

## Story significance
His death is the emotional hinge of Part One — Badinga and Garpask travel in near-total silence afterward, grieving, which sets up their meeting with [[Gaston Lentinson]] and [[Mahaya]] in Part Two. Later, when Badinga, Garpask and [[Mahaya]] enter the [[Ancient Crypt]], all three say "For Elizium" before opening the gate.
