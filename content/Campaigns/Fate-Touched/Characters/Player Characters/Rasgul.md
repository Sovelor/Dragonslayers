## Summary
A human duskblade who fights with the Dragonslayers and carries the essence of **Eliphaz**, one of the Sons of [[Atropos]]. He and [[Garpask Losark|Garpask]] think [[Badinga Alpenrok|Badinga]] tries too hard to prove he is the strongest, and they don't like him always trying to change himself.

## Appearance
Young and wiry, with wild, spiky brown hair and glowing red eyes. He wears crimson and gold armour with a high pointed collar, purple scarves and a ragged white-and-purple tabard, bone-spiked gauntlets and ornate red boots. His weapon is an enormous scythe whose haft ends in a red-eyed skull, and wisps of dark energy trail around him.
