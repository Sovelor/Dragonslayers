# Lavern Norphyra

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Λαβέρν]]

![[Characters - Lavern.png|300]]

**Role / Appears in:** Player character and member of the Dragonslayers.

## Summary
Lavern is one of the Dragonslayers. She took over the leadership of the [[Brotherhood of Light]] after the death of [[Negnar Belmont]]. Ten years later she is [[Lady Lavern]], High Lady of the Brotherhood of Light.

## Appearance
In her portrait, a young woman with long dark-brown hair and pointed ears, with large white feathered wings. She wears white-and-gold plate armour with rose-pink panels and holds a longsword with a winged crossguard, in a sunlit colonnaded courtyard.
