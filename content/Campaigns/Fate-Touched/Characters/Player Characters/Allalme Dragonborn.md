# Allalme Dragonborn

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Αλλάλμε Ντράγκονμπορν]]

![[Characters - Allalme Dragonborn.jpg|300]]

> [!warning] Spoilers
> Reveals the end of *Fateful Meeting* and the chapter *The Elf with the Purple Hair* in Part Two, and, under *Ten years later*, the end of the saga: Allalme's Ascension.

**Role / Appears in:** High elf found asleep in the [[Ancient Crypt]]; joins the party at the end of Part Two, *The Elf with the Purple Hair*.
**Greek name:** Αλλάλμε Ντράγκονμπορν
**Faith:** Selûne (Σελούνε), the Lady of the Moon
**Facts:** Elf · Druid, level 24 · female · Chaotic Good · home: the forest under the moonlight · languages: Common, Elvish, Druidic, Celestial, Sylvan

## Summary
Allalme is a young elf woman whom [[Badinga Alpenrok]], [[Garpask Losark]] and [[Mahaya]] find lying on a stone altar in a hidden crypt deep inside a mountain, bathed in moonlight from a crack in the ceiling. Her wounds are bandaged with dressings of leaves and branches and have almost healed. Garpask has seen her before, in a vision shown to him by his father [[Frederick Frostfire]]. When she wakes she has lost almost all of her memory, but the three men invite her to travel with them and she accepts.

## Appearance and gear
- Long, thick purple hair; emerald-green eyes; white, almost pale skin.
- Ornate brown leather armour decorated with green leaves that look alive.
- An ornate elven blade with a silver edge, worn at the end of the chapter as a scimitar (γιαταγάνι) at her belt.
- **Later:** her hair turned black and deer antlers grew on her head. She wears leather armour with dragon scales and tree leaves on the shoulders, and a cloak that is green outside and holds all the stars of the sky inside. Two short swords hang at her belt, left and right, and she wears a crown with five stars. Her voice is calm and angelic.
- A travel pack, found hidden under the altar's stone lid with a bedroll, dried fruit, vials of coloured liquids and used first-aid supplies.
- **The Pearl** (η Πέρλα): given to her by her grandfather, who told her it now belonged to her. It is the one thing she panics about losing. Garpask feels a strange pull towards it.

## What she remembers
- Her grandfather **Tarsus** (Τάρσους) raised her and taught her the ways of Mother Nature.
- She and Tarsus protect Selûne's beloved creatures under the starry sky. She thanks "the Silver Mother" when her pack is found.
- She knows Icewind Dale lies north of the Spine of the World, but has no idea how or why she got there.
- She speaks flawless Common and the high-elf dialect. Mahaya says her dialect is typical of the high elves of Silverymoon (Σιλβέριμουν); the name feels familiar to her.
- The word "alone" frightens and hurts her, though she does not know why.

## Character beats
- Her first words on waking are two names none of the party recognise: **Sovelor** and **Gzave** («Σόβελορ; Γκζέιβ;»).
- Surprisingly strong: she shifts the altar's heavy stone lid on her own before the others join in.
- Mahaya answers her in Elvish and greets her formally; Garpask reassures her that her memories will likely return with time.

## Personality
Quiet, usually serious, and of course "the squad's mom". A calm strength: decisive, and always trying to bring balance to everything.
- **Ideals:** balance.
- **Bonds:** with all her companions.
- **Flaws:** stubborn and headstrong.

## Goals & fears
- **Goals:** to finally become a goddess and bring balance to this world.
- **Fears:** being left alone.

## Relationships
- [[Vincent Vermilion|Vincent]]: the love of her life, and her husband
- [[Sovelor]]: they grew up together; he is like a brother to her
- [[Garpask Losark|Garpask]]: her best friend
- [[Mahaya]]: the little brother she never had, whom she was always scolding
- [[Lavern]]: the friend who was always by her side
- **Enemy:** [[Atropos]]

## Notable items
- The **crown of stars**, the **cloak of nature** and her **short swords** are heirlooms.

> [!info] How she got there
> In Garpask's vision, two figures carried her into the crypt, sealed it and left, sadly and as if they had no choice: a slender tiefling with demonic horns, and a bald human, pale as moonlight, with a cloak and a large staff. Mahaya's reading of the tracks fits: two pairs of footprints, heavier going in and lighter coming out, about two days old.

## Ten years later
After the Battle of Destiny against [[Atropos]], Allalme married [[Vincent Vermilion]] and then ascended to godhood. During her Ascension she created the **Great Forests Plane**, where Vincent followed her as Her Champion and Harbinger of Her teachings. Her faithful include the druids of the Circle of the Moon, also called the Circle of Allalme.
