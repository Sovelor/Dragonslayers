# Elonia

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Ελόνια]]

![[Characters - Elonia.jpg|300]]

**Role:** Elf bard and member of the Dragonslayers
**Facts:** Elf · Bard · female

## Summary
An elf bard and a member of the Dragonslayers. She is listed among the fellow Dragonslayers of [[Shaureya]], together with [[Mahaya]], [[Garpask Losark]] and [[Sovelor]].

## Appearance
An elf with long silver-white hair, pointed ears, a slender circlet and a confident smile. She wears a green coat trimmed with gold over a brown leather vest with pauldrons, black trousers and tall boots. She plays a silver harp and carries a brass horn at her hip. In her portrait a great ice-white dragon coils behind her.
