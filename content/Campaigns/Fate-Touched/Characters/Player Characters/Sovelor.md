# Sovelor

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Σόβελορ]]

![[Characters - Sovelor.png|300]]

> [!warning] Spoilers
> Reveals events up to the fight with Atropos and its aftermath.

**Role / Appears in:** Tiefling swashbuckler of the Dragonslayers, son of Asmodeus; founder of the Edge of Banishment; the Blood Prince of the Nine Hells.
**Facts:** Tiefling of human heritage · Swashbuckler/Rogue (Daring Outlaw), level 24 · male · Chaotic Good · languages: Common, Infernal, Abyssal, Celestial

## Summary
Sovelor is the son of Deina and the archdevil Asmodeus, and carries Asmodeus's essence. Tortured and imprisoned in the Nine Hells, he was rescued by the Dragonslayers and became one of them. He later founded the Edge of Banishment, a secret branch of the Order of Freedom that hunts down and banishes evil outsiders, and in the end bested Asmodeus himself. Six months after the fight with [[Atropos]], he mysteriously disappeared.

## Appearance
Ash-grey skin, a dexterous build and dark hair, with short white horns. A mask covers the lower half of his face. He wears crimson and black leathers (the Millenial Chainmail), with a rapier (the Bastard Slayer) and a black dagger at his side.

## Personality
Arrogant, always mocking the enemy, and always ready to do what must be done: goal-oriented, efficient and effective. Torture in the Nine Hells damaged him; travelling with the Dragonslayers healed him.

## Background
Sovelor adventured on the material plane with his mother and his childhood friends [[Allalme Dragonborn|Allalme]] and Gzave, until Asmodeus's devils captured him and Gzave. Back in the Nine Hells he was tortured by his brother **Amet**, who sat on the throne. Sovelor managed to kill Amet by stealth and took the Rod of Asmodeus, the Millenial Chainmail and the throne of the Nine Hells, ascending as the **Blood Prince**.

Amet's followers overthrew him and imprisoned him inside a suit of armour, draining his essence to power it. To him it felt like three hundred years, and it left him mentally unstable. The Dragonslayers rescued him and made him part of their group, and with their help he killed a resurrected Amet. He infused his rapier with his Asmodean blood, creating the **Bastard Slayer**.

He joined the Order of Freedom, and after the attack of the Dragons he founded the **Edge of Banishment**, a secret branch of the Order dedicated to hunting down and banishing evil outsiders. He finally bested Asmodeus in a one-on-one fight in the deepest parts of his mind, and imprisoned him in Allalme's mirror. Six months after the fight with Atropos, he mysteriously disappeared.

## Goals & fears
- **Goals:** kill Asmodeus; then rid the material plane of every evil outsider; then exterminate all the gods, so that mortals can thrive on their own freedom.
- **Flaws:** his arrogance (he mocks even the gods) and his certainty that he is always right.
- **Fears:** death.
