**Role:** Human frenzied berserker and one of the Dragonslayers; Champion of Bahamut
**Facts:** Human · Frenzied Berserker · male · Champion of Bahamut · died in the attack of the Dragons

## Summary
A human frenzied berserker, Champion of Bahamut, who fought beside the Dragonslayers. He respects [[Badinga Alpenrok]] and counts him among the strongest of the companions. He died in the attack of the Dragons.

## Appearance
A broad, weathered man with long black hair, a thick dark beard and a scowl. His armour is made of overlapping red and grey dragon-scale plates, with great curved horns on the shoulders and a dragon skull at the chest, and he fights with a blade wreathed in flame.
