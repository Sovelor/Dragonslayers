**Role:** Human paladin and one of the Dragonslayers; former leader of the [[Brotherhood of Light]]
**Facts:** Human · Paladin · male · died in the Well of Unborn Souls

## Summary
A human paladin who led the [[Brotherhood of Light]] before [[Lavern]] and fought beside the Dragonslayers. He is the Dragonslayer closest to [[Badinga Alpenrok]]: the two are brothers in arms. He died in the Well of Unborn Souls, and Lavern took over the leadership of the Brotherhood after his death.

## Appearance
Lean and stern, with short light-brown hair and a hard, watchful stare, usually seen with a white hood drawn up. He wears polished steel plate trimmed with gold under a white tabard and a long white cloak with gold filigree, and carries a huge war hammer inscribed with glowing runes and a winged emblem.

## Later in the campaign
Beyond the manuscript so far. [[Badinga Alpenrok|Badinga]] meets him later in his journeys, when he arrives in Luskan, together with [[Bloodthrill]]. Negnar dies in the Well of Unborn Souls, and [[Lavern]] succeeds him as leader of the Brotherhood of Light (ten years later she is [[Lady Lavern]], its High Lady).
