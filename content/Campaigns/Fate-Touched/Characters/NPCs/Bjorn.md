# Bjorn

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Μπγιόρν]]

> [!warning] Spoilers
> *Notable items* reveals events from later in the campaign, beyond the manuscript so far.

**Role:** Dwarf, [[Badinga Alpenrok]]'s godfather and teacher
**Home:** The settlement of Eastside, in the frozen north

## Summary
An old dwarf who trained Badinga in the fighting style of the Dwarven Warriors and in smithing, and taught him to recognise and understand rocks, minerals and metals. Badinga is fond of him and credits him for every battle skill he uses. His first thought in his first fight is "This is for you, Bjorn!"

## Character beats
- Wakes Badinga before dawn for hard daily training, with a booming voice and a joking tone ("Wake up, my lad! It's noon already!").
- Taught the shield-and-hammer defence that lets Badinga hold off several goblins without being hit.
- His own blows in training are described as far harder than any goblin's.
- Taught Badinga pride and honour.
- Badinga calls him "his beloved teacher".

> [!question] Status unknown
> The manuscript does not say where Bjorn is now.

## Notable items
- **Belt of Draconic Heritage**: he made it for Badinga through the **Soul Forger** ritual, a once-in-a-lifetime ritual that Moradin allows and blesses so that an artisan can create an artifact.
