# Jennifer

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Τζένιφερ]]

**Role:** [[Badinga Alpenrok]]'s first and only love; daughter of the local lord of Eastside
**Appearance:** Thick red hair, big brown eyes, a bright smile

## Summary
The daughter of Eastside's local lord and Badinga's childhood friend. He fell in love with her as they grew up, but her parents never accepted him, thinking him unworthy to stand beside her.

Badinga dreams of her almost every night. In the Prologue's dream she starts by calling him toward her, then her form fades and he hears cries for help, and he wakes not knowing whether she is in danger. She also appears in the *Badinga* flashback chapter.

## What the manuscript says
- Badinga left his village and believes that getting away from her will make him stop thinking about her. It hasn't worked.
- In his dreams she is close; "in reality things were worse".
- Something she always told him ("he was not good at judging people") stays in his mind.
- When he is woken by a man calling for help, his first thought is that it really is Jennifer.
