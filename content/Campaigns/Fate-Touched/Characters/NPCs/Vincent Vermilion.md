## Summary
Vincent was a fighter in a second group that met [[Allalme Dragonborn|Allalme]], [[Badinga Alpenrok|Badinga]], [[Garpask Losark|Garpask]] and [[Mahaya]] in the city of Lonlywood, under the command of Lieutenant Lavern. His group walked a different path from the Dragonslayers, but he fell in love with Allalme. Fate brought them together many times: as companions, as friends, as allies and as lovers. After Allalme's Ascension he became Her Champion and Harbinger of Her teachings. He can be found in the **Great Forests Plane**, the plane of existence Allalme created during her Ascension, where he followed his Lady. He is friendly to the party.

## Appearance
Hazel eyes; short dark hair streaked with grey, especially around the temples and the top of his head; a neatly trimmed beard and moustache, also greying. Strong, mature features, a steady, determined gaze and scars on his face. Powerful and broad-shouldered, the build of a veteran fighter.

He wears elaborate heavy plate covered in engravings, with large pauldrons, a thick grey fur mantle around the shoulders, and a dark cloak or tabard beneath. A wide leather belt carries pouches and adventuring gear. He holds a large sword with a blue gemstone in the hilt, its blade engraved with glowing runes.

## Personality
Always smiling. When he is serious his voice is low and slow; when he is happy and excited it is loud and quick. Strong-willed and fair: someone you can depend on.
- **Ideals:** always do good, or die trying; protect the weak; be there for your friends and companions.
- **Bonds:** friendship and companionship with the people of his groups; Champion of his Goddess.
- **Flaw:** impulsive: first act, then think.

## Background
Vincent's late father, a captain of the Waterdeep Guard, raised him as a future soldier and taught him to fight; his mother was a caring and loving woman. When his father died in an attack by evil creatures on Waterdeep, his mother could not bear it and died of grief. Vincent decided to travel the world and help everyone who needed it, becoming an adventurer and protector of the weak. Later he joined the Brotherhood of Light and became a **Knight of the Light**.

When Allalme was taken by the forces of [[Atropos]] and held prisoner, Vincent joined the Dragonslayers to rescue her. They succeeded, but Vincent had to take her place inside her prison so that the demi-plane would become stable again, giving the Dragonslayers and an unconscious Allalme time to flee. He was trapped there in her place until the Dragonslayers found a way to free him, and the two were together again. He fought in the **Battle of Destiny** against Atropos and her forces. After the victory, Vincent and Allalme married, just before Allalme ascended to godhood.
