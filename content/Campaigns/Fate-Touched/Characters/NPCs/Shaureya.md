# Shaureya

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Σαουρέγια]]

![[Characters - Shaureya.jpg|300]]

**Role:** NPC, former antagonist and now ally, a member of the Dragonslayers and Deputy Leader of the [[Brotherhood of Light]]. Found in the Belmont Castle-State, the Brotherhood's headquarters; friendly to the party.
**Race:** Astra Deva (formerly), Half-Celestial native outsider (now) · **Class:** Paladin of the Angelic Order of Light (formerly); warrior and knight of good, protector of the Material Plane (now) · **Pronouns:** she/her · **Age:** aeons · **Alignment:** neutral good · **Home:** Celestia · **Languages:** Common, Celestial, Infernal, Abyssal

## Summary
Once a proud aide-de-camp of the celestial angelic forces, Shaureya was one of the most powerful fighters of the Legions of Angels. As an astral deva she was second in the hierarchy of the Legion of the Archangel Zariel. She followed her general in her fall, and after a long captivity in the Nine Hells she was freed by the Dragonslayers. She now walks among mortals as one of them, half-celestial and half-mortal, a defender of the Material Plane.

## Appearance
Shaureya is an imposing angelic figure who joins her dark past as a fallen angel with her return to the light. Her long white wings are tipped with deep red, blood-coloured streaks. Her snow-white hair flows with matching red highlights, which symbolise the bloody road that led her to redemption. Her skin is extremely pale, almost alabaster, like marble. Her eyes, the most striking feature of her face, are intensely dark, almost black.
She wears full angelic armour of angelic silver, a Robe of Angelic Songs and the Angelic Greatsword of Celestia's Wrath. Her voice is melodic, like a soft song.

## Personality
Quiet, observant, patient and restrained.
- **Ideals:** debt carries the heavy obligation to fulfil it; good always wins in the end.
- **Fear:** losing her mind again and returning to the darkness.
- **Goal:** to fulfil her debt to the Dragonslayers and defend the Material Plane.

## Background
Shaureya was the second in rank of the Legion of Archangel Zariel. When Zariel and her Legion attacked the Nine Hells, hoping to weaken the influence of devils on the Material Plane and keep them from winning the eternal Blood War, the crusade failed completely. The Legion of the Sun's Light was defeated in the Battle of Ride, and almost everyone in it was slaughtered by the devils of Avernus, the first layer of the Nine Hells. Zariel, her lieutenant Shaureya and their aides were captured and tortured. Zariel was the first to succumb to the corruption of the Hells, and of Asmodeus himself.
Shaureya was sent to the Prison of Divided Wrath, in the depths of Nessus, the ninth layer. There she was tortured, her wings were mutilated and her mind broke. Only hatred and pain remained, and together with the corruption of the Hells they drove her to madness and to a change into a devil-like being.
Later the Dragonslayers, who were there for the rescue of Deina (Sovelor's mother), found and freed her. They offered Shaureya a way to make amends and return to the path of good, and the astral deva gladly accepted and helped them finish their mission. She joined their group and was present in the battles against the Forest Kingdom and the forces of Fate under Atropos, and in the Battle of Destiny. These deeds freed her completely from the corruption, and [[Allalme Dragonborn|Allalme]] granted her the reward of redemption.
