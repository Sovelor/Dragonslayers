# Languages and Incantations

**Role / Appears in:** Spell words and Elvish phrases used in the manuscript, with the meanings given in the manuscript's own footnotes.

## Summary
[[Garpask Losark]] speaks his spells' activation words in the language of dragons (Draconic). [[Mahaya]] and [[Allalme Dragonborn]] speak Elvish to each other in the high-elf dialect. Everyone else speaks Common (η Κοινή).

## Draconic incantations (Garpask)
| Words | Greek | Meaning | Used in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arcanis Nil'Nosi | Αρκάνις Νιλ\`Νόσι | "Magic Missile" | *Loss*, *The Goblin Ambush* |
| Arcanis Litrix | Αρκάνις Λίτριξ | "Mage Armour", a silver cloud that shields the caster | *Loss*, *Fateful Meeting* |
| Mitne | Μίτνε | "Light". Garpask lights his fist or Badinga's shield with glowing moss | *Loss*, *Fateful Meeting* |

Garpask's components include a scrap of leather (Mage Armour) and phosphorescent moss (Light).

## Elvish phrases (high-elf dialect)
| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Μαν ενέθ λιν ιβέλ | "Are you well?" |
| Ναν ινίρα λάρα | Polite reply: "I am well, thank you for asking" |
| Ελέν γιούμ ερίν λουμέν ομέν τι έλβο | Formal greeting between two elves: "May the Moon light this meeting" |
| Ένυα ναν | Introduction, before a name or title: "My name is / I am" |
| Μεν πεντίκ λούθιεν | "Are you ready, princess?", asked before setting out |
