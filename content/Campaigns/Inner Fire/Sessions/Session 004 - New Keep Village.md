# Session 004 - New Keep Village

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Συνεδρία 004 - Το χωριό Νιου Κιπ]]

## Recap
On the way to New Keep, [[Lenor|Lenόre]] had a sudden flashback of herself in a room full of books, with the smell of parchment and ink. She was reading a book about a city's management and its layers of hierarchy, until her teacher, a female figure she cannot make out, sternly called her out for being there. The teacher struck young Lenόre's hands with a birch.

The party reached **New Keep**. [[Lenor|Lenόre]] and Nyra were worried about how they looked and tried to cover themselves as much as possible. Inside, the village was in a state of emergency: chaos and concern, fear and desperation among the villagers.

They were greeted by [[Robin]], a druid who looks after a place the villagers call *the temple*, and **Matataias**, a young half-elf who is the new captain of the guard; the old captain, his father, was killed in an attack. Robin, the friendlier of the two, explained that the village is attacked by day by a creature: an **ettin**, with two heads. One head is always asleep while the other takes over. By day the aggressive head roams free and destroys everything; by night a gentler, peaceful head only visits the temple and prays.

The party offered to help. The creature lives in an old tower in the forest, but the roads are full of orcs and the way is dangerous. They accepted, and retired for the day to the inn **Gabros' Beer**, where the innkeeper, **Gabros**, greeted them and gave them a room.

That night Lenόre had a nightmare: she stood in a line with ten more tieflings of about her age, all repeating the words *"Blood and Death"*. When Lenόre refused to follow the same instructions, the same teacher punished her with a whip.

**Key beats**
1. On the road: Lenόre's flashback of her strict teacher
2. Arrival in New Keep; Lenόre and Nyra hide their appearance
3. A village in a state of emergency
4. Robin the druid and Matataias, the new captain of the guard
5. The ettin: a destroyer by day, a pilgrim to the temple by night
6. The party agrees to find the ettin in its tower; orcs on the roads
7. A room at Gabros' Beer
8. Lenόre's nightmare of the tieflings and the whip
