# Session 007 - The Cursed Shield and the Broom

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Συνεδρία 007 - Η καταραμένη ασπίδα και η σκούπα]]

## Recap
Old **Jarl**, the innkeeper, told the party the story of the shield (DM). With his wife **Maggie** and their son **Mike** he had bought a motel, the **Osterhaus Inn**, for a fairly good price, because it had burned down for unknown reasons. The only thing they found intact was a mysterious **shield**. Mike was in charge of cleaning it, and so touched it; rumour has it he was cursed, and one night he died in the barn in a fire that started out of nowhere. Since then the shield has been an attraction for anyone brave enough to touch it, and willing to pay the innkeeper for it. The party also learned that the secret meeting place of the sect, the **Followers of the Flame** (DM), was in a mountain about three days from New Keep, near the **Lipen waterfall**. They decided to go to the motel and look into the rumours about the shield.

At the motel a huge crowd was waiting for Jarl to begin the show. [[Grafin]], [[Meive]] and the griffon Skythane were at the inn too (DM). Some people were rude and openly racist towards [[Lenor|Lenόre]] and [[Nyra]] because of how they look, since neither race is common around there. Then Jarl appeared and gave a performance about the shield and its curse. The crowd was let into the inn a few at a time; many cast *detect magic* on the shield, and Jarl said there was truly no trick. He charged a good amount of gold to anyone brave enough to touch it, and although many did, no one got hurt. All the while Maggie looked uneasy, and the new stable boy, **Toby**, tried to look after all the visitors.

The party booked a room and, once the crowd had cleared, spoke with Maggie. She has never recovered from her son's death and wants the shield gone; Jarl, on the other hand, believes the show and the visitors are, in a strange way, what they are owed for Mike's death. Maggie confessed that both she and Toby have bizarre dreams of a tall woman with grey skin and pointed ears, holding a trident: they dream of the **Harbinger**. She also told them that **Mira**, the woman of the ettin's tower whose journal the party had found (DM), had stopped by often, offering money for the shield, but Jarl never accepted. As the party tried to connect the pieces, they wondered whether any of them had had such incidents before; only Nyra had. Inside the inn she once had a vision of an armoured man with a flaming bastard sword, who attacked her from behind; she woke at the foot of the stairs and realised the attack had not been real, that she must have imagined it (DM).

Then Lenόre had another horrifying flashback. Her sister, **Saria**, stood facing another, younger, yellow tiefling. The teacher was there and told the children to attack each other. Saria was faster and incinerated the other tiefling. As Saria cried, Lenόre tried to comfort her, but Saria looked back at her in anger and turned away. Coming out of the trance, Lenόre told the others nothing, but she is deeply disturbed by her awakening memories.

That night the party stayed up late to examine the shield up close; after the show it had been hung on the wall. To distract young Toby, Lenόre asked him for a late-night snack, and while he went to fetch the inn's homemade marmalade, Nyra climbed behind the counter and touched the shield. Nothing happened. Toby came back, Lenόre got her snack and they all went to bed.

All except Nyra. She saw a strange shadow on the inn's rooftop and tried to follow it, but it was faster. When she finally tried to sleep she had nightmares and woke suddenly, thinking the inn was on fire. Going downstairs, she had a vision of the **Red Knight** (DM), who pushed her down and burned her shoulder. The innkeepers helped her and told the rest of the party.

The next morning the others let Nyra rest, and Lenόre left [[Oscar]] with her to keep an eye on her. At breakfast Lenόre ate a huge amount of sweet pancakes but saved some for Nyra. Once Nyra woke up, they talked with the innkeepers and decided it was time for the shield to go. They wrapped it carefully and took it with them, and left a **magical broom** behind for Toby, who was thrilled with his new magic item.
