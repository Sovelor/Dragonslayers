# Session 013 - The Chosen of the Flame

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Συνεδρία 013 - Ο Εκλεκτός της Φλόγας]]

## Recap
As [[Bergen]] absorbed the six souls of the cursed Staufen children into his amber gem, he rose as the new **Chosen of the Flame**. The **Griffon Company**, with [[Helmut]]'s help, fought him in the ritual chamber under the manor's chapel. The battle was hard, and Bergen, now an Angel of the Flame, struggled to master his new powers: because the Company had stopped the Ritual before he could corrupt and collect Helmut's soul as well, his powers were not complete. Helmut landed a telling blow on him, calling on the divine power of **Tyr** with *Smite Evil*.

Just as the Company was about to prevail, the temperature in the chamber shot up. A flaming *Gate* opened and the Harbinger of Fire herself, [[Pandora]], arrived. She turned to Bergen and accepted him as the Chosen of the Flame.

[[Grafin]] challenged Pandora to fight, readying his axe. In a show of power she incinerated him with a single spell, a *Scorching Ray*. The rest of the Company watched, unable to react, as their friend turned to ash. Seconds later, which felt like hours to them, Grafin was standing exactly where he had been before the Harbinger's attack, unharmed. Pandora told the Company that this is their future if they are foolish enough to resist her. Then she and Bergen stepped through the flaming *Gate* and were gone.

Helmut and the Company gathered the bodies of his siblings and carried them to the chapel. Helmut asked what had happened since he fainted at the meal, and Grafin and [[Nyra]] told him, without evasion, everything up to the moment they saved him from the Ritual. He then asked to see his father. The two men talked in a heavily charged atmosphere: **Lord Vandrik** asked Helmut's forgiveness and told him that the legacy and the name of Staufen now rest in his hands and on his shoulders. They spent the night together beside the bodies of Lady Staufen and the dead children of the family. Helmut swore to his father that he will make amends for his family's crimes and clear the Staufen name, and that he will find and free the souls of his six siblings from Bergen.

The next morning the people of **Brost**, once called **Staufendorf**, gathered outside the manor demanding the heads of the Staufens. They were led by [[Nestor Kirsten]], known to the party as old **Krip**, now a changed man: clean armour and cloak, and a well-groomed look. They call him General Nestor. Lord Vandrik gave himself up to Nestor, and the guard took him to prison.

The Company went back into the manor, to the last room they had not visited: the **Recreation Room**. There they faced the cursed spirit of [[Leopold Graufor]], the manor's jester and bard, who has haunted the room and its theatre since his violent death a few years ago. The whole fight was a performance of Leopold's: he hurled insults, toyed with the heroes' minds and tried to corrupt their souls. The Company found his body and the things he loved in life: his hat with the little bells, his flute and the crumpled papers with his jokes. These were the source of the haunting, and when the companions destroyed them, the room was cleansed and his tormented soul was set free.

1. Bergen absorbs the six souls into his amber gem and rises as the Chosen of the Flame
2. The Griffon Company and Helmut fight him; his powers are incomplete without Helmut's soul
