# Session 005 - A Day of Memories

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Συνεδρία 005 - Μια μέρα αναμνήσεων]]

## Recap
After her nightmare, [[Lenor|Lenόre]] woke in distress and tried to distract herself by reading her spells. In **New Keep** the party learned more about the **ettin**. People say it lives in the forest, in an old tower that seems to have been the lair of **Mira**'s father, **Erasmus**, and later hers as well. Even so, the villagers avoid the area because of orc camps near the path that leads there. **Matataias**, the leader of the guard, furious at the death of his father, wanted to take the few soldiers left and kill the ettin himself. With [[Robin]]'s help the party stepped in and agreed to go first and clear the path, and possibly the ettin too. They asked for a three-day head start before the guard acted: if the three days passed, the party would be counted as dead and the guard would have to finish the job.

In a chat with Robin, [[Nyra]] shared parts of her past. Robin had known Nyra's teacher, **Celandir Shadwoglim**, and was saddened to learn that he had been killed along with the other monks. Nyra arrived here as a slave from a different plane and was saved, by accident, by a man named [[Axel]]. Before that she lived in a monastery of the moon in the Stars Mountains, learning the path of the shadow. Someone set the monastery ablaze and burned everything; her teacher died trying to save her, telling her to get stronger and giving her his ring.

At the mention of the monastery's destruction Lenόre had her second flashback, which left her shocked and unmoving for some time. She remembered the voices of children repeating *"Blood and Death! We are the worthy ones! Death to the weak"*, and bits of a strict upbringing, learning magic from a sun elf. The teacher was extremely strict and always repeated *"You are the chosen ones!"*, and beat anyone who made a mistake with a birch. Then Lenόre remembered being surrounded by three older tieflings, one of them a gray-skinned girl who led the group and whom she thinks is her sister. The girl took a wooden toy from Lenόre and broke it, explaining that toys are for kids, and then all three kicked and hit her while Lenόre screamed *"Saria, no!"*.

Nyra shook Lenόre out of her memories, worried, and asked if she was all right. Lenόre said yes. They took a carriage from the village and set off for the ettin's hideout.

**Key beats**
1. Lenόre wakes from her nightmare and reads her spells to calm down
2. The ettin's lair: an old tower in the forest, once Erasmus's and then Mira's; orc camps on the path
3. Matataias wants to kill the ettin with the remaining guards; the party, with Robin's help, offers to go first
4. The deal: a three-day head start, after which the party is counted as dead
5. Nyra tells Robin about her past: Celandir's death, Axel, the burned monastery and the ring
6. Lenόre's second flashback: the sun-elf teacher, the birch and the three tieflings, "Saria, no!"
7. The party takes a carriage from the village towards the ettin's hideout

> [!warning] Conflicting accounts
> - **Nyra's teacher:** Lenόre's player writes "Selandir"; the DM confirms he is Celandir Shadwoglim, whose death in the burning monastery Nyra's profile describes.
> - **The monastery:** Lenόre's player places it in the "Tsoult forest" (the Jungles of Chult); the DM confirms Nyra's profile: it stood in the Stars Mountains, not in the place where her people were taken to be sold as slaves.
