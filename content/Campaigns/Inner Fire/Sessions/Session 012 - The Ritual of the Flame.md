# Session 012 - The Ritual of the Flame

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Συνεδρία 012 - Η Τελετή της Φλόγας]]

## Recap
The **Griffon Company** wandered deeper into **Staufen Manor**. On the second floor they went to **Rosalyn Staufen's study** (once the Staufens' own study), where a faint light flickered. The room had a thick luxurious carpet with footprints on it leading to a recliner sofa, with a table beside it holding a golden cup and empty plates. At the back a large fireplace smouldered, flanked by two statues: **Elzbeth Staufen** (the mother) seated and reading, and **Vandrik Staufen** standing proud. In front of the fireplace was a dusty desk with a book lying closed upside down: the Staufen family tree. It tells the family's history, which began 500 years ago with the founding of **Brost**: they always served the good and were sworn to the god **Tyr**, and by their agreement with Baldur's Gate the eldest son was to be dedicated, free of charge, to training paladins in the city's temple of Tyr. Vandrik and Elzbeth, however, sent their last son to be made a paladin of Tyr, not the eldest. Lenόre's player recalls the room as beautifully arranged, with many bookcases, and describes the family book as a generational record going back 500 years that shows a family always kind and aligned with the good, which seems strange given the current situation.
Besides the family tree the party found documents with sketches of the Abyss. The sense of them is that Elzbeth went mad and asked for power; the flame appeared to her with a truth, and she was to make a great sacrifice (seven sacrifices), her children becoming vessels of the fire and its power. **Bergen** is the Chosen, who spoke to her in dreams: *"When the seven vessels are gathered, his ascension will take place."* Nearby lay a neatly folded sheet with Bergen's instructions for Elzbeth to summon a **bearded devil**. Lenόre's player adds that the party also found Lady Elzbeth's diary: she had found peace in the truth shown to her by the priest, "the eclipse shall set everyone free", and she seemed to have lost her mind in her search for power, something **Bergen** took advantage of. He promised her power if she made seven sacrifices; she agreed and sacrificed her children, making each of them a vessel of power. Only Helmut, who had been sent away, was missing; after the sacrifice she would have her power and the priest would rise anew as a champion of the eclipse (the DM says the eclipse is not the Flame). She was the shadow that rode away on a horse when the party fought the bearded devil and saved Helmut.

They climbed back to the second floor and decided to see what was hidden in the bedrooms. There were seven doors, and outside one stood one of the family's personal servants, whom the party had seen at dinner. Five of the seven rooms were empty (those of Eric and Helmut, Chris, Rosalyn, Amalinda and Leanor). Lenόre's player describes them: Rosalyn's was locked, but inside everything was expensive and clean, and a strange whisper could be heard inside the walls; Chris had taken Helmut's room, showing his pride and jealousy of his brother; Leanor and Amalinda shared a room dirty with food; Chris's own room was filthy beyond belief, a sign of how bored he was of cleaning even his own things; and Jack's room, also locked, was the most disturbing, showing off different sadistic methods of torture he used for his own pleasure. Nyra appeared in the corridor where the servant stood; he looked at her in surprise and demanded to know why she was there and not in her room. As he came toward her she backed away, to draw him to where her hidden companions could see him. He stopped, turned back toward the central door and realised she was signalling her allies; at that moment he changed form, a flaming trident appearing in his hand, and struck the door, shouting in a deep voice "Intruders! Come out quickly!" Five more servants, also seen at dinner, came out of the room and all turned into demons.

The fight began with **[[Lenor|Lenόre]]**, for the first time decisive and sure of herself, stepping forward to hurl a ray of lightning at a demon. In Jack's room hung a naked man, hooked all over his body and chained from the ceiling, gagged and trying to ask for help. With great effort the party took him down and ungagged him, and Nyra gave him a healing potion to close his wounds a little. They told him to head for the servants' quarters and leave with the other servants they had smuggled out; he thanked them and went. He was **Varros**, the husband of **Maria**, a servant of the manor whom the Company had once rescued (DM).
The Company also defeated the demonic **Herlequins** on the second floor, with difficulty (Lenόre's player calls them the Herlequin butlers, creatures of the Abyss; the head Herlequin begged *"Lets not resolve to violence"*). The party also made a last stop at the stables to save the final two servant boys: for the first time **Lenόre** tried to intimidate the children in order to help them, and succeeded, but soon felt guilty. Then they discovered that behind everything that had happened was **Lady Elzbeth**, allied with the priest **Bergen**, and defeated Lord and Lady Staufen in their private bedchamber while the couple were at prayer. The fight was hard: Lord Staufen tried to protect the deranged Lady Elzbeth, who seemed unwilling to fight. Lady Elzbeth fell and burned in an unexpected fire that suddenly broke out on her, and the party knocked the Lord unconscious and chained him to his armchair.

They went deeper, down below the chapel (Lenόre's player: through a hidden hatch in the chapel) through the catacombs, where **Bergen** was performing the **Ritual of the Flame** with the six possessed children of the Staufens, trying to corrupt **Helmut** too. [[Grafin]] told him: *"We didn't come here to talk. We came to stop you!"* The Griffon Company interrupted the ritual, saving Helmut and using several tactics to exploit the weaknesses of the possessed children. Helmut, the young paladin, was released from his chains and joined the group against Bergen (DM); he fought at their side against his possessed siblings. In the fight all the siblings died except Helmut (Lenόre's player); Meive's player recalls that in the chapel Jack and Chris killed each other. Bergen used the corrupted souls of the children to raise himself as the **Chosen of the Flame**, changing into something no longer human and ready to collect the final soul, that of the last child; the ritual was only partly completed. The final battle with Bergen begins next session.

**Key beats**
1. Rosalyn's study: the family tree and documents on Elzbeth's pact with the flame and the seven vessels
2. The seven bedroom doors; the servants reveal themselves as demons
3. Fight with the demonic Herlequins; a tortured man (Varros) freed in Jack's room
4. Lady Elzbeth, allied with the priest Bergen, and Lord Staufen defeated at prayer
5. Under the chapel, through a hidden hatch: the Ritual of the Flame is interrupted and Helmut saved
6. The children turned against each other; Helmut joins the fight against his siblings
7. Bergen absorbs the corrupted souls of the six children and rises as the Chosen of the Flame, no longer human
8. At the stables, Lenόre intimidates the servant boys for the first time, and feels guilty afterwards (Lenόre's player)
