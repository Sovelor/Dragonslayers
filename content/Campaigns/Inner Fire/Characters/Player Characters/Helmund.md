# Helmund Staufen

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Χέλμουντ]]

**Role:** Party member in *Inner Fire* · human paladin of Tyr (he/him), "The Last Hope of House Staufen"
**Facts:** **Race:** Human · **Class:** Paladin of Tyr · **Pronouns:** he/him · **Alignment:** Lawful Good · **Deity:** Tyr, God of Justice · **Affiliation:** House Staufen · **Betrothed:** [[Lenor|Lenόre Bèliath]]

## Summary
The youngest of the seven children of the noble House Staufen and a paladin of Tyr, the God of Justice: "The Last Hope of House Staufen". Sent away to train as a paladin, he escaped the terrible fate that awaited his family. The Griffon Company saved him from the Ritual of the Flame in Session 12, and in Session 13 he fought beside them against his possessed siblings and against [[Bergen]]. He has sworn to restore the honor of his family's name and to free the souls of his six siblings from Bergen's grasp.

## Appearance
As pictured in his portrait: a young man with short fair hair and blue eyes, in plate armour over chain mail, a white tabard bearing the scales of justice, and a white hooded cloak, with a longsword in his hand.

## Personality
A devoted servant of Tyr, guided by a strong sense of justice, honor and responsibility. He believes that true justice is not merely about punishing the wicked, but also about protecting the innocent and offering redemption to those who can still be saved. Despite the tragedy that befell his family, he refuses to abandon his principles or to let vengeance consume him. He carries the burden of his family's past, determined to prove that the Staufen name can once again stand for righteousness.

## Background
Born into the noble House Staufen, a family with a centuries-old tradition of honor, faith and devotion to Tyr, Helmund was raised to believe that justice, duty and sacrifice are the foundations of a worthy life. As the youngest of seven children, he was sent away from his family's estate to train as a paladin of Tyr. What once seemed an ordinary path of religious devotion ultimately saved him from the terrible fate that awaited his family.

Upon his return, he discovered that his family's legacy had been corrupted by dark forces. His mother, **Lady Elzbeth Staufen**, had fallen under the influence of [[Bergen]], a servant of the Flame, whose ambitions led to the sacrifice of Helmund's six siblings. Helmund survived and fought alongside a group of adventurers to stop the ritual, but Bergen escaped, carrying the souls of his brothers and sisters.

## Goals & fears
**Goals:** to restore the honor of House Staufen and free the souls of his six siblings from Bergen's grasp; to bring Bergen to justice, confront the forces behind the Flame, and prevent other families from suffering the same fate.
**Fears:** his greatest challenge may not be defeating his enemies, but remaining faithful to Tyr when grief, anger and the desire for revenge threaten to overwhelm him.
