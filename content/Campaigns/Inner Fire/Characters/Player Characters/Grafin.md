# Grafin

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Γκράφιν]]

**Role:** Dwarf traveller and spellcaster from Splendarrmornn

## Summary
A dwarf of Splendarrmornn, a traveller for many years who set out with a pack on his back to know the world. His thirst for knowledge of secret magic lets him cast spells, and as a dwarf he is also trained in melee and weapons. He recognised [[Meive]] as a friend he had made on one of his journeys and shielded her when the adventurers surrounded them.
