# Lenόre Bèliath

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Λενόρ]]

![[Characters - Lenor.jpg|300]]

**Role:** Party member in *Inner Fire* · tiefling evocation wizard (she/her) with no memory of her past, travelling with her familiar [[Oscar]]
**Facts:** **Race:** Tiefling · **Class:** Wizard, School of Evocation (level 4) · **Pronouns:** she/her · **Age:** unknown · **Alignment:** neutral · **Home:** unknown · **Languages:** Common, Elven, Infernal, Draconic, Sylvan, Celestial

## Summary
A blue tiefling wizard trying to regain her lost memory. She knows only her name and her spells.

She joined the party in Session 3, when she ran out of the forest begging for help against spiders on the way to New Keep, and was relieved when the party agreed to travel on together. In Session 4 she and [[Nyra]] tried to cover up their appearance in New Keep. In Session 12 she stepped forward "for the first time decisive and sure of herself" and hurled a ray of lightning at a demon.

## Appearance
Medium-sized, with light blue skin and two long horns that sweep up and backwards in a shade that matches her skin. Her long wavy hair is a deep dark blue and reaches the middle of her back. Her eyes, a bright golden colour, are the only contrast in her appearance; her features are soft and gentle.

She wears a simple black dress that fits her upper body and flows comfortably below, with sleeves fitted tightly to her arms in black lace. She carries a leather-bound spellbook with red flowers on the cover and a simple straight staff with a similar pattern on its ends. Her familiar, [[Oscar]], rides on her horns or around her neck and blends in, since he has the same blue as her skin.

Her voice is soft, low and sweet, but usually fearful and stressed.

## Personality
Very timid and shy, but as soon as she is asked about anything magical she opens up fast and talks with enthusiasm. She has a sweet tooth and can consume large amounts of sugar, which she says helps her think better. She is afraid of almost everything and doesn't like to be away from shelter; nevertheless she is quite curious about the world and wants to explore many places, mostly for their culinary arts.

She usually stays back, afraid to engage, but is very capable of taking action when needed. She stands quietly, holding her staff close, with a warm soft smile, and always seems to be in thought, either trying to remember something or talking telepathically with Oscar. She likes to write in her journal about the places she visits and the best pastry she has tasted.

- **Ideal:** be kind to others; you can choose who you wish to be despite your past.
- **Bond:** Oscar.
- **Flaws:** fear and amnesia.

## Background
She remembers nothing of her past except small pieces that come back to her at random. The last thing she remembers is her sister, **Saria**, calling for her, and the two of them leaving together for an unknown place.

> [!info] Rumours and open mysteries
> Who and where her sister is, and where she comes from, are still unknown.

A second flashback shows her strict upbringing: children repeating "Blood and Death! We are the worthy ones! Death to the weak", a sun-elf teacher who repeated "You are the chosen ones!" and beat mistakes with a birch, and three older tieflings, led by a gray-skinned girl she thinks is her sister, who broke her wooden toy and beat her while she screamed "Saria, no!". %% src:IF-S005 d4 %%
