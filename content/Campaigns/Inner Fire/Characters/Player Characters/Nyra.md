# Nyra

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Νύρα]]

![[Characters - Nyra.jpg|300]]

**Role:** Party member in *Inner Fire* · Tabaxi Shadow Monk who serves the goddess [[Allalme Dragonborn|Allalme]]
**Facts:** Full name Nyra Nyx · Tabaxi · Monk, Shadow Monk (level 4) · she/her · 20 · Neutral · born in the Dimension of Beasts; trained at the monastery in the Stars Mountains · languages: Common, Tabaxi, Abyssal

## Summary
Nyra is a Tabaxi monk of the goddess Allalme. Taken from her home as a child to be sold as a slave, she was freed by a mutiny at sea, then found by the monk Celadrin Sandoglim, who took her to his monastery in the Stars Mountains and trained her. When the monastery burned down and her teacher died, she set out to find who set the fire, and why. In Session 1 she steps out of the shadows to save Meive and Grafin from slavers in the Olsen Forest, and the three travel on together.

## Appearance
Glossy black fur. Her right eye is sky blue and her left eye purple; though different colours, both glow faintly in the dark. She wears the simple garb of her monastery, but in black, with a black hooded cloak and a black scarf over her face. She usually keeps the hood up and the scarf on so as not to draw looks. Ornate bracers give her extra agility, and a ring with the symbol of Allalme hangs around her neck. She speaks with a strange accent: she is clearly from somewhere else.

## Personality
Full of energy and curiosity; calm, focused, determined and protective. Often curious, like a cat.

## Background
Nyra was born in the Dimension of Beasts. She remembers her family only dimly, as an image rather than clear memories. One day strangers came, burned her whole village, killed the adults and took the children with them to another dimension to sell them as slaves. In the Forest of Tsoult she was sold to a lord who lived far from the mainland, so she had to travel by ship to reach him. On board, one of the sailors, **Axel**, a man with sun-tanned skin and black hair and a specialist with the rapier, discovered that most of the passengers were slaves who had been bought. He led a mutiny and freed them all.

Back on land, Nyra ran from the cities of men and hid in a forest, sheltering in the hollow of a great plane tree and living on wild berries and whatever else nature offered. There a half-elf found her: pointed ears, grey eyes, and grey-silver hair, half tied back in a braid and the rest falling to his shoulders. He gave her water and bread, and she hesitantly took what he offered. For the next month he came every day, brought her food, taught her the Common tongue so she could understand others, and showed her ways to defend herself and fight. So she learned his name, **Celadrin Sandoglim**, and told him hers. He offered to take her to live with him at the monastery and teach her the monk's art and the powers his goddess could grant her. She hesitated, but in the end followed him. At the monastery in the **Stars Mountains** she was warmly welcomed, began her training and felt she had found a new family.

Some years later, coming back from her daily training in the forest, she saw the monastery in flames, with black smoke rising. She ran with all her strength to save her people but found no one: among the many burnt bodies she could not tell who was who. All she found, on the floor of her mentor's burnt room, was the ring he always wore. Full of anger and grief, she set out to find who burned her monastery, and why, and to avenge the death of her teacher, the elf she called Father.
