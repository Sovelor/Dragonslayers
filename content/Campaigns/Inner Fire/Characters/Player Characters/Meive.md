# Meive

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Μέιβ]]

**Role:** Party member in *Inner Fire* · halfling (she/her) · one of the three chosen of [[Mahaya (Lord of Time)|Mahaya]]

## Summary
A halfling from the small village of Haidown, north of the Snowy Peaks on the edge of the Silmistar Forest; a skilled tracker and a sling specialist. In Session 1 she is caught in a net trap in the Olsen Forest and meets Grafin, an old friend, and Nyra.

Seen in [[Nenya]]'s vision in *Ancient Blood*, carrying the same rune-marked [[Hourglass of Time]].
