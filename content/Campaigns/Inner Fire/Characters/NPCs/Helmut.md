# Helmut

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Χέλμουτ]]

**Role:** Youngest son of the Staufens, paladin of Tyr; ally of the Griffon Company

## Summary
The young paladin of Tyr whom the Griffon Company saved from the Ritual of the Flame. He fought beside them against his possessed siblings and against Bergen. His father told him that the legacy and name of Staufen now rest on him, and he swore to make amends for his family's crimes and to free the souls of his six siblings from Bergen.
