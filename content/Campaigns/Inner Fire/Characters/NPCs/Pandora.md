## Summary
The Harbinger of Fire. She arrived through a flaming Gate in the ritual chamber under Staufen Manor, accepted Bergen as her Chosen of the Flame, and showed the Griffon Company, in a vision of [[Grafin]] burned to ash, what awaits anyone who resists her.

## Appearance
A tall woman with pointed ears and a head of living flame for hair, her skin glowing violet-blue and her eyes burning. She wears black dragon-scale armour under a long cloak that blazes like fire, holding a fiery orb in one hand and a flaming trident-staff in the other.
