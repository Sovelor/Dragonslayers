# Leopold Graufor

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Λεοπόλδος Γκράουφορ]]

**Role:** Jester and bard of Staufen Manor; a cursed spirit, now at rest

## Summary
The jester and bard of Staufen Manor, who met a violent death a few years ago because of the change in the Staufens. His cursed spirit haunted the Recreation Room and its theatre until the Griffon Company found his body and destroyed the things he loved in life: his hat with the little bells, his flute and the crumpled papers with his jokes.
