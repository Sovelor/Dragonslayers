# Robin

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Ρόμπιν]]

**Role:** Druid, caretaker of the temple of New Keep

## Summary
A druid who looks after the place the villagers call the temple in New Keep. Friendlier than the new captain of the guard, he explained to the party that an **ettin** attacks the village by day. One head is always asleep while the other takes over: by day the aggressive head destroys everything, and by night a gentler head only visits the temple and prays.
