## Summary
A lizardfolk druid and follower of **Belak**.

She is Belak's apprentice: she and her band of lizardfolk have orders from Belak to watch over [[Irewin]] and protect her. She led an ambush on the party at Irewin's home while they rested. %% src:AB-S002b d2 %%

## Appearance
A lizardfolk woman with green scales and a crested, reptilian head, wearing a ragged moss-green cloak over a leather corset and skirt. She carries a curved sickle-blade and stands on a rock in a ruined, overgrown valley, with a small lizard at her feet.
