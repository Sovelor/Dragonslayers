# Penkous

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Πένκους]]

![[Characters - Penkous.jpg|300]]

**Role:** Dangerous smuggler of [[Shadowshore]], ally of [[Vanthus Vander]]

## Summary
Penkous Duskhead, a human and the biggest smuggler of [[Shadowshore]]. With [[Vanthus Vander]] he forced [[Panchie]] to sell them a caravel and turned an old pirate lair on [[Parrot Island]] into a smuggling warehouse. Vanthus betrayed him and left him to die in the hideout under the island, where he turned into a salt mummy. The party destroyed the mummy and learned who it was from his gold ring and his last, bloodstained letter.

## Appearance
A tall, gaunt human, seen as the salt mummy he became: his body is wrapped in rotting, moss-eaten rags, with a mask-like, ruined face and clawed hands. He stands in a flooded cavern among skulls and a treasure chest.
