# Lavinia Vander

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Λαβίνια Βάντερ]]

**Role:** Member of the Vander family · ally of the party

**Race:** human · **Occupation:** noble of the Vander family · **Pronouns:** she/her · **Age:** 18-19 · **Alignment:** good · **Home:** [[Daggerford]] · **Languages:** Common · **Where found:** [[Vander Manor]], in the Nobles' District of Daggerford · **Attitude to the party:** friendly

![[Characters - Lavinia Vander.png|300]]


## Summary
Owner of the [[Blue Nymph]]. The party returned her ship, her father's ring and gold, then escorted her to the family vault on [[Terraknian Island]], where she recovered the ownership documents she was looking for. She let [[Nenya]] keep the [[Hourglass of Time]]. Her brother [[Vanthus Vander]] is missing.

Daughter of Verik and Larissa Vander, members of the [[Order of Freedom]] and heroes of Daggerford, and twin sister of [[Vanthus Vander]]. A noble of the city and heir to the Vander fortune, she holds a prominent place in Daggerford because of her name. She begged the party to bring back the stolen ship that belonged to her parents and to learn where her brother has gone. **Astriel Shalyn**, who is investigating for the Order of Freedom how far Daggerford is controlled by the [[Zhentarim]], has asked the party to build ties with the city's nobles, and Lavinia matters to that plan.

## Appearance
Long blonde hair, carefully combed back, with a few strands left to frame her face. Her face has fine, harmonious features, her gaze is steady and direct, and her blue-green eyes are beautiful, expressive and full of kindness. She wears a refined noble's dress in deep blue, trimmed with silver-grey details and white fabric at the sleeves and bodice. An ornate pendant with a blue stone hangs at her neck, matched by earrings, and a thin chain of beads falls discreetly from the waist of the dress.

She speaks in a measured, quiet way, in a soft, sweet voice with a Daggerford accent. She does not seem very sure of herself; she is shy and hesitant.

## Personality
Lavinia is shy and reserved. She is not one to raise her voice in a discussion or claim the attention of a room. She prefers listening to speaking and often watches the people around her before giving an opinion, and when she does speak, she chooses her words with care and courtesy.

For all her timid nature, she is not weak. There is a quiet determination in her gaze: she can be afraid, doubt and worry, yet still go on when she has to. Hers is not the courage of the warrior who charges into battle, but of someone who bears hardship without complaint.

She finds it hard to believe in herself and often thinks others are more capable, cleverer or more important than she is; praise makes her lower her eyes or change the subject. Those who really know her see the exceptionally kind and loyal soul behind the shyness. She is compassionate, gentle with the weak and willing to help without expecting anything in return. Rather than judge, she tries to understand; rather than demand, she asks; and rather than seek glory, she finds worth in small acts of kindness.

## Goals & fears
She wants to preserve her family's good name and to be reunited with Vanthus, the only relative she has left, so as to bring him back to the path of virtue. She fears that her family's name and legacy will be disgraced, and that her brother has turned his back on the good.
