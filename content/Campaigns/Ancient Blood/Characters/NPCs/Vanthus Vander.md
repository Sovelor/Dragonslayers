# Vanthus Vander

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Βάνθους Βάντερ]]

**Role:** Lavinia's brother · missing

**Race:** human · **Occupation:** possibly a member of the newly formed [[Lotus Dragons]] · **Pronouns:** he/him · **Age:** 18-19 · **Home:** [[Daggerford]] · **Languages:** Common, Infernal · **Where found:** no one knows · **Attitude to the party:** hostile

![[Characters - Vanthus Vander.png|300]]


## Summary
According to [[Mumzar]], he often visited [[Terraknian Island]] with his wife [[Rowin]]. The party suspects him of raiding the Vander vault: gold was missing, a potion's bottles lay broken, and one lock of the inner safe was already open. Some believe he may be dead.

Twin brother of [[Lavinia Vander]]; their parents, Verik and Larissa Vander, died recently in strange circumstances. He left the [[Vander Manor]] several months ago and his trail was lost in the city of Daggerford. Finding him and discovering what he is up to is the main purpose of the mission Lavinia gives the heroes. In a room full of heroes, Vanthus is the one who draws the eye; in a room full of villains, he is the one who makes the rest wonder who is really at the top. When he smiles, no one is entirely sure whether they have just won a friend or made a very dangerous enemy.

## Appearance
He is tall and well built, with an athletic body that shows familiarity with both the sword and the hardships of the road. His shoulders are broad and he carries himself with a confidence that is almost provocative. His hair is long and unkempt, a dark brown close to black, falling irregularly around his face: not the groomed look of a courtier, but that of a man more interested in his deeds than his image. His face is angular and masculine, with a strong jaw, high cheekbones and a light beard. The permanent half smile at the corners of his lips makes it hard to tell whether he is amused, mocking, or has just thought of something dangerous.

His most striking feature is his eyes. Whoever meets his gaze hardly forgets it.

His clothes suggest a fallen noble and an adventurer at once: a heavy, dark coat of leather and cloth, worn by travel and conflict, over a white shirt and a garment of deep red. They are of good quality but battered, as if they once belonged to a nobleman and spent years far from the comforts of a manor. A silver pendant hangs at his neck, and on his right hand he wears a heavy signet ring, probably a family heirloom; both suggest descent from an old and powerful line.

His voice is low and calm. He rarely raises it, because he has no need to: when he speaks, most people fall silent of their own accord. There is a strange certainty in his words, as if he has already weighed them and knows exactly what result they will have.

## Background
Vanthus vanished from the [[Vander Manor]] several months ago, abandoning his family. A little later, when his parents lost their lives, he did not even appear to comfort Lavinia.
