# Axel Nightvale

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Άξελ]]

![[Characters - Axel.jpg|300]]

**Role:** Party member

**Race:** Human · **Class:** Swashbuckler / Fighter (level 4 + 1) · **Pronouns:** he/him · **Age:** 26 · **Home:** **Dagger Falls** (**Daggerdale**) · **Languages:** Common, Illuskan, Elven, Dwarven, Draconic, Orcish, Aquan, Giant

## Summary
A human swashbuckler from Dagger Falls, a sailor and pirate of the **Sea of Swords**, and a fugitive wanted by the [[Zhentarim]].

Took the *Duellist's Blade*, a rapier granting +2 AC against a single opponent in solo combat, and the *Elven Mithral Chain Shirt* from the Vander vault. [[Jareth]] once protested Axel's suspicion: *"You suspect ME?"*

In *Inner Fire* he is the sailor who led the mutiny on the slave ship and freed [[Nyra]] and her people (the same Axel, according to the DM).

## Appearance
About 1.85 m tall, with brown hair, green eyes and Mediterranean skin, his face and body crossed by many scars. He wears light armour over linen clothes.

> [!warning] Conflicting accounts
> Nyra's profile (*Inner Fire*) remembers the sailor who freed her people as sun-tanned with black hair; his player gives him brown hair and Mediterranean skin.

## Personality
Very polite; he bows a lot.

## Background
Axel was born and raised in Dagger Falls, in Daggerdale. When he was a teenager his mother confided in him that he was the son of someone very powerful. They were poor and stole to eat, living under the oppression of the Zhentarim, who had ruled the town for many years and had exiled its rightful lord, forcing him to live as an outlaw and a rebel in his own land. Some years ago Axel, with others, helped Lord **Randal Morn** take back his seat, driving off and killing Zhentarim. The Zhentarim put a bounty on everyone who had helped the lord, and Randal Morn told Axel it would be best to leave, as it had become too dangerous for him to live there.

He headed south towards **Sembia** and spent some time on the **Pirate Isles**. Looking for "work" there, he and his companions heard that some great merchants of **Amn** wanted help crossing the sea to the **Moonshae Isles**, having fallen victim to pirates many times. They went to **Athkatla** and met three of the region's great merchants, who paid them over 500 gold for every voyage, on condition that the goods arrived back safe and untouched; for a poor young man from Daggerdale, that kind of money existed only in his imagination. On the voyages to the islands things grew strange: screams came from the cargo crates. Searching them, they found that the merchants were flesh traders, taking women and children of every race as slaves and for pleasure. His companions told him to forget it, keep his well-paid job, and that he owed nothing to anyone.

One night, moored at **Oman Isle**, Axel could not sleep for guilt, as if the fate of those people were his fault. In the tavern he bought a drink for a half-orc at the next table who sat holding his head, close to tears: **Durgal**, tall and hulking but kind-hearted, the blacksmith of **Iron Keep**. His seven-year-old daughter had been missing for two days, and the authorities had found nothing. Suspicious, on the night before the ship was due to leave, Axel went down to the harbour, boarded the galley, searched it like a thief, opened the wooden crates and began setting people free. He found the girl, but the captain, who was asleep in his cabin rather than at the brothel with the rest of the crew, found him. Badly wounded and close to fainting, Axel prevailed: the next day the ship had no captain, not that one at least, and Axel had a brand-new sword he had always envied. He brought the girl back to Durgal at the tavern and fainted from loss of blood.

He woke two or three days later in a hunting cabin in the island's forest, where Durgal had hidden him. Durgal said he was in his debt and gave him his friendship forever, and a knife of black metal made by his grandfather. When Axel learned that the merchants had put a bounty on him too, he left one night with some fishermen, who took him across on their schooner. He is now a fugitive and a rebel, and he will always look after the poor and the weak.
