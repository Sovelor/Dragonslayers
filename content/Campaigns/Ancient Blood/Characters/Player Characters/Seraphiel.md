# Seraphiel Caran-Viriel

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Σεράφιελ]]

![[Characters - Seraphiel.jpg|300]]

**Role:** Party member (she/her) · paladin of [[Allalme Dragonborn|Allalme]] · cousin of [[Melda]]; joined in Session 13

**Race:** Star elf · **Class:** Paladin (level 5) · **Pronouns:** she/her · **Age:** 24 · **Alignment:** Lawful Good · **Home:** Evereska · **Languages:** Common, Elvish, Celestial

## Summary
A star-elf paladin of [[Allalme Dragonborn|Allalme]] from the lost city of **Evereska**: brave, solitary and single-minded, and the cousin of [[Melda]], her only family.

A kind paladin who was relieved to find her cousin [[Melda]] in town. She joined the party and sailed with them to [[Terraknian Island]].

## Appearance
Long black hair, silver eyes and pale skin. She wears heavy black armour bearing the holy symbol of Allalme, with purple and rose-gold details, carries a greatsword and keeps a dagger on her. Her voice is calm and rarely changes its tone.

In her portrait: a woman with pale skin, pointed ears and very long, wavy black hair, wearing black-and-purple plate armour engraved with trees and crescent moons. She holds a greatsword that glows violet, before a large crescent moon and banners with a tree emblem.

## Personality
Brave and solitary. Politely formal and distant, she does not wish to form personal ties with those around her. She is single-minded and will let nothing stand in the way of completing a mission. She does not show her thoughts or feelings. She loves nature and animals, where she finds a moment's peace.
- **Ideal:** to fulfil her holy purpose and be a worthy representative of her goddess
- **Bond:** her faith
- **Flaw:** she is cold and does not show her feelings; she finds it hard to become part of a group

## Background
Seraphiel was born and raised in **Evereska**, a city near the Forgotten Forest, where Allalme planted her Sacred Willow; its wardens are Kallirroi, a water nymph, and her husband Aliakmonas, a water genasi (see [[Circle of the Moon]]). Fourteen years ago Evereska suddenly vanished from the map.

Her parents were star elves, and her family was very close. She loved most of all her aunt's family (her mother's sister), and above all her cousin [[Melda]]. Her childhood was carefree and innocent: the two cousins were inseparable and always stood up for each other. Seraphiel was loved by all and everyone wanted her company, but from a young age she hated lies and mockery, so she spent her time mostly with the children who needed it most; she believed that being different is a strength, not a reason for scorn. That is how, growing up, she came to want to be a warrior and a defender of the weak. In their free hours the cousins played in Evereska's narrow cobbled lanes and listened to the songs and stories of **Essa**, a bard famous in those days.

She read a great deal and was hopelessly romantic, hoping to grow up and live a great love like those in her books, or like her parents'. She loved animals and walks in nature, and liked to watch the sky, the stars and the moon, whose returning fullness she found magical. She devoted herself to the goddess **Selûne** and decided to train as a paladin, while Melda, following the call of nature, wanted to become a druid. Their paths parted as they grew up, but they always found time for each other, in hard times and happy ones: they were like sisters.

One morning, old enough now to be allowed out of the city, the two girls went exploring and walked as far as the edge of the Forgotten Forest. Melda wanted to see it up close, but Seraphiel insisted they had gone too far and must go home. On the way back they could not find Evereska and its tall castles anywhere. *"I think we're simply lost. We must find shelter somewhere and set out again by daylight,"* Seraphiel said, and they spent the night in the hollow of a great plane tree. A small white rabbit with red eyes came to Seraphiel and a white cat with blue eyes to Melda; with many fireflies they kept the girls company and helped them find food. The cousins never found out what had happened to their city, and had to live on the edges of the Forgotten Forest for about four years; the animals often led them to its borders, but they never crossed them.

On the last night Melda prayed to Sylvanus and Selûne, and a silver beam of light fell towards the heart of the forest. The rabbit licked Seraphiel awake, and the two animals set off into the forest. *"Do you trust me?"* Melda asked, and Seraphiel nodded and took her hand. Trusting her cousin's instinct blindly, she walked hand in hand with her, under the protection of the moon, to the heart of the forest, where a vast willow spread silver beams and there were hidden houses, elves and humanoid creatures they had never seen before. Of their families they found only a few friends. There they learned of the fall of Selûne and the rise of Allalme, and began to believe in her. The two girls grew up under the care of Allalme's priestesses: Seraphiel became a holy warrior in Allalme's name, and Melda a druid of the Daughters of Allalme.

Seraphiel met the love of her life, **Elandor**, also a paladin. They fell in love quickly; for the first time since her family vanished she felt that luck had smiled on her. They decided their bond had to be blessed at once, and they were married, in a simple ceremony with few guests, so that as one they could devote their lives to their holy cause. Melda, the only family she had left, was there. After the wedding, shortly before their paths parted, the cousins spent two days together and promised always to be there for each other when needed, whatever the cost. Then Melda left for her training; by the time she came back, Seraphiel had already left for her own training and set out on her first mission with her husband.
