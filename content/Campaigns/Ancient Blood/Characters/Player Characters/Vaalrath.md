# Vaalrath

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Βάαλραθ]]

![[Characters - Vaalrath 4.png|300]]

**Role:** Party member · identified as an *Illumian* by the Vander vault golems · member of an Illumian cabal

**Race:** Illumian · **Class:** Wizard/Rogue (level 5) · **Pronouns:** he/him · **Home:** his cabal · **Languages:** Common, Infernal, Abyssal, Dragonic

## Summary
An Illumian wizard/rogue with glowing sigils that orbit his head, always looking for knowledge and not good with social skills. Flew back with [[Melda]] to report to [[Lavinia Vander]], returned her father's ring and the gold found on the [[Blue Nymph]], and learned from her about the family vault. Encouraged a frightened Lavinia: *"You won't always have someone to protect you, so you have to become strong!"* Carries the *Wand of Melf's Acid Arrow*.

## Appearance
Pale skin and a bald head, always well dressed and clean. Glowing sigils orbit his head.

## Personality
Eager to learn everything that can be "knowledge". Calm and almost emotionless, because he cannot easily process emotions; calculating and logical. His behaviour changes mysteriously when the sigils turn red.
- **Ideals:** knowledge, logic, adaptation
- **Bonds:** knowledge; his teammates; the Life-giving Prince; **Chlapis**, a summoned water elemental he found
- **Flaw:** cannot process emotions

## Background
The party found him trapped in a magic book. He has the mysterious power of returning inside the book and communicating through written words. He and his cabal are almost obsessively searching for knowledge about the Life-giving Prince. He knows nothing about his life beyond his life in the cabal, as if he had been born a few years ago even though he looks like a grown human.

> [!info] Rumours and open mysteries
> - Rumour: that he is not a human.
> - What exactly happens when the sigils turn red?
> - Who is the Life-giving Prince?

## Goals & fears
**Goal:** to learn everything about the Life-giving Prince. **Fear:** not learning everything about the Life-giving Prince.

## Relationships
- **[[Nenya]]:** he studies her to learn her magic, and promised her he will not learn Kelgor's Fire Bolt.
- **Chlapis:** ally, a summoned water elemental companion he found during a quest.

## Affiliations
- **Zar'keth Vash'tor**, an Illumian cabal ("The End of Punishment" in Common): his cabal, seeking the knowledge of **Zir'Vael Thul'Rakth** (the Life-giving Prince in Common). It is the same cabal as the Pilgrims of Mercy ("The End of Punishment") of *A New Beginning*, and the Life-giving Prince is the one of [[King Theron]] there, so Vaalrath's story connects the two campaigns.
