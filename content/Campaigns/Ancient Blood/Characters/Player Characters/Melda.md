# Melda

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Μέλντα]]

![[Characters - Melda.png|300]]

**Role:** Party member (she/her) · druid of [[Allalme Dragonborn|Allalme]] · cousin of [[Seraphiel]]

**Race:** Moon elf · **Class:** Druid (level 5) · **Pronouns:** she/her · **Age:** 24 · **Alignment:** Chaotic Good · **Home:** Evereska · **Languages:** Druidic, Elvish, Common, Draconic

## Summary
A moon elf druid of [[Allalme Dragonborn|Allalme]] from the lost city of **Evereska**: decisive, cheerful and always ready to help, and the cousin and closest friend of [[Seraphiel]].

Flew back with [[Vaalrath]] to inform [[Lavinia Vander]]. Hearing that her cousin was in town, she sought her out and reunited with [[Seraphiel]], who then joined the party.

## Appearance
Long silver hair, silver-grey eyes and fair skin. She wears light armour covered in forest leaves, the **Armor of the Fallen Leaves**, carries a small hand shield and a great staff, the **Quarterstaff of Life**, and has an elven scimitar at her waist. An earring with the symbol of Allalme is her focus. Her voice is melodious.

## Personality
Decisive, with a dynamic character; friendly and optimistic, smiling and always willing to help. She is deeply bound to nature.
- **Ideal:** to be worthy of her goddess by doing her will
- **Bond:** nature
- **Flaw:** when she is angry, her rage goes past every limit and out of her control; she has not yet learned to tame it

## Background
Melda was born and raised in **Evereska**, a city near the Forgotten Forest, where Allalme later planted one of her Sacred Willows, guarded by Kallirroi and Aliakmonas (see [[Circle of the Moon]]). Fourteen years ago Evereska suddenly vanished from the map.

Her father was a star elf, one of the rare exceptions her mother's family made in letting one of theirs marry outside the clan. From him she took her silver hair and silver-grey eyes, unusual among her mother's people, whose hair is black with silver highlights like the moon. She had a carefree childhood beside her cousin [[Seraphiel]], who always stood by her and defended her when other children mocked the colour of her hair; with her help Melda learned to love being different and to stand up for herself. The two spent their free hours in Evereska's narrow cobbled lanes, listening to the songs and stories of **Essa**, a bard famous in those days.

From a young age Melda was fascinated by how plants grow, how birds build their nests and spiders weave their webs, and she would approach animals and talk to them together with her father, who guided her towards the path of the naturalist he himself had once followed, until he fell in love with her mother and chose to stay with her for ever. Her father often spoke to her of **Sylvanus**, the god of nature, and her mother of the goddess **Selûne**, so she believed in both, with a slight preference for the god of nature, as she had already felt nature's call. Seraphiel devoted herself to Selûne and wished to train as a paladin; their paths began to part, but they always found time for each other.

When they were old enough to leave the city, the two girls went out one morning to explore and walked as far as the edge of the Forgotten Forest. Melda wanted to see it up close, but Seraphiel insisted they had gone too far, and they turned back. They never found Evereska again: the city with its tall castles was gone. *"I'm sure we took the right road,"* Melda said sadly. They sheltered in the hollow of a great plane tree, where a small white rabbit with red eyes came to Seraphiel, a white cat with blue eyes to Melda, and fireflies kept them company. For about four years they lived on the edges of the Forgotten Forest, helped by the animals, who often led them to its borders, though the girls never crossed them.

On the last night, Melda prayed with all her strength to Sylvanus and Selûne, and a silver beam of light fell towards the heart of the Forgotten Forest. The cat and the rabbit set off into the forest. Melda wiped her tears and held out her hand to Seraphiel: *"Do you trust me?"* Hand in hand, under the protection of the moon, they entered the forest for the first time. At its heart they found a vast willow spreading silver beams through the forest, and hidden houses, elves and humanoid creatures they had never seen before. Of their families they found only a few friends; there they learned of the fall of Selûne and the rise of Allalme, and began to believe in her. The two girls grew up under the care of Allalme's priestesses: Melda became a druid and joined the **Daughters of Allalme**, and Seraphiel became a holy warrior in Allalme's name. Shortly before their paths parted, Seraphiel married, and the cousins spent two days together before Melda left for her training.

Melda completed her training, but she was heartbroken, because her teacher had vanished. When she returned to the Forgotten Forest, Seraphiel had already left for her own training. Some time later her superiors gave Melda a mission: to find out what was happening in the **Misty Forest** and to find her teacher, **Belak**.

Belak was her teacher and mentor in the [[Circle of the Moon]]. On the orders of the Council of the Circle of the Moon she led a team to bring back Belak and [[Irewin]], who had betrayed the Circle; the team was defeated, and the two handed her over to the [[Daemonfey]]. %% src:AB-S002b d4 %%

She led the team [[Allalme Dragonborn|Allalme]] sent to investigate **Belak** and the **Galthia Tree**. The team was defeated; her three companions were taken to the **Dark Fortress** and Belak handed her over to **Aurora Drabek**. %% src:AB-S003 d6 %%

She woke in a room, with a human and a half-orc looking at her. She was freed from a magical metal circlet in the Hatchery's birthing room, full of rage and impatient to leave, with no memory of how she got there. Her name was in the [[Book of Coupling]], beside **Gorgomorz**, with the result left blank. %% src:AB-S001 d18 %%
