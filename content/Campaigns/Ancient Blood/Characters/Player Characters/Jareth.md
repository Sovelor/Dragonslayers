# Jareth

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Τζάρεθ]]

**Role:** Party member

## Summary
Played sailor songs as the party brought the [[Blue Nymph]] into the [[Daggerford]] harbour in broad daylight. When [[Axel]] doubted him: *"Εμένα υποψιάζεσαι; Που πριν λίγο στη μάχη σε βοηθούσα; Εμένα;"* ("You suspect ME? I was helping you in the fight a moment ago! Me?")

## Appearance
A young, handsome man with tousled light-brown hair and an easy smile. He wears a chain shirt under a dark cloak, plays a guitar-like lute and carries a rapier at his hip, standing outside a tavern called The Wandering Lute.
