# Ulf the Northman

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Ούλφ ο Βόρειος]]

![[Characters - Ulf the Northman.png|300]]

**Role:** Human fighter; former player character of the first party

**Race:** Human · **Class:** Fighter (level 2) · **Pronouns:** he/him · **Age:** young · **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral · **Home:** **Raven Rock**, near **Mirabar** · **Languages:** Common, Illuskan

## Summary
A human Northman of the **Raven Clan** who wants revenge on his father and to claim the leadership of the clan.

A human fighter who was travelling along the foothills of the Troll Mountains when the mountain exploded. He joined forces with the half-orc [[Liga]], and together they found the Hatchery and freed [[Melda]] and [[Nenya]]. He is no longer in the party; the DM says he is dead, maybe.

## Appearance
Dark blond hair, green eyes and fair skin. He wears medium armour with a woolly rhino hide for warmth, and speaks with a northern accent. His chest bears the giant X his father cut into it.

## Background
Ulf was born during a great winter snowstorm at **Raven Rock**, near **Mirabar**, into the Raven Clan. His father was one of the four greatest warriors the clan had ever seen, his mother a fearsome shieldmaiden. He grew up fast, tall and strong as the mountains he was born in.

At thirteen, as was the clan's custom, his parents took him on his first raid: a small village two days away, at the southern tip of the **Lurkwood**, attacked by about twenty raiders, five of them children. When the villagers fled or fell, Ulf asked why they had to kill everyone instead of just taking what they had and leaving. His father struck him until his mouth filled with blood, his mother ordered him to do it, and he shouted "No". His father beat him senseless, cut a giant X across his chest as unworthy, and left him for dead.

He woke five or six days later on a hay bed, taken in by an old woman named **Frida**, whom he came to call grandma. She was a brewer and a fine cook, and she raised him. His body healed within a couple of months; the deeper wounds did not. Frida often worked for a rich family nearby, and Ulf kept their children company; the son, **Brumeron**, dreamed of becoming a squire and then a city guard in **Silverymoon**.

From fifteen he worked as a lumberjack to help her, needing fewer than ten swings to fell a tree. Years later a passing rider on a warhorse (black hair, piercing green eyes, shining chainmail) stopped to watch him: "Nice swings lad. I hope someday you'll be a mercenary and use these swings to cut down some bloody orcs and trolls, and all the evil in those lands." From that moment Ulf knew he wanted to be an adventurer.

> [!info] Rumours and open mysteries
> Who the rider who set him on his path was, Ulf never learned.
