# Nenya

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Νένυα]]

![[Characters - Nenya.jpg|300]]

**Role:** Party member · Sun Elf · one of the three chosen of [[Mahaya (Lord of Time)|Mahaya]]

**Race:** Sun Elf · **Class:** Duskblade (level 5) · **Pronouns:** she/her · **Age:** 55 · **Alignment:** Chaotic Good · **Home:** **Evermeet** · **Languages:** Common, Elven, Draconic, Dwarven, Celestial, Infernal

## Summary
A Sun Elf Duskblade from the island of **Evermeet**, daughter of a noble house whose head sits on the **Royal Council**. She left home after arguing with her father, and she wants answers about her mother, whom she has never met, and about her mother's origins.

Killed the achaierai on the [[Blue Nymph]] with *Kelgor's Fire Bolt*. The vault golems branded her "an extremely dangerous Sun Elf who must be destroyed". Touching the [[Hourglass of Time]] pulled her into a vision where Mahaya, wearing her colours and face, chose her *among three* for his mission, alongside [[Elizium (A New Beginning)|Elizium]] and [[Meive]]. She accepted, and Lavinia let her keep the hourglass, which seems tied to her tribe.

## Appearance
Of medium build, with grey-blonde hair, green eyes and white skin. A scar runs from the middle of her face down to her jaw, and she has a tattoo on her neck. She wears a breastplate and carries a longsword. Her voice is melodic, with an elegant Elven accent.

## Personality
She carries herself regally, a little above everyone else, yet she is kind-hearted and fair. Patient but proud, generous but ruthless when she must be: she does not think herself superior to anyone, but she lets no one call her lesser. She likes chocolate and dancing, and she loves beautiful things: art, music, fine clothing, elegant surroundings.

She was taught from a young age to be strict, to follow the rules and to look and act as expected, and deep down she longs for the childhood she never had, for freedom and the fun of a simple life. She has felt lonely most of her life: no siblings, no partner, few bonds of her own. She is grateful for her comfortable life and cannot imagine living in a poor village, but she knows that family, friends and bonds matter more.

- **Ideal:** rulers and royals exist to help their people. Authority is powerful, but never for selfish reasons; those in charge should remember that their role is to serve others, not the other way around.
- **Bonds:** few, because she grew up in luxury, where authenticity and honesty were scarce.
- **Flaws:** she enjoys the comforts of her station too much (music, dancing, the arts), and she is easily offended, having been taught that the Sun Elves are a higher race. She is trying to change, and that is one more reason she was eager to leave home: to see the world and understand the needs of its people, not only her own.

## Background
She was born into one of the oldest noble houses of Evermeet, among halls of white marble and living trees shaped by magic. Raised in the Sun Elves' ideals of perfection, tradition and security, she learned court manners, composure and duty early, and came to believe that a crown, a title or a seat on the Council is a responsibility, not a reward. Drawn to what has been lost (forgotten history, buried secrets, magic beyond the reach of scholars), she chose the path between blade and spell: the blade taught her precision and decisiveness, magic taught her possibility and adaptability.

Her father, **Calix de Silva Roth**, sits on the Royal Council. She does not want to inherit a place there only by birth: she travels to learn what being worthy of it truly means, and to understand people whose struggles she has never seen. She also seeks the truth about her mother, who left before Nenya ever met her.

She was freed from a magical metal circlet in the Hatchery's birthing room, with no memory of her past at all. Her name was in the [[Book of Coupling]]. %% src:AB-S001 d17 %%

> [!info] Rumours and open mysteries
> Questions surround her mother's origins and lineage; some truths may have been forgotten, others deliberately buried. If Nenya's suspicions are right, she may be a descendant of **Queen Amlaruil**.

## Goals & fears
- **Goals:** to find the truth about her mother and her lineage; to become someone who deserves a place on the Council, and return to Evermeet with knowledge earned through experience.
- **Fears:** being alone; failing the people who need her.

## Relationships
- **Calix de Silva Roth**: her father, a member of the Royal Council of Evermeet. She left home after an argument with him.
