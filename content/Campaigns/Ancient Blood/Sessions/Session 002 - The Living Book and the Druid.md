# Session 002 - The Living Book and the Druid

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Συνεδρία 002 - Το ζωντανό βιβλίο και η δρυΐδης]]

## Recap
Still inside the [[The Hatchery|Hatchery]], [[Ulf the Northman|Ulf]], [[Liga]], [[Nenya]] and [[Melda]] rested in the private chamber of [[Aurora Drabek]] and searched for their personal belongings, Melda's earring in particular. They found a book that radiated magic, with only two words written on it: *UR/KRAU*. As they tried to read it, a man appeared before them: finely dressed, completely hairless, with glowing magical runes floating around his head. His name was [[Vaalrath]], an Illumian wizard and rogue on a personal mission of his own. Aurora had imprisoned him in the book when she caught him spying on her organisation, the [[Daemonfey]], a caste that worships demons and wants to create powerful mages by mixing the blood of elves and demons. Vaalrath had overheard Aurora and another woman saying they had found a way to achieve what they wanted. Grateful to be freed, he joined the party, a new player character from this point on.

As the companions left the Hatchery together, two strange extraplanar creatures attacked them: two **Ethereal Marauders**, which the party beat easily.

On the way out, Melda felt that the forest around them was falling sick, and had flashes of why and how she came to be here: she remembered her own group, other companions who had come here with her to find someone, though she could not remember whom or why. The party decided to find out why the forest was sick and why Melda had travelled this far. Melda spoke with an animal, and it confirmed that the forest was sick, because of a bad man who comes from the ruins in the **Great Canyon**.

They set out for the Great Canyon. Deeper in the forest, at a crossroads, they saw a hut and a lady tending her garden, and asked her name and the way. She was [[Irewin]], a hermit and a half-elf druid like Melda, though she said she had left that life behind. She took them in and told them her story. With her husband **Belak** she had served Melda's own goddess, [[Allalme Dragonborn|Allalme]], but they left their home and came here for Belak's research, to look after the [[Galthia Tree]], which Belak tends. Her husband had since gone away and asked her not to look for him: she had not seen him in years, as his experiments had driven him away from his old self and from her. She asked Melda and her companions to find her husband and stop him, because madness had taken hold of him. She also told them of Belak's experiment with the Galthia Tree and the two fruits it bears: a white one, which grows at the summer solstice and has healing powers, and a red one, which grows at the winter solstice and is deadly.

While the party rested, a band of lizardfolk led by a lizardfolk druid, [[Ualeyn]], attacked them, together with two **twig blights**. Irewin seemed to know Ualeyn and begged her to stop. Then she confessed to Melda who she really was and how she knew her. Irewin and Belak had been the Wardens of the Sacred Willow in the **Misty Forest**, but they broke away from the [[Circle of the Moon]] under the influence of a mysterious woman known only as the **Blood Seer**. Belak had also been Melda's teacher and mentor back in the Circle of the Moon. Melda and her group had in fact come here, on the orders of the Council of the Circle of the Moon, to find and stop Irewin and Belak. Melda's earlier team took on the mission to bring the traitors back, but it was defeated in battle; the two traitors handed Melda over to the Daemonfey and made an alliance with them. Now regretting what she and her husband had done, Irewin is trying to make amends to Melda by telling her how to find Belak in the [[Sunless Citadel]], where he experiments with the Galthia Tree and its magic fruits.

**Key beats**
1. Searching the Hatchery for their belongings (Melda's earring) in Aurora Drabek's chamber
2. A magic book shows the words *UR/KRAU*; Vaalrath, imprisoned in it by Aurora, appears and joins the party

4. Two Ethereal Marauders attack as the party leaves the Hatchery
