# Session 015 - Dragons, Blood and Salt!

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Συνεδρία 015 - Δράκοι, αίμα και αλάτι!]]

## Recap
At the start of the session [[Vaalrath]] suddenly came out of the book where he had been trapped; no one knows why he could not come out before. [[Jareth]] had stayed behind in the city and was not with the party on Parrot Island.

After [[Vanthus Vander]] killed [[Shefton Rosk]] and sealed the hatch, trapping the party in the crypt under [[Parrot Island]], the heroes pushed deeper to find a way out. Two doors lay ahead. With the help of **Chlapis** they learned that undead waited behind both: behind one they wore black robes, behind the other red.

They took the red door first, which [[Melda]] opened deftly by breaking its hinge. Inside were four **salt zombies** in red armour, cloaks and hoods, strange creatures that seemed full of sea water and salt. Around their necks hung a medallion with a symbol the party had never met before: a drop of blood ringed by strange signs. During the fight two **salt mephits** appeared; summoning circles were drawn on the floor of the room. The party killed them all and searched the room. [[Vaalrath]] saw that the circles had been deactivated by hand, and concluded that whoever summoned the mephits let them loose on purpose, and that they had turned everyone in the room into zombies by draining all the moisture from their bodies. Here the party found a **Wand of Detect Secret Doors**, which [[Nenya]] kept.

The other door was barred from the inside. Vaalrath was thinking of sending Chlapis through to unbar it when [[Seraphiel]] simply kicked it down. Behind it were six more salt zombies, four in black and two in red. The ones in black were dead [[Zhentarim]]: they bore the organization's tattoo on the arm and chest. The party destroyed them and went on.

Deeper in, the caves and chambers were flooded. Chlapis was sent ahead to scout and came running back in terror, chased by five **lacedons** (sea ghouls). They wore green hoods and gear and bore the tattoo of a dragon with a lotus in its mouth: probably members of the [[Lotus Dragons]]. The party killed them, using lightning, but [[Axel]] was bitten in the fight and, though no one knew it yet, infected.

After swimming through an opening they reached another door, barred from the other side and much sturdier. Vaalrath told Seraphiel to try breaking this one too, to prove what a bad idea it is to break through every door; her shoulder failed against it. Chlapis went in to unbar it and reported that behind it was something they should not meet. He had removed three planks when he felt pain, and a hideous hand reached out through another door inside. With one plank left, Vaalrath called him back, told Nenya that her warhammer would have better luck than a shoulder, and told everyone to prepare for a fight.

Nenya broke a hole in the door and, against Vaalrath's advice, put her hand through to remove the last plank. Something with badly dried-out skin seized her arm and began to drain the moisture from her body. She was left at the edge of death. Then the creature broke the door down: a human **salt mummy**, immensely strong, able to drain the moisture from anyone it touched.

The fight was long and hard. Melda turned into a dragon and struck it with her icy breath; Nenya hit it with fire magic and her hammer; Seraphiel burned it with the divine fire of her goddess; Vaalrath attacked with magical energy. Axel tried to get behind it to flank it. Vaalrath shouted *"No, don't do it!"*, but Axel did not listen: the mummy caught him by the face, pinned him to the wall and began to drain him, leaving him nearly dead. At that moment the sigils around Vaalrath's head turned red and he said in Infernal *"No means NO!"* He cast *Cat's Grace* on himself and, while Melda, Seraphiel and Nenya kept attacking, stepped in and finished the mummy with *Shocking Grasp*, saving Axel at the last moment.

Among the mummy's things they found a gold ring bearing the name [[Penkous|Penkous Duskhead]] and a bloodstained letter, written by Penkous himself in his last sane moments. The mummy was [[Penkous]], the smuggler: Vanthus had betrayed him and left him here to die, slowly and in torment, until he turned into a salt mummy.

When Melda tried to heal Axel, they discovered the infection from the ghoul's bite. Luckily they had a potion of *remove disease*, and Melda gave it to him.

Vaalrath, his sigils still red, then turned on Nenya and Axel and lectured them for throwing their lives away, speaking of himself in the third person. A little later his sigils changed back and he spoke calmly, as if someone else had taken part in that conversation and not he: he remembered nothing of it. When Nenya asked him whether he was aware of it, he said no, and added that knowledge comes from observation: if he cannot observe what is happening to him, the others should. Nenya promised they would keep watch.

**Key beats**
1. Trapped under Parrot Island, the party goes deeper to find a way out
2. Salt zombies in red, wearing a medallion with a drop of blood, and two salt mephits set loose on purpose
3. Nenya keeps a Wand of Detect Secret Doors
4. Salt zombies with the Zhentarim tattoo behind the barred door
5. Lacedons with the Lotus Dragons' tattoo in the flooded caves; Axel is bitten and infected
6. Nenya puts her hand through the last door and the salt mummy nearly kills her
7. The mummy pins Axel to the wall; Vaalrath's sigils turn red and he destroys it with *Shocking Grasp*
8. The mummy was Penkous, betrayed by Vanthus and left to die
9. Melda cures Axel with a potion of *remove disease*
10. Vaalrath lectures Nenya and Axel in the third person and then remembers nothing

> [!warning] Conflicting accounts
> - **How many:** Nenya's player counts four mephits, coming out of a well, and four ghouls; the DM and Vaalrath's player count two mephits and five lacedons, which is the version used.
> - **Who wore what:** Nenya's and Axel's players remember the six undead behind the second door all in black; Vaalrath's player has four in black and two in red, and the DM also mentions red armour and capes there. Axel's player remembers the green clothes and the Lotus Dragons' tattoo on further zombies, not on the ghouls; the DM and Vaalrath's player put them on the lacedons.
> - **The doors:** Axel's player has Melda open the first door by breaking its hinge; Vaalrath's player has Seraphiel kick open the barred one. Both are kept, one for each door.
> - **Spellings:** Axel's player writes Penkus; the DM's Penkous is used.

## Places
- [[Parrot Island]]: the underground hideout beneath it, with a storage area, an operations chamber, and flooded caves and chambers deeper in

## Fights
- **Salt zombies in red armour and cloaks, with two salt mephits**, in the storage area: an easy win
- **Salt zombies with the marks of the Zhentarim, and others in red**, in the operations chamber: an easy win
- **Lacedons** (sea ghouls) with the Lotus Dragons' tattoo, in the flooded caves: an easy win, but Axel was bitten and infected
- **Penkous, the salt mummy**: a hard-won victory; the creature hurt Axel and Nenya very badly. Seraphiel used *Lay on Hands*, Nenya *Kelgor's Fire Bolt*, and Vaalrath landed the final blow with *Shocking Grasp*
