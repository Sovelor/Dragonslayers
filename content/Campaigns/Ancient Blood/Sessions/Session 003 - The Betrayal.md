# Session 003 - The Betrayal

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Συνεδρία 003 - Η προδοσία]]

## Recap
**Irewyn** knew about [[Melda]]'s past. She and her husband **Belak** had belonged to the **Circle of Allalme**, and had left because [[Allalme Dragonborn|Allalme]] told them to come here and "heal" the forest. But the **Galthia Tree** corrupted Belak, and he wanted it for himself. So Allalme sent a team to investigate, and Melda was its leader.

The team was defeated. Melda's three companions were taken to the **Dark Fortress** (the Sunless Fortress), and Belak handed Melda over to **Aurora Drabek**.

In the swamps of the Plain of Ash, the party fought **Ualein**, a lizardfolk druid and follower of Belak.

**Key beats**
1. Irewyn reveals Melda's past: she and Belak belonged to the Circle of Allalme
2. Allalme sent them to heal the forest, but the Galthia Tree corrupted Belak
3. Allalme sent a team led by Melda to investigate; it was defeated
4. Melda's three companions taken to the Dark Fortress; Belak handed Melda to Aurora Drabek
5. Fight with Ualein, Belak's lizardfolk follower, in the swamps
