# Session 003 - The Betrayal

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Συνεδρία 003 - Η προδοσία]]

## Recap
> [!info] The same scene as Session 2
> Irewin's confession and the fight with Ualeyn are the scene of [[Session 002 - The Living Book and the Druid|Session 2]]: the party fought Ualeyn at Irewin's house, while they were trying to rest (DM). Melda's player's notes for this session tell it again, from Melda's side.

[[Irewin]] knew about [[Melda]]'s past. She and her husband **Belak** had belonged to the [[Circle of the Moon]], and had left because [[Allalme Dragonborn|Allalme]] told them to come here and "heal" the forest. But the [[Galthia Tree]] corrupted Belak, and he wanted it for himself. So Allalme sent a team to investigate, and Melda was its leader.

The team was defeated. Melda's three companions were taken to the [[Sunless Citadel]] (also called the Dark Fortress), and Belak handed Melda over to [[Aurora Drabek]].

At Irewin's house, the party fought [[Ualeyn]], a lizardfolk druid and follower of Belak.

**Key beats**
1. Irewin reveals Melda's past: she and Belak belonged to the Circle of the Moon
2. Allalme sent them to heal the forest, but the Galthia Tree corrupted Belak
3. Allalme sent a team led by Melda to investigate; it was defeated
4. Melda's three companions taken to the Sunless Citadel; Belak handed Melda to Aurora Drabek
5. Fight with Ualeyn, Belak's lizardfolk follower, at Irewin's house
