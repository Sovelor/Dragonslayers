# Daemonfey

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Ντέιμον-φέι]]

**Role:** Caste that worships demons and breeds mages

## Summary
A caste that worships demons and wants to create powerful mages by mixing the blood of elves and demons. Its symbol was found on the cloak of [[Aurora Drabek]], and the [[The Hatchery|Hatchery]] belongs to it. What [[Vaalrath]] told the party about it is all that is known so far.
