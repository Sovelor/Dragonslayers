# Book of Coupling

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Βιβλίο Ζεύξης]]

**Role:** Record of the Hatchery's unholy unions

## Summary
A thick, leather-bound catalogue found on a desk in the [[The Hatchery|Hatchery]]: a left column with a name (elven, most likely), a middle one with another (certainly demonic) and a right one with the result, "failure" or "success". In more than 500 pages only two successes are recorded, **Hawi** and **Vris**, with no demon named. The names of [[Nenya]] and of [[Melda]] (beside **Gorgomorz**, result left blank) are in it.
