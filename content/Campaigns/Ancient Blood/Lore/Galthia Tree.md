# Galthia Tree

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Δέντρο Γκάλθιας]]

**Role:** Tree that corrupted Belak

## Summary
A tree that [[Irewin]] and her husband **Belak** came to look after. At the summer solstice it bears a white fruit (life); at the winter solstice, a red fruit (death). It corrupted Belak, who wanted it for himself.
