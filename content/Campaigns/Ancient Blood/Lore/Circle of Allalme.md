# Circle of Allalme

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Κύκλος της Αλλάλμε]]

**Role:** Circle of druids of Allalme that Irewin and Belak belonged to

## Summary
A circle of the druids of [[Allalme Dragonborn|Allalme]]. [[Irewin]] and her husband **Belak** belonged to it until Allalme told them to come to the forest and "heal" it. When the [[Galthia Tree]] corrupted Belak, Allalme sent a team from the circle to investigate, led by [[Melda]]. The team was defeated.
