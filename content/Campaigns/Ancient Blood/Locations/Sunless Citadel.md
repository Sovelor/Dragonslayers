# Sunless Citadel

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Ανήλιαγο Οχυρό]]

**Role:** Dungeon fortress where Melda's three companions were taken

## Summary
A dungeon fortress, also called the Dark Fortress or the Sunless Fortress. **Belak** experiments here with the [[Galthia Tree]] and its magic fruits, and [[Melda]]'s three companions were taken here after their team was defeated. Its entrance hall has a mural of the red dragon **Ashardalon** on the ceiling and, when the party arrived, a small white dragon dead for one or two months, probably **Kalgrix**. A battle between goblins and young hatchling dragons had been fought inside. The party fought goblins and found water mephits imprisoned in a barrel by the Dragon Priest of Ashardalon.
