# The Hatchery

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Το Εκκολαπτήριο]]

**Role:** Hidden complex of the Daemonfey inside the Troll Mountains

## Summary
A hidden complex inside the Troll Mountains, belonging to a sect that worships devilish beings, with a strong presence of Asmodeus. It has a birthing room, a summoning area, a huge pit of corpses and a sacrificial altar. It is a hatchery of the [[Daemonfey]], who want to create powerful mages by mixing the blood of elves and demons. [[Melda]] and [[Nenya]] were held here, and [[Aurora Drabek]] was killed here.
