# Parrot Island

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Νησί των Παπαγάλων]]

**Role:** Old pirate lair west of [[Daggerford]], possibly a Lotus Dragons smuggling warehouse

## Summary
The old lair of the region's pirates, turned into a smuggling warehouse by [[Vanthus Vander]] and the smuggler Penkous. A hidden hatch in its forest leads down to a crypt, where Vanthus trapped the party.

Beneath the island lies an underground hideout with a storage area, an operations chamber and flooded caves. The party found it full of undead: salt zombies, lacedons and the salt mummy that the smuggler Penkous had become. %% src:AB-S015 d12 %%
