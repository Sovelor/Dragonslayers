# Dark Fortress

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Σκοτεινό Οχυρό]]

**Role:** Dungeon fortress where Melda's three companions were taken

## Summary
A dungeon fortress, also called the Sunless Fortress. [[Melda]]'s three companions were taken here after their team was defeated. Its entrance hall has a mural of the red dragon **Arsandalon** on the ceiling and, when the party arrived, a small white dragon dead for one or two months, probably **Kalgrix**. A battle between goblins and young hatchling dragons had been fought inside. The party fought goblins and found water mephits imprisoned in a barrel by the Dragon Priest of Arsandalon.
