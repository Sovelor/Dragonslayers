# Session 007 - Selina's Omen

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Συνεδρία 007 - Ο οιωνός της Σελίνα]]

> [!info] One account
> Only Ezariel's player has logged this session so far.

## Recap
As [[Elizium (A New Beginning)|Elizium]] and [[Ezariel]] went to meditate for the night, [[Selina]] took the watch and decided to cast *Omen of Peril*, a spell that lets her see the dangers ahead. It took a long time, and the vision was deeply disturbing. She saw [[King Theron|the king]] himself, in the days of his rule, killing all his friends and companions and telling her that they had betrayed him and deserved it. He also told her that he knew her in some way, but could not remember from where. Selina broke off the spell, not wanting to see more.

The next morning the party went on and began to find traps. Ezariel and [[Edric]] disagreed about opening locked doors; Edric set off a trap and was nearly hurt, and Elizium saved him at the last moment. Further in they found a room filled with necromantic energy. Selina, very upset, pushed on until the party came to a strange place: a huge area with a cube in the middle, like a separate room in itself. Inside it stood a construction of some sort, brimming with a necromancy that almost made Selina vomit.

There they met an undead centaur, other undead and an orcish cleric who claimed to be **Zeeron** the Illumian. The party knew he was lying. The orc had a strange crystal on its head, and they quickly understood that someone else was speaking through it. The fight began, and after some time things looked difficult. Ezariel drank a potion that made her invisible to the undead and slipped inside the cube room to eliminate the orc. [[Azhyra]] took out a strange dagger that no one had seen before, secretly activated it, tore a piece of the air and appeared in the same spot as Ezariel. Only Ezariel saw it, and she said nothing to anyone.

The party came out of the fight on edge, and everyone was in a bad mood.

**Key beats**
1. On watch, Selina casts *Omen of Peril* and sees the king killing his friends and companions
2. The king says he knows her from somewhere; she breaks off the spell
3. Traps in the morning: Ezariel and Edric disagree about locked doors, and Elizium saves Edric at the last moment
4. A room of necromantic energy, then a huge area with a cube at its centre and a construction brimming with necromancy
5. An orcish cleric claims to be Zeeron the Illumian; someone is speaking through the crystal on his head
6. Ezariel goes invisible to the undead and gets into the cube room
7. Azhyra's strange dagger tears the air and brings her to the same spot; only Ezariel sees it
