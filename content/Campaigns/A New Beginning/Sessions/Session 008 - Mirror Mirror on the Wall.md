# Session 008 - Mirror Mirror on the Wall

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Συνεδρία 008 - Καθρέφτη, καθρεφτάκι μου]]

> [!info] One account
> Only Ezariel's player has logged this session so far.

## Recap
The party entered a **maze of mirrors**. In some they could see only themselves. In others they saw parts of the story of the [[King Theron|Life-giving Prince]]: his beloved friends and family, his kingdom, but also his failures and fears. Other mirrors showed each of them aspects of their own lives, people they miss, days of their past, and sometimes a dark version of themselves, either bad or terribly hurt. [[Elizium (A New Beginning)|Elizium]] could only ever see himself: in the mirrors that showed the past, he seemed to see nothing.

They reached a room with a single altar, whose inscription told them to sacrifice a memory in order to continue, and each chose a memory they were willing to part with. In the next room they all tried to work out whether they had really given one away. Elizium solved the mystery, saying that the past year, since the day he woke up, was all he remembered. In the room after that stood two mirrors, one showing the best version of yourself and the other the absolute worst. Each member looked at both, but they all had to go through the same one. [[Azhyra]] seemed disappointed that they chose the good version, and very satisfied with the bad mirror.

They then found a trap hatch in the floor with a badly hurt woman inside. They rescued her, and she explained that she was the last member of the original team hired by the Illumian at the inn. The party told her that only one more of her allies was alive, and that this ally had left the city by now. Beyond lay an open natural cave with water, a waterfall and, strangely enough, a ladder leading outside, so they helped her out of the dungeon. Before she left, she told them what to expect.

Further in they met some spiders. Elizium, ever willing to set things on fire, burned the room right before the door closed; he killed all the spiders and hurt no one. In the charcoal-coated room he found a small **bag of holding**.

The next room held tapestries and intricate murals that continued the story of the prince, and then came two rooms like memorials or prisons, built for the prince's friend and for his wife. The woman's tomb was pierced with swords, as if someone had tried to keep her inside. The other was empty. In the next room the party found the prince's friend, sitting on a throne, cursed to go on living, but barely. In pain, he told them he was the one who had betrayed the prince: he had loved the prince's wife and killed him, and the prince's followers then caught him and cursed him to suffer forever as punishment.

As he told his story, [[Ezariel]] tried to sneak around the room to find the Illumians, but the traitor saw her and stood up, ready to fight. Before him stood a mirror that gave him sight over the dungeon and drove mad whoever looked into it. [[Edric]] looked, but nothing bad happened; he even gained a boost that upset the traitor, and the party killed him.

In the next and final room, exhausted, the party at last found all the [[The Illumians|Illumians]]. After some conversation about what a real hero is, they ended up fighting, and the party prevailed. The ghost of the Life-giving Prince appeared and asked them "what is a hero". They each gave an answer that left him satisfied.

That night Ezariel was extremely irritated at the amount of danger she had been in, arguing that this was something she shouldn't be doing, and Edric tried to calm everyone down. Azhyra was still thinking about her evil version in the mirror. Elizium was the only one satisfied: he finally had his new bag of holding.

**Key beats**
1. The maze of mirrors shows the prince's story and each hero's past; Elizium sees only himself
2. The altar demands a memory from each of them; Elizium says he remembers only the past year
3. Two mirrors, the best and the worst of yourself; Azhyra prefers the worst
4. A badly hurt woman, the last of the team hired by the Illumian, is rescued from a trap hatch and leaves by a ladder
5. Elizium burns a room full of spiders and finds a bag of holding
6. Tapestries and murals; the sword-pierced tomb of the prince's wife and the empty one of his friend
7. The cursed traitor confesses from his throne; Edric survives his mirror and the party kills him
8. In the last room the party fights the Illumians and prevails
9. The ghost of the Life-giving Prince asks "what is a hero" and is satisfied with their answers
