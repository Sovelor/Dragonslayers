# Session 001 - A New Beginning

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Συνεδρία 001 - Μια Νέα Αρχή]]

## Recap
*The DM's account of the setup:* **Selina Nightwell**, a half-elf cleric of Allalme and a Knight of the Brotherhood of Light, took an important mission from Lady Lavern herself: to find out what had happened in the faraway village of Brook Hollow. **Lia**, an apprentice cleric of Torm based in the village, had sent the Brotherhood a worrying message asking for urgent help, because the village's High Priest, **Pastor Darion**, had died in strange circumstances. Selina set out accompanied by **Azhyra Vorneth**, a tiefling warlock sent along to watch over her. The two travelled to the small oasis town of **Sayantar**, near the border of the Anauroch desert with Cormanthor, the ancient kingdom of the first elves. There they added a young recruit of the Sayantar Branch, **Edric Hale**, who gladly accepted the task of escorting Selina.

In the middle of summer, **Selina** and **Azhyra** left the [[Brotherhood of Light]] for [[Brook Hollow]], a village in the far east. On the orders of **Lady Lavern**, they were to find out what had happened to [[Pastor Lia]], a cleric of the village. It was Selina's first mission as a new cleric and she wanted to prove her worth; Azhyra came along, in a way, as her supervisor.

As they left, they met the third member of their small group: **Edric**, a human fighter and an associate from the desert department, also on his first mission and there to protect them both. Closer to the border with Cormanthor they met [[Elizium (A New Beginning)|Elizium]], an elf warrior-mage walking in the opposite direction, who said he knew the area well and offered to help them find Brook Hollow, since he had come that way. Asked where he came from, he answered "I come from distant and dangerous places".

Just as the introductions among the four travellers were ending, they came upon a young half-elf woman with short red (orange) hair, wounded, her hands bound in manacles and wearing a silver mask that covered two thirds of her face, begging for help: this was **Ezariel McAlister**. The party helped free her from her shackles, and then strange zombies, which seemed to come out of the forest, attacked. They all fought the zombies together, and once it was over Ezariel was the first to go through the corpses to get her belongings back, shrugging off Elizium's remark that good manners demand the rescued thank their saviours politely. With the rush of the fight gone, the five agreed to travel together for the next few days, each for their own personal reasons: they also had better odds of surviving that way. Everyone was suspicious of everyone else, except Edric, who was delighted to meet them all, and Selina, who simply was not interested, thinking of her mission; hints, insults and suspicions followed one after the other.

Night fell in the desert, and the party took shelter in an abandoned watchtower outside the village. It was suspicious: dirty, as if no one had been there for a long time, and as if someone had been killed inside.

The next day they made their way to the village. With a bad feeling about it all, Selina cast an *omen of peril* to glimpse the dangers ahead. The vision was terrible, full of death and blood, and as she spoke of it the party entered Brook Hollow. Every villager had been turned into a strange zombie: not the usual undead, but something new and dark.

**Key beats**
1. Midsummer: Selina's first mission, from Lady Lavern, to learn what happened in Brook Hollow after the cleric Lia's urgent message and Pastor Darion's strange death; Azhyra as her supervisor
2. Edric, a fighter from the desert department, joins to protect them
3. Elizium, an elf warrior-mage, offers them safe passage
4. Fight with strange zombies; Ezariel rescued and her belongings recovered
5. The group agrees to stick together
6. Night in an abandoned, blood-stained watchtower outside the village
7. Selina's *omen of peril*: a vision of death and blood
8. Brook Hollow: every villager turned into a new kind of zombie
