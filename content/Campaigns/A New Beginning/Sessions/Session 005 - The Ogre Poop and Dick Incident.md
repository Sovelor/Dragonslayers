# Session 005 - The Ogre Poop and Dick Incident

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Συνεδρία 005 - Το Περιστατικό με τις Κουράδες και το Πουλί του Όγκρου]]

## Recap
Descending further into the ruins, the party found a door inscribed *"Here lie Verdun and Gola"*, with statues of the two: fallen heroes of the past. Nearby, a small spring of magical water took away the fatigue of anyone who drank from it. The room beyond was their tomb, and the party found some useful scrolls there.

Further into the dungeon they reached the top of a staircase. Ezariel tried to sneak a peek around the corner, but at the same moment, at the same corner, the enemy was trying to see who was there. The result: Ezariel came face to face with an **ogre's** private parts, and was traumatised. Elizium found it hilarious, and after the fight he teased Ezariel with some of the ogre's poop from a nearby pile. Searching the pile, he found a gem and put it in his bag.

The group went on to a **room of riddles**. Its door was shut and could only be opened by solving a puzzle set by a **beholder**. More riddles waited inside, and after a long time they finally moved on to the next chamber, leading further down into the dungeon.

**Key beats**
1. The tomb of Verdun and Gola, fallen heroes, with their statues
2. A magical spring that removes fatigue; useful scrolls in the tomb
3. Ezariel and the ogre peek around the same corner
4. The ogre fight; Elizium teases Ezariel with ogre poop and finds a gem
5. The room of riddles: a beholder's puzzle opens the door
6. More riddles, then the way further down
