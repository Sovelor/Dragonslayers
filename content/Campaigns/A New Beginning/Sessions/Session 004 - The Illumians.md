# Session 004 - The Illumians

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Συνεδρία 004 - Οι Ιλλούμιαν]]

## Recap
The bald humans the party had met are called **Illumians**: a strange race who seem to worship knowledge and gather all of it that exists. They claim to have been cleansed of distractions such as feelings. Letters float around their heads and change colour with their mood, and their inn room holds an unbelievable number of scrolls and books written in an unknown language.

A group of Illumians had split up, and three of them, **Zeeron**, **Lyira** and **Terail**, went to a place and never came back. The search party sent after them never returned either. The Illumians said they were worried, since their race is very peaceful, and asked the party to investigate. In exchange they would send an urgent letter to the Brotherhood of Light by magical means. They paid up front, and the party set out.

At the **ruins of the Life-giving Prince**, a pack of wolves and a dire wolf were guarding an entrance. In the first chamber inside, the party found the previous search team dead. Pressing on, they fought a band of orcs and found a lone survivor, an elf woman. She told them that the **leader of the Illumians** had been giving the orcs their orders, and that he was the one who killed her friends.

**Key beats**
1. The Illumians: knowledge-seekers "cleansed of feelings", with floating letters that change colour
2. Three Illumians (Zeeron, Lyira, Terail) and their search party are missing
3. The party takes the job; the Illumians will send an urgent letter to the Brotherhood of Light
4. Wolves and a dire wolf guard the entrance to the ruins of the Life-giving Prince
5. The previous search team found dead; a fight with orcs
6. The lone survivor, an elf: the Illumians' leader commanded the orcs and killed her friends
