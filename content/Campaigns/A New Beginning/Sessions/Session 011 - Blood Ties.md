# Session 011 - Blood Ties

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Συνεδρία 011 - Δεσμοί Αίματος]]

> [!quote] Lilith's player's account
> Much of this recap comes from the detailed account Lilith's player wrote of the session. Read it in full, in her own words: [[Blood Ties, Lilith's player's account]].

## Recap
The party reached **Saiyaddar**, [[Edric]]'s oasis city in the Anauroch desert, escorted by Edric's three childhood friends **Ferith**, **Scioh** and **Wolly**, whom they had saved from the satyr. On the way the three told Edric that the patrols had been increased: they had lost the satyr's trail three or four days earlier and it had taken them prisoner, which rarely happens. Creatures and monsters had been roaming the area since before Edric left, about a month and a half ago, but lately it had grown far worse. Saiyaddar is a small, multicultural city of about a thousand people, mostly humans and half-elves with a few elves and dwarves, small enough to walk around in an hour; it grew up in the desert because there was water, and people call it **Anauroch's Emerald**. It is built around its oasis, with a giant statue in the middle: a monument to the city's hero, **Galag**, Edric's mentor, who vanished some years ago. The people welcomed Edric, [[Selina]] and [[Azhyra]] with honours, as heroes, and Edric knelt to pay his respects at a place in his home city.

On the carriage, which [[Elizium (A New Beginning)|Elizium]] was driving, [[Ezariel]] told him that she needed to speak with him alone: she needed his help, in complete secrecy, even from the rest of the party. Selina and Azhyra were due to leave the next day, and Elizium told Selina that he would go with them. Azhyra did not want to go, but said nothing.

The group split up for a while. Selina and Azhyra bought supplies for their journey and went straight to the city's Garrison headquarters to announce the party's return and report that Edric had completed his mission. Edric set off there too with his childhood friends, but the townsfolk kept stopping him to welcome him home, which made him a little uncomfortable. He reported that the group was back and that all had gone well; asked whether he was happy in the Brotherhood of Light, he spoke of his group in the warmest terms, and when they admired his new sword he told them he had named it **Thelisis**, from the Greek for "will" (he names all his gear). The Garrison Commander released him from duty, telling him to report to the Branch as well and to go and see his parents, who would be worried.

[[Elizium (A New Beginning)|Elizium]] and [[Ezariel]] left the carriage at the city stables, free of charge since it was Edric's, and a local pointed them to the **Golden Camel** inn and the city's other sights. On the way Elizium told her again that he has no memories beyond what he remembers of the hourglass, and he was completely honest about it. When Ezariel asked for his help and told him that what she wanted to discuss must never be told to anyone, that he must take it to his grave if need be, Elizium seemed to lose himself, as if he could no longer hear her: he saw himself dying, wounded all over and with a shattered ankle, in a frozen place high in the mountains. When she reached out to touch him he caught her deftly, took her sword and held it to her throat, then threw it down and seemed to come back to himself. *"Where are we? Were we attacked?"* he asked, gripped by something like a migraine, drenched in sweat at dusk and with his hands shaking so badly that he could not close his flask. He put it down to tiredness. At the Golden Camel, a three-storey building, the innkeeper **Omar Bin Rashid**, a thin, restless man over fifty who runs the place alone, welcomed Ezariel with salep, the drink that keeps desert travellers cool. She booked rooms for the night for 4 silver pieces and ordered everything on the menu (salted meat, pastourma, dried fruit, salads, boiled vegetables and camel milk), and told him the rest of the party would follow, giving only Elizium's name (he does not remember his surname) and, for herself, only her surname, McAlister.

From the Garrison, Selina and Azhyra went to the seat of the **Golden Sand Branch**, the [[Order of Freedom]]'s Branch in Saiyaddar, to report the end of their mission. In the hall were **Naira the Silent**, a local half-elf taking notes left-handed, and **Iben Farouk**, sharpening his weapons; both knew the two women, and Iben was amazed to see them alive. The Branch Master, **Sarena Al'Nasim**, received Selina first and Azhyra after her. They told her everything that had happened and asked her to send word at once to Zelfar and the Order's headquarters about what they had found. The revelations shocked her; Azhyra also told her that a shadow dragon had appeared in a dwarf colony. Azhyra asked to speak with her own superior, [[Loroves]], but no one there knew who that was, which left Selina with questions. Sarena took them down to the cellar, where the Branch keeps a sending circle. While Sarena was in the circle, Azhyra told Selina that she would stay in Saiyaddar instead of going with her to the Castle-State; Selina, upset and afraid of failing, was told to go to the Order and speak with Lavern, leaving at first light, and asked Azhyra to look after the others. Confused, the Branch Master sent the message, and Azhyra destroyed the written reports, hers and Selina's, saying it was too dangerous for copies of what they had discovered to exist; when Sarena protested that they had to be kept, Azhyra told her to write a short summary herself and that she would take the blame if need be.

> [!info] What Lilith overheard (Lilith's player)
> Listening from the counter, Lilith caught parts of the meeting before the door closed:
> - the orders from High Lady [[Lady Lavern|Lavern]] to go to the Castle-State; Sarena asked whether they should worry, Selina said they did not know, and Azhyra asked whether headquarters had sent any word;
> - Sarena said the Order's communications were busy, with matters under the code name **Reptile** being looked into, and not only those;
> - most serious of all, there were reports of **Shar** appearing;
> - when they told her what they had found, Sarena asked if they were joking: *"The shadow swords were destroyed."* *"Obviously not,"* Azhyra answered, and spoke of the temple of Shar destroyed, the holy sword defeated, "the six" broken, and a shadow dragon seen in a dwarf colony.
>
> These are the events of *Old Shadows* ([[Session 030 - Shar is Dead!|OS · Session 030 - Shar is Dead!]], [[Session 031 - The Fall of the Dragon!|OS · Session 031 - The Fall of the Dragon!]]). The shadow swords are the six Shadow Swords of Garagos (see [[Sword of Garagos]]); one of them, the Shadow Longsword, was given to Kor Bloodaxe in [[Session 002 - The Mausoleum of Brook Hollow]].

At the Branch were two travellers. One was a human with light brown hair and a beard, charismatic in his movements, and eyes of two colours, the right green and the left copper: [[Garrick|Garrick Coppervain]]. With him was a tiefling, [[Lilith|Lilith Gravesend]], whose skin looked human rather than any of the usual tiefling colours, with long brown hair, golden-green eyes and a finely carved bow, and her blink dog, **Muffin**, coloured like her and giving off wisps of vapour. The two had been travelling together for about a year. Azhyra, intrigued to see a tiefling, asked about them, and Garrick and Lilith in turn wondered who the women were; they had noticed the symbol on Selina's shield. Garrick told Iben that they were looking for a relic and had come from the **Cold Wood**, and that his own village is **Tulrun**, while Lilith only smiled ironically. Azhyra went to meet them just as Edric arrived at the Branch, equally curious about the pair. The travellers explained that they were looking for a powerful relic that would help them free their village from the vampires who had enslaved it, and that they had suffered much in searching for it; Iben, listening, sharpened his swords more loudly than he needed to. Azhyra and Edric agreed at once to help and gathered information at the Branch, and Azhyra asked Sarena to see the travellers first the next day. The relic was the **Vampire Killer**, a weapon Edric knew: his hero Galag had brought it to their city about nine years ago, and the Branch protects it. It was said to lie in the city's old cemetery, the one with the enormous palm tree, guarded by a spirit. Sarena asked Azhyra and Edric to look into it; Edric gave his own report, far too brief, and she did not press him. The travellers thanked Azhyra and Edric and went to the inn, Muffin following Lilith without a word from her, and on the way Lilith told Garrick everything she had overheard. Garrick thought Azhyra and Selina could help them; Lilith, wary, agreed to talk to them later.

> [!info] The Vampire Killer
> According to Sarena, the **Vampire Killer** was made by the weaponsmith and inventor **Rinal Kant Olfi**. Hiding it was Galag's second-to-last mission: a lost weapon that the Brotherhood of Light asked him to keep hidden, as a safeguard against the armies of **Carmilla**, one of the Lords of the Shadows of *The Dragonslayers Saga*. Legend says that **Lord Edgnar** asked Galag to hide and secure it; Galag took it to his own city, Saiyaddar, and hid it in the **Palm Cemetery** under the protection of Mielikki, the city's goddess, and **Nimue** sent a spirit of nature to guard it.

Meanwhile Ezariel, in a rare moment of trust, asked Elizium for help. In their darkened room, curtains drawn, she showed him a rune-carved box holding a vessel of red fluid that she called "the dragon egg". Elizium opened it with two movements, and a smell of sulphur filled the room: the fluid was fiendish blood, a devil's blood to be exact, shot through with golden threads. *"This is not a good thing,"* he told her: it could be used to poison people, as a catalyst to desecrate holy places, or to kill large stretches of natural life. He asked her for a single drop on a cloth, sent her out of the room for her own safety and set up his alchemist's bench for a test of a quarter of an hour, to see which of the Nine Hells it came from. When he called her back he threw the burning cloth to the floor: it was pure blood of a fiend, and in fact of the Lord of the Nine Hells himself, **Asmodeus**. That should be impossible, he told her, since Asmodeus has been dead for about ten years, although he could not say how he knew this, as his own memories go back only a year. When she pressed him he clutched his bag of holding, trembling: *"I must have read it somewhere,"* and then, *"I don't know!"* Ezariel admitted that the vessel was what the men in red cloaks had been after in the previous town, and that the Illumian had also seen it and knew what it was; Elizium examined the box and concluded that the Illumians had disabled the tracking spell on it. He told her that the next day he would leave with Selina for the [[Brotherhood of Light]], which seemed to upset her, and gave her a strange gold coin, finely minted and badly worn, from none of the known cities; he had it on him when he woke, and she would have sworn she had seen it before, in a book or a history lesson. He asked her to guard it with her life because it mattered greatly to him. Ezariel told him she had a mission, through which she had found the egg, and that her time was running short; she asked him to trust no one, except perhaps Edric, and to research the "dragon egg" secretly while he was there and read everything he could find about the city of **Silverymoon**, from its founding to the present day. She admitted that she considered him a dear friend; he asked her to come with them, but she refused: *"As long as I am with you, you are in danger!"* They swore to keep each other's secrets and find out what was happening.

Edric took Azhyra to dinner at his family's home as a thank-you, while Selina stayed at the inn. His father, a man of about forty-five, was feeding his animals when they arrived, while passers-by congratulated him on his son's return; he knocked on the window to call his wife. Azhyra did not want them to touch her, and Edric explained that it is not done where she comes from, so they did not. The evening passed quietly, and his reunion with his parents, **Aldric Hale** and **Nadia**, was moving; their faces filled with pride as they heard of their son's deeds, and when they learned that Edric would be proposed for knighthood his mother cried. Edric showed his father how Trusty had grown and his new sword, Thelisis, "a new member of the family". His father found Thelisis too perfect, light and well balanced: *"Nothing is ever perfect. Perhaps it is easier to do harm with it."* When Azhyra asked whether it could be evil, he answered that a tool is never evil, only the hand that uses it. Edric stayed the night with his family, and Azhyra went back to the inn with a gift of dates for the party.

Back at the Golden Camel, around nine in the evening and crowded with travellers, Selina greeted Garrick and Lilith with a slight bow; Garrick returned it, Lilith did not. Elizium, a high elf mage in armour, looked at Lilith, said *"Interesting"*, and admitted he had never seen a tiefling in whom the human nature prevails; *"Maybe only on the outside,"* she answered sourly. He asked where she came from (Luskan, in the West, she said) and about her parents, which annoyed her, while Selina tried to excuse him. Azhyra joined them, and Lilith sent Muffin to sniff her: he licked her, and she fed him salted meat from the table. Ezariel came down too, a half-elf with much of her face hidden behind a mask; her instincts told her nothing about the newcomers, only a strange feeling about Garrick, the same one Azhyra had had when she met him. Garrick told them they were looking for the weapon called the Vampire Killer, though they had no idea what it looked like, and Selina, warmer than usual, said she would have helped them herself but had to leave; she would speak to the Order for them, and they could turn to Edric for anything they needed. Elizium soon left, feeling unwell: it was hard for him to keep such an important secret from his friends, since Ezariel had asked him to tell them nothing of what they had found. A little later, after yet another quarrel with Azhyra (*"Listen to your mum, you brat"*), Ezariel went upstairs and spent the night with Elizium, who looked exhausted. In the room she shared with Selina, Azhyra explained why she wanted to stay, asked her to keep an eye on Elizium, and to ask Lavern to let Loroves know that she would wait for word.

At dawn Selina prayed. Elizium had not lain down to sleep: when Ezariel came out of her trance she found him standing silently at the window, looking out. He went to find Selina, and he and Azhyra had one last smoke together. After breakfast together, the whole group visited Edric's family home, bringing a small gift. The house is decorated with swords: Edric's father served in the Guard and never wanted a rank, preferring to be out helping people. Galag had been Edric's idol, still a trainee when Edric was a small boy. Edric and his parents told them that for the last two years there had been unrest and hostile creatures on the move, as if something was drawing them, though no one knew why. Elizium stayed silent, unlike himself, following Selina's lead in everything.

At the Branch, five minutes away, the whole Branch was gathered: Sarena, a veteran ranger of the Anauroch; Naira; Iben, a former caravan mercenary; **Voran Ironborn**, a dwarf with a beard down to his belly, in vestments, with an axe, a hammer and the symbol of Moradin, the Branch's healer and blacksmith; and **Elis Astrammos**, a half-elf with completely white skin, a wizard who studies the Netherese ruins. Selina told them that Garrick and Lilith knew of the relic and proposed that the Branch help them, and Edric and Azhyra agreed. Sarena said that the Branch's own task was to protect the relic, but that if the city agreed, two of their members could go with it, and that she would speak with the caliph; the Branch stood with them because Selina and Azhyra took responsibility for protecting the Vampire Killer and bringing it back. Selina then gave Edric a letter of recommendation she had written, proposing to the Brotherhood that he join as a Soldier of the Light, the first rank on the path of knighthood that leads to Knight of the Brotherhood of Light. She asked the others to join her and Elizium there once they had finished what they had to do. After a tearful farewell, in which she thanked each of them in turn, she and Elizium left; Edric and Azhyra bowed deeply, and Elizium answered with a bow so graceful that it surprised Ezariel. The remaining three, Azhyra, Edric and Ezariel, stayed with Garrick and Lilith to get permission to use the weapon. The Branch Master said she would settle the matter with the authorities herself, and a little later Iben brought word that they could use the Vampire Killer as long as it did not fall into the wrong hands, with a thinly veiled threat of what would happen otherwise, and a warning that the road, a day's journey, could be dangerous. At **Sun Lake**, the city's central market, Ezariel bought a silver arrow carved with runes that, the seller said, never misses but can be used only once. Then the new group set out by cart, with Edric as their guide, to find the relic.

On the road to the **Palm Cemetery** a desert troll tried to ambush them. Muffin growled, and when Lilith let him go he blinked away for the first time in front of the party, to a dune about thirty feet ahead; the dune, about ninety feet from the cart, looked freshly dug, as if something large lay hidden beneath it, and when Garrick threw a stone at it a terrible stench rose and a one-eyed troll stood up out of the sand. With the help of their two new companions they escaped the ambush and brought the creature down. Garrick revealed himself as a Copper Dragon Shaman, and Lilith as an excellent ranger. Lilith tended Garrick's wounds and then Edric's, while Ezariel and Garrick dug up what the troll had buried. As night fell a mist began to rise from the great palm, and the gate of the cemetery came into sight.

## Places
- **Saiyaddar**: the oasis city, Edric's birthplace, also called **Anauroch's Emerald**; about a thousand people of many peoples. Its sights, as a local described them:
  - **Sun Lake**: the heart of the city and its central market
  - **The Hero's Island**: where the statue of Galag stands, a knight of the Brotherhood of Light; some in the city say he rode with the [[Fate-Touched|Dragonslayers]]
  - **The Court of Five Wells**: the city hall, home of the caliph **Terin Alias**
  - **The Stone Terraces**: built on the ruins of an ancient empire, Netheril
  - **The House of Stars**: something like a planetarium, where the scholar lives
  - **The Freedom Hostel**: the seat of the Golden Sand Branch
- **The Golden Sand Branch**: the [[Order of Freedom]]'s Branch based in Saiyaddar, with a sending circle in its cellar
- **The Golden Camel**: the party's inn in Saiyaddar, a three-storey building about fifteen minutes' walk from the market
- **Edric's family home**, decorated with swords
- **The Palm Cemetery**: the city's old cemetery, a day's journey away and no longer used since before Galag's time, named after a palm tree three times the usual size. The dead are not buried there: their ashes are scattered, and instead of gravestones there are monuments bearing the names of those who rest there. It lies in thick mist, and Galag told Edric never to go near it, as it is dangerous. The Vampire Killer is said to be hidden there, guarded by a spirit.

## Fights
- A **desert troll** ambushed the group on the road to the Palm Cemetery; with Garrick's and Lilith's help it was brought down.
  - Edric took a fully defensive stance; the troll leapt at him, its claws sparking off his shield, and swore in Giant. Garrick raised his aura, a pulsing green-yellow energy spreading over Edric, Azhyra and Ezariel, and breathed a line of acid that left the troll reeling. Muffin blinked behind it, Lilith's arrows wrapped in spectral thorny vines, Azhyra's red energy struck its chest (*"Kneel, stupid creature,"* in Infernal) and Ezariel rolled in under it with her short sword.
  - The troll singled out Garrick for his acid, catching his shoulder, then Edric, tearing his shoulder open. Edric cut off its wrist, which it pushed back into the sand and reattached; Garrick shifted his aura into a field dripping acid and struck it in the eye with his morningstar.
  - Azhyra's ray hit its jaw and Ezariel cut both its tendons; it fell against a rock and broke its neck. While it tried to recover, Edric told Garrick to burn it, and his acid melted it away.
