# Session 011 - Blood Ties

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Συνεδρία 011 - Δεσμοί Αίματος]]

## Recap
The party reached **Saiyaddar**, [[Edric]]'s oasis city, escorted by Edric's three childhood friends **Ferith**, **Scioh** and **Wolly**. The city is built around its oasis, with a giant statue in the middle: a monument to the city's hero, **Galag**, Edric's mentor, who vanished some years ago. The people welcomed Edric, [[Selina]] and [[Azhyra]] with honours, as heroes.

The group split up for a while. Selina and Azhyra went straight to the city's Garrison headquarters to announce the party's return and report that Edric had completed his mission. Edric set off there too with his childhood friends, but the townsfolk kept stopping him to welcome him home. [[Elizium (A New Beginning)|Elizium]] and [[Ezariel]] left the carriage and booked rooms at the **Golden Camel** inn. Edric reported to the Garrison Commander, who released him from duty so he could visit his parents.

From the Garrison, Selina and Azhyra went to the seat of the **Golden Sand Branch**, the [[Order of Freedom]]'s Branch in Saiyaddar, to report the end of their mission. They told the Branch Master, **Sarena Al'Nasim**, everything that had happened and asked her to send word at once to Zelfar and the Order's headquarters about what they had found. The revelations shocked her; she asked whether they had met a shadow dragon, since one had been reported elsewhere, but they knew nothing of it. Azhyra asked to speak with her own superior, [[Loroves]], but no one there knew who that was, which left Selina with questions. Confused, the Branch Master sent the message, and Azhyra destroyed the written reports, hers and Selina's, saying it was too dangerous for copies of what they had discovered to exist.

At the Branch were two travellers. One was a human with brown hair and a beard and eyes of two colours, one green and one copper: [[Garrick|Garrick Coppervain]]. With him was a tiefling, [[Lilith|Lilith Gravesend]], whose skin looked human rather than any of the usual tiefling colours, and her blink dog, **Muffin**. Azhyra went to meet them just as Edric arrived at the Branch, equally curious about the pair. The travellers explained that they were looking for a powerful relic that would help them free their village from the vampires who had enslaved it. Azhyra and Edric agreed at once to help and gathered information at the Branch. The relic was the **Vampire Killer**, a weapon Edric knew: his hero Galag had brought it to their city about nine years ago. It was said to lie in the city's old cemetery, the one with the enormous palm tree, guarded by a spirit. The travellers thanked Azhyra and Edric and went to the inn.

Meanwhile Ezariel, in a rare moment of trust, asked Elizium for help. She showed him a box holding a vessel of red fluid that she called "the dragon egg". Elizium studied it and found that the fluid was fiendish blood: pure blood of a fiend, and in fact of the Lord of the Nine Hells himself, **Asmodeus**. He explained that it could be used to poison people or kill large stretches of natural life. That should be impossible, he told her, since Asmodeus has been dead for about ten years, although he could not say how he knew this, as his own memories go back only a year. Ezariel admitted that the vessel was what the men in red cloaks had been after in the previous town, and that the Illumian had also seen it and knew what it was. When she asked him to "take the secret to his grave", the words woke a strange feeling in Elizium: he saw himself dying, wounded all over and with a shattered ankle, in a frozen place high in the mountains. He told her that the next day he would leave with Selina for the [[Brotherhood of Light]], which seemed to upset her, and gave her a strange gold coin that she remembered from history books, asking her to guard it with her life because it mattered greatly to him. Ezariel asked him to research the "dragon egg" while he was there, and anything he could find about the city of **Silverymoon**. She admitted that she considered him a dear friend; he asked her to come with them, but she refused. They swore to keep each other's secrets and find out what was happening.

Edric invited Azhyra and Selina to dinner at his family's home as a thank-you. The evening passed quietly, and his reunion with his parents, **Aldric Hale** and Edric's mother, was moving; their faces filled with pride as Azhyra and Selina told them of their son's deeds.

Back at the Golden Camel the party met Ezariel, Elizium and, for the second time that day, Garrick and Lilith, and they all sat together. Elizium soon left, feeling unwell: it was hard for him to keep such an important secret from his friends, since Ezariel had asked him to tell them nothing of what they had found. A little later, after yet another quarrel with Azhyra, Ezariel went upstairs and spent the night with Elizium, who looked exhausted.

The next morning Selina announced that she and Elizium would leave at once for the Brotherhood of Light, and asked the others to join them there once they had finished what they had to do. She gave Edric a letter of recommendation she had written, proposing to the Brotherhood that he join as a Soldier of the Light. After a tearful farewell, the remaining three, Azhyra, Edric and Ezariel, met Garrick and Lilith and went back to the Branch for permission to use the weapon. The Branch Master said she would settle the matter with the authorities herself, and the new group set out, with Edric as their guide, to find the relic.

On the road to the **Palm Cemetery** a desert troll tried to ambush them. With the help of their two new companions they escaped the ambush and brought the creature down. Garrick revealed himself as a Copper Dragon Shaman, and Lilith as an excellent ranger.

**Key beats**
1. The party reaches Saiyaddar with Ferith, Scioh and Wolly; Edric, Selina and Azhyra are welcomed as heroes
2. Selina and Azhyra report at the Garrison, then to Branch Master Sarena Al'Nasim at the Golden Sand Branch; Azhyra destroys the written reports
3. Edric is released from duty to visit his parents
4. Ezariel shows Elizium the "dragon egg": the blood of Asmodeus, who should have been dead for ten years

6. Elizium gives Ezariel a strange gold coin to guard with her life; they swear to keep each other's secrets
