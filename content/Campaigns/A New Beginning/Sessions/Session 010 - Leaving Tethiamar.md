# Session 010 - Leaving Tethiamar

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Συνεδρία 010 - Αποχώρηση από την Τέθιαμαρ]]

## Recap
The man in the feathered red hat, [[Zacharious]], gave [[Ezariel]] a message from someone he claimed they both knew, while four of his associates headed for the rooms of [[Elizium (A New Beginning)|Elizium]] and [[Azhyra]] at [[The Flower and the Tiara]]. The two were resting when strangers knocked, claiming to bring fresh towels from the inn. Elizium noticed he already had clean towels. No one entered the rooms.

[[Zarik Turbar]] had warned [[Edric]] and [[Selina]] that Ezariel was in danger. Edric rushed, but Selina told him to be cautious. They reached the inn just as Zacharious and his four men were leaving, and the two groups met at the door. Edric tried to talk to him. *"Hello, I am Edric!"* *"Zacharious, my young man."* Selina pulled Edric away from the man's studying gaze. Then Zarik came in, and Zacharious put his hand over the boy's head; it looked like a threat. For a moment it was tense, but no fight broke out.

The party pressed Ezariel to tell them who these men were and whether they were in danger. She told them the people after her are very dangerous and that even knowing about them could put them in trouble, and would say no more. Edric tried to open her up, promising to protect her, and when she dismissed him he got up to talk to the innkeeper; she took his bag, gave it to him and brought him back to the table. Then she said something like "I do not follow orders like Edric does from Selina". Edric asked Selina, as formally as he could, to be dismissed for the night so he could rest. Selina told him she would not order him to stay, but that she wanted him to stay as a friend. He was not moved and went to his room. Azhyra told Ezariel she needed to know which organization was after them: the Zhentarim, someone else? She belonged to an organization herself, and maybe they could help. Ezariel would not be moved, and Azhyra left too, saying it wasn't worth it. Selina told Ezariel she would not force her to come with them, but that she saw her as a friend who needed help and was willing to give it.

Azhyra went to Edric's room, told him he was a valued member of the group and should try again with Ezariel, and be himself more often than a soldier. He showed her the new magic in **Trusty**; she showed him her rapier, which he named **Grace**. That night Ezariel did not go back to her room. She slept in the main area of the inn and, without telling anyone, watched over the party in case the hunters returned. Zarik spoke with her there. He told her that those people were her friends, since they are always together, that she is a nice person who doesn't deserve to be alone, and that friends are how people get as far as a mountain top. She was moved, and admitted that Zarik was indeed her friend. Later she talked his father into doing his best so that Zarik could learn to read and write.

In the morning the party prepared for the road. Elizium tried to get an elegant new scabbard for Trusty to lighten Edric's mood, and Ezariel went alone to a shop and bought potions against poison and antitoxins. Back at the inn, Zarik was being scolded by his father for doing reckless things, but as soon as he saw Ezariel he showed her the berries he had picked at the top of a mountain, where his friend had helped him climb, and introduced her to that friend.

The party left for **Atar** by carriage, with camels, along the **Caravan Road** through the Anauroch desert, with a stop at Edric's city, Saiyaddar, on the way. Edric drove, since he knew the way, and easily found shelter from the sandstorms. Ezariel sat beside him and tried to apologise in a very reserved way: *"There are extremely rare occasions that I may be slightly, just slightly, not entirely on the right."* He told her he was confused. He is always asked to be himself and to take initiative, yet he is still treated as a soldier; they did not seem to care when he was poisoned, he is not taken seriously when he says he can help, and even Ezariel was not on this journey because of him. She said she does not like words like duty and responsibility. He answered that what had convinced her to come was authority, not friendship. Ezariel stayed silent.

At a desert shrine of **Mielikki**, the **Shrine of Safe Travels**, Edric offered a potion as a tribute. The goddess blessed them for the rest of the journey and they gained a great deal of ground.

A day before reaching Saiyaddar they heard a flute and found a cave, and Ezariel and Elizium followed Edric in, to support his initiative. Inside was the satyr [[Redwood Oakblossom]], who takes pleasure in enchanting unsuspecting passers-by and making them hurt one another. He had enchanted **Ferith**, **Scioh** and **Wolly**, three siblings of the Saiyaddar Guard and Edric's childhood friends, who had heard his music on border patrol, and was forcing them to dance with their swords to his tune, wounding each other. The fight was very hard and Edric and Elizium fell under the spell of the flute, but Ezariel resisted. With Selina's *Silence* and Elizium's *Pyrotechnics* the satyr was left helpless and exposed, and Ezariel finished him with a coup de grâce. The three were saved, and they all went on together towards Edric's city.

**Key beats**
1. Zacharious brings Ezariel a message; his men knock on Elizium's and Azhyra's doors ("Clean towels!")
2. Edric and Selina meet Zacharious at the inn's door; his hand over Zarik's head looks like a threat
3. Ezariel will not say who is after her; Edric, then Azhyra, leave the table upset
4. Selina tells both Edric and Ezariel she wants them around as friends
5. Azhyra talks to Edric, and he names her rapier Grace
6. Ezariel keeps a secret watch; Zarik talks to her about friendship and she admits he is her friend
7. The party leaves Tethiamar by camel carriage along the Caravan Road; Edric and Ezariel have it out on the road
8. Edric's offering at the Shrine of Safe Travels earns Mielikki's blessing
9. The satyr Redwood Oakblossom is defeated with *Silence*, *Pyrotechnics* and Ezariel's coup de grâce; Ferith, Scioh and Wolly are saved

> [!warning] Conflicting accounts
> - **The scabbard:** the DM says Elizium ordered an elegant scabbard for Trusty; Edric's and Ezariel's players say he tried, but it did not lift Edric's mood (Edric's player: "he would not").
> - **Who enchanted whom:** Edric's player says "they defeated him and saved them"; the DM's and Ezariel's player's fuller version is used.
> - **Names:** Ezariel's player and Edric's player write Zacharius (also Zaccharius), Wally and Saioch or Scioch. The DM's Zacharious, Wolly and Scioh are used.

## NPCs met
- [[Zacharious]]: the man with the red feathered hat, with four associates in red hoods; brought Ezariel a message from someone they both know
- [[Zarik Turbar]] and his father, **Ian Turbar**
- [[Redwood Oakblossom]]: a cruel satyr living in a hidden cave; he lures travellers with his magic flute and makes them hurt each other; killed
- **Ferith**, **Scioh** and **Wolly**: three siblings of the Saiyaddar Guard, Edric's childhood friends and colleagues; saved from the satyr

## Places
- [[The Flower and the Tiara]], in Tethiamar

- **The Shrine of Safe Travels**: dedicated to Mielikki, goddess of journeys and protector of the countryside
- **Redwood Oakblossom's lair**: a hidden cave, a day from Saiyaddar
- *On the way to:* Saiyaddar, then the oasis city of Atar

## Fights
- [[Redwood Oakblossom]] in his cave: a very hard victory. Selina's *Silence* and Elizium's *Pyrotechnics* left the satyr helpless and exposed, and Ezariel finished him with a coup de grâce.
