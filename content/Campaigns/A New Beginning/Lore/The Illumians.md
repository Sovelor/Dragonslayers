# The Illumians

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Οι Ιλλούμιαν]]

**Role:** Bald, knowledge-gathering people who hired the party

## Summary
A strange race of bald humans who seem to worship knowledge and gather all of it that exists. They claim to have been cleansed of distractions such as feelings. Letters float around their heads and change colour with their mood, and their inn room holds an unbelievable number of scrolls and books in an unknown language. Their race is very peaceful. When three of them (and a search party) went missing, they paid the party up front to investigate the ruins of the Life-giving Prince and promised an urgent letter to the [[Brotherhood of Light]]. [[Vaalrath]] of *Ancient Blood* is also an Illumian.
