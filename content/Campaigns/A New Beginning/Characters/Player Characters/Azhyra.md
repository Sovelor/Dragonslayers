# Azhyra Vorneth

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Αζίρα]]

![[Characters - Azhyra.jpg|300]]

**Role:** Party member in *A New Beginning* · of the Brotherhood of Light (she/her) · plans to run a tobacco business with [[Elizium (A New Beginning)|Elizium]] in the future
**Facts:** **Race:** Tiefling · **Class:** Warlock (level 4) · **Pronouns:** she/her · **Age:** 30 · **Alignment:** neutral · **Languages:** Common, Infernal

## Summary
A tiefling warlock devoted to serving her patron.

Left the Brotherhood of Light with [[Selina]] for Brook Hollow as her supervisor on Selina's first mission. Her line in Session 1 was *"I don't want you touching me."* In Session 9 she and Elizium talked about their planned tobacco business on the road, and at the abandoned roadside inn she declared the place hers and terrified the looters (*"Are you robbing my inn?"*). Later she saw something she could not possibly have seen, and her mind erased the image at once.

## Appearance
Long black hair, red eyes and grey skin. She wears a black corset and a long black skirt slit at the legs, tall black boots and a long black hooded cloak. A belt at her waist holds her ornate rapier, a small knife is hidden in one of her boots, and she carries the Staff of Alertness.

Her voice is calm and sensual. She draws almost every gaze wherever she is.

## Personality
Very self-confident and quite secretive; she always watches and analyses the people around her. She does not like to be touched, or to touch anyone. Her only bond is with her patron.

## Background
> [!info] Rumours and open mysteries
> People mistake her for someone else. Others think she is full of herself.

## Goals & fears
**Goal:** to serve her patron well.
**Fear:** death.

## Relationships
She does not form relationships easily: to her everyone is expendable, and very few are worth it. Her allies are [[Selina]], [[Edric]], [[Elizium (A New Beginning)|Elizium]] and [[Ezariel]].

## Affiliations
- Member of the [[Edge of Banishment]], the secret elite group of the [[Order of Freedom]]
