# Ezariel McAlister

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Εζάριελ]]

![[Characters - Ezariel.jpg|300]]

**Role:** Party member in *A New Beginning* · half-elf rogue (she/her) · ginger hair, a silver mask over most of her face
**Facts:** **Race:** Half-elf (high elf) · **Class:** Rogue (level 4) · **Pronouns:** she/her · **Age:** 23 · **Alignment:** chaotic good · **Home:** Waterdeep · **Languages:** Common, Elvish, Undercommon, Dwarven, Giant

## Summary
A half-elf (high elf) rogue from Waterdeep, on a mission to find a missing woman.

Found on the road in Session 1, hands bound and chased by strange zombies; she was the first to search the corpses for her belongings, and the party agreed to stick together afterwards. In Session 5 she came face to face with an ogre's private parts when both peeked around the same corner. In Session 9 she secretly asked an Illumian for a favour. Young Zarik Turbar let slip that old friends were looking for her (*"They are not my friends... I DON'T have friends!"*), and at the end of the session a man in a red cape and feathered red hat sat at her table and took her hand: *"There's no need to make a scene."*

## Appearance
Medium-sized, 168 cm tall, with fair skin and freckles, ginger hair in a long bob cut with soft waves, and striking bluish-green eyes like a crystal lake. The most distinctive thing about her is an intricate, well-crafted silver mask that covers most of her face, leaving only her mouth and part of her cheek visible.

She dresses like a rogue, well fitted and lightweight: black studded leather armour with runes carved on it, black leather pants, a soft off-white shirt with puffy sleeves beneath the armour, black leather boots with intricate details and leather bracers over her forearms. A pale lilac cloak reaches just beneath her knees and covers her head well. She wields a masterwork short sword as her main weapon, multiple daggers, a masterwork short bow and a masterwork runic rapier.

Her voice is melodious and velvety, but it usually carries a sarcastic, annoyed or disappointed undertone.

## Personality
Very secretive; she doesn't trust easily and quickly judges people. Her defence walls are so high that it is hard for her to open up to anyone, even someone who tries to help. She is usually on high alert, which makes her responses sharp, abrasive and cold, and she only smiles when making a sarcastic comment.

She makes minimal movements and prefers to observe and assess a situation from a safe distance before acting. Her judgmental expression can be read even behind the mask. She stands with her arms crossed over her chest and taps her foot when something irritates her; the biggest tell is an exaggerated eyeroll, even in the middle of a fight, followed by a "tsk". She hates resins, and being addressed in the plural.

## Background
She grew up with her parents, but their broken marriage made her a difficult, rebellious child. Her half-elven mother never showed her much love and rarely visited, even though they lived in the same city. Ezariel lived with her father, a very distant, cold and strict elf who, in his efforts to give her a good life, devoted himself to his work so much that she grew up alone or with caretakers. She never had a friend except one, whom she trusts with her life. She got into trouble many times as she grew up and messed with the wrong people more than once. In the end that friend gave her a mission, and it finally made her leave her city.

## Goals & fears
**Goal:** to complete her mission.
**Fears:** losing sight of who she is, and ending up alone.

## Relationships
