# Edric

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Έντρικ]]

![[Characters - Edric.png|300]]

**Role:** Party member in *A New Beginning* · human fighter (he/him) · from the Brotherhood of Light's desert department · recruit, being proposed for knighthood

**Race:** Human · **Class:** Fighter (level 4) · **Pronouns:** he/him · **Age:** 21 · **Alignment:** Lawful Good · **Home:** Sayadar, a town in the desert · **Languages:** Common, Dwarven

## Summary
A kind, timid and talkative young human fighter from **Sayadar**, a town in the desert, on his first mission with the Brotherhood of Light, sent to protect [[Selina]] and [[Azhyra]] on the road to Brook Hollow. He dreams of becoming the greatest longsword wielder. He carries **Trusty**, his longsword, and **Will**, the longsword of the Life-giving Prince ([[King Theron]]). In Session 9 the ettercaps' poison paralysed him for the first time in his life; that night, in his fever, he kept repeating the word *Merthouvial* ("Consecrator" in Celestial) and remembered nothing in the morning. After an eight-hour ritual at the temple of Tethiamar, Trusty became a +1 longsword, strengthened with the power of a +1 bastard sword. Selina told him she will propose him for knighthood in the Brotherhood of Light. He has a signature move: he uses his longsword to trip opponents and attack them while they are down (the Improved Trip feat).

## Appearance
Tall and athletic but not bulky, with black hair and a small trimmed beard. He has an expressive, friendly face and warm, enthusiastic eyes. His readiness to smile is his most distinctive feature. He wears a chain shirt he calls **Roni** and carries a shield called **Steady** and the longswords Trusty and Will.

## Personality
Kind, timid, naive, talkative and enthusiastic, warm and eager, and easily embarrassed. He cares for people and things quickly and deeply, asks endless questions and loves a good story. He talks a lot when he is excited, and he is a terrible liar who confesses almost at once. He gives names to his equipment because he believes people take better care of things they know; he cleans and sharpens Trusty every night and talks to him like a trusted companion.
- **Ideal:** protecting others is a duty
- **Bond:** **Galag**, his mentor, who gave him Steady
- **Bond:** Trusty, his father's sword, and the people who believed in him
- **Bond:** his growing feelings for [[Ezariel]]
- **Flaw:** acts before he plans, as in the well rescue
- **Flaw:** naive, and too ready to trust

## Background
Edric grew up in **Sayadar**, a town in the desert, and was always curious about what lay beyond it. As a child he wandered too far into the desert to see a distant rock formation and was found sunburned and hungry, still excited about the strange creatures he had seen. His father gave him his first real longsword, Trusty, once he grew strong enough to lift it. Edric once jumped into a well on a rope to save a child before anyone had a plan.

His mentor was a knight named **Galag**, confident and cocky, who taught him that protecting people is a duty and gave him the shield Steady. As far as Edric knows, Galag left on an important mission and never came back. Galag wanted Edric to become a knight, but Edric wants to be the greatest longsword wielder. An elderly widow, **Mira**, gave him a whetstone before he left, and he still sharpens Trusty with it. Edric joined the Brotherhood of Light and was sent to protect Selina and Azhyra on the road to Brook Hollow.

> - What does *Merthouvial* ("Consecrator" in Celestial) mean, and why did Edric keep saying it in his fever?
> - What is a hero? King Theron searched for the answer, and Edric saw the question in the ruins.

## Goals & fears
**Goals:** to become the greatest longsword wielder; to live up to Galag's lesson that a hero protects people; to earn the knighthood Selina wants to propose for him, while staying himself.

**Fears:** helplessness (in Session 9 the ettercaps' poison paralysed him and he panicked), failing the people he is supposed to protect, and the expectations of his father, Galag and the villagers.
