# Edric

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Έντρικ]]

**Role:** Party member in *A New Beginning* · human fighter (he/him) · from the Brotherhood of Light's desert department

## Summary
A fighter on his first mission, sent to protect [[Selina]] and [[Azhyra]] on the road to Brook Hollow. He carries **Trusty**, his longsword, and **Will**, the longsword of the Life-giving Prince ([[King Theron]]). In Session 9 the ettercaps' poison paralysed him for the first time in his life; that night, in his fever, he kept repeating the word *Merthouvial* ("Consecrator" in Celestial) and remembered nothing in the morning. After an eight-hour ritual at the temple of Tethiamar, Trusty became a +1 longsword, strengthened with the power of a +1 bastard sword. Selina told him she will propose him for knighthood in the Brotherhood of Light.
