# Lilith

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Λίλιθ]]

**Role:** Tiefling ranger, Garrick's travelling companion; joins the party in Saiyaddar

## Summary
A tiefling ranger whose skin looks human rather than any of the usual tiefling colours, travelling with her friend [[Garrick]] and her blink dog companion. The two came to Saiyaddar looking for the Vampire Killer, a relic to free their village from vampires, and set out with [[Azhyra]], [[Edric]] and [[Ezariel]] to find it.

## Background
She has been travelling with [[Garrick]] for about a year. Asked by Elizium where she came from, she said **Luskan**, in the West. Her blink dog, **Muffin**, follows her without needing a command; he blinked for the first time in front of the party on the road to the Palm Cemetery. %% src:NB-S011b d2 %%
