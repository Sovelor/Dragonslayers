# Garrick

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Γκάρικ]]

**Role:** Human dragon shaman with the powers of the copper dragons; joins the party in Saiyaddar

## Summary
Garrick Coppervain is a human with brown hair and a beard and eyes of two colours, one green and one copper. With his companion [[Lilith]] he came to the Order of Freedom's Branch in Saiyaddar looking for a powerful relic, the Vampire Killer, to free their village from the vampires who enslaved it. He joined [[Azhyra]], [[Edric]] and [[Ezariel]] to find it, and on the road revealed himself as a Copper Dragon Shaman.

## Background
He has been travelling with [[Lilith]] for about a year. At the Golden Sand Branch he said that they came from the **Cold Wood** and that his own village is **Tulrun**. %% src:NB-S011b d1 %%
