# Kor Bloodaxe

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Κορ Μπλάνταξ]]

**Role:** Father Arias; servant of the Prophet, ruined Brook Hollow

## Summary
The young, charismatic cleric who arrived in [[Brook Hollow]] three months after Pastor Darion died and took his place as Father Arias. Within his first months people began to disappear while others were sent to dig a tunnel beneath the church; [[Lia]] suspected him from the start and was dismissed and demoted when she tried to warn the village. In "Kor's Lodging", a hidden room in the mausoleum, the party learned that his real name is Kor Bloodaxe: he had turned to the shadow and followed a mysterious woman, the Prophet ([[Hecate the Prophet]]), who promised him power and gave him a sword, the Shadow Longsword, one of the six Shadow Swords of Garagos (see [[Sword of Garagos]]). All he had to do was gather 50 souls in it as a sacrifice, and he took far more: the whole village, children included, was turned into zombies.

The DM's account (Session 2): Kor took the place of Pastor Darion after murdering him; the sword was brought to him a year earlier by Mallen the Black, the Prophet's envoy. The party found his corpse before the altar beneath the mausoleum, petrified in shadow: as he performed the ritual that covered everything in shadow, the shadow took him too. His corrupted soul, steeped in the Shadow, attacked as a wraith and was destroyed. His memoirs end: "Tomorrow I will touch immortality! O Lady of Shadows! Accept me as worthy of your Grace!" %% src:NB-S002b d8 %%

The party does not have the Shadow Longsword: it was not on the altar when they arrived, and its whereabouts are unknown. The party also does not know whether the Lady of Shadows he prays to is Shar, whom they know died ten years ago at the Battle of Destiny (DM).
