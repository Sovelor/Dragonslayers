# Kor Bloodaxe

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Κορ Μπλάνταξ]]

**Role:** Father Arias; servant of the Prophet, ruined Brook Hollow

## Summary
The young, charismatic cleric who arrived in [[Brook Hollow]] three months after High Priest Dalton died and took his place as Father Arias. Within his first months people began to disappear while others were sent to dig a tunnel beneath the church; [[Pastor Lia]] suspected him from the start and was dismissed and demoted when she tried to warn the village. In "Kor's Lodging", a hidden room in the mausoleum, the party learned that his real name is Kor Bloodaxe: he had turned to the shadow and followed a mysterious woman, the Prophet ([[Hecate the Prophet]]), who promised him power and gave him a sword. All he had to do was gather 50 souls in it as a sacrifice, and he took far more: the whole village, children included, was turned into zombies. His fate is unknown.
