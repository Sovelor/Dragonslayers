**Role:** Cruel satyr who lived in a hidden cave near Saiyaddar

## Summary
A cruel satyr who lured travellers to his cave with his magic flute, enchanted them and made them hurt one another. He enchanted three guards of Saiyaddar. The party defeated him; [[Ezariel]] finished him with a coup de grâce.

## Appearance
A satyr with curling horns, black curly hair and a goatee, with a goat's legs and hooves and tattoos on his arms. He wears a red cloak over a waistcoat, carries a curved sword at his belt and plays a set of panpipes, with a ruined castle town behind him.
