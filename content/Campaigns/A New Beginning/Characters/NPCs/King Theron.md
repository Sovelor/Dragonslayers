# King Theron

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Βασιλιάς Θέρον]]

**Role:** The Life-giving Prince · creator of Therosar · long dead, buried in the ruins that bear his name

## Summary
His tombstone in the ruins reads: *"Here lies King Theron. Creator of Therosar. He who is called the Life-giving Prince. Slayer of the usurper Dorl Taviani, the fake god. Fell by the hand of a beloved and trusted friend."* His longsword, **Will**, is now carried by [[Edric]]. After the party mentioned him, one of the Illumians seemed suspiciously changed: the Illumians seek the Prince's knowledge.
