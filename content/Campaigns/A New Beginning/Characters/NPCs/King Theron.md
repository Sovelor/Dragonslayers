# King Theron

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Βασιλιάς Θέρον]]

**Role:** The Life-giving Prince · creator of Therosar · long dead, buried in the ruins that bear his name

## Summary
His tombstone in the ruins reads: *"Here lies King Theron. Creator of Therosar. He who is called the Life-giving Prince. Slayer of the usurper Dorl Taviani, the fake god. Fell by the hand of a beloved and trusted friend."* His longsword, **Merthuvial** (the name written on it; [[Edric]] calls it Will), is now carried by Edric. After the party mentioned him, one of the Illumians seemed suspiciously changed: the Illumians seek the Prince's knowledge.

His killer was his friend, who loved the prince's wife. The prince's followers caught the traitor and cursed him to suffer forever; the party found him on a throne in the ruins and killed him. %% src:NB-S008 d9 %%
