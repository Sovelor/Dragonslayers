# Lia

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Λία]]

**Role:** Apprentice cleric of Torm in Brook Hollow · the reason for the party's mission in *A New Beginning*

## Summary
An apprentice cleric of Torm in [[Brook Hollow]]. After the village's High Priest, **Darion**, died in strange circumstances, she sent the [[Brotherhood of Light]] an urgent message asking for help (Session 1). An excitable cleric who often clashed with Pastor Darion. After Darion died and the young, charismatic Father Arias took his place, she suspected him of dark practices; when she tried to warn the village she was dismissed and demoted. She kept going down to the tunnel being dug under the church to help, and discovered the hideout in the Quickfeet Inn's basement. Her last note says Otis and Newton were in danger and that she had to convince them that day. The village's journal ends *"We didn't listen to Lia. She tried to warn us."*

In Session 2 the party found her in the Temple of Torm, turned into a zombie and chained to its desecrated altar, under a message written in blood: "The punishment for your blasphemy will be eternal. The Shadow has accepted you. You will serve it forever." Her cursed soul rose as a shadow and was set free when Elizium burned her body (DM). %% src:NB-S002b d6 %%
