# Pastor Lia

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Πάστορας Λία]]

**Role:** Cleric of Brook Hollow · the reason for the party's mission in *A New Beginning*

## Summary
An excitable cleric who often clashed with High Priest Dalton. After Dalton fell ill and died and the young, charismatic Father Arias took his place, she suspected him of dark practices; when she tried to warn the village she was dismissed and demoted. She kept going down to the tunnel being dug under the church to help, and discovered the hideout in the Swiftfoot Inn's basement. Her last note says Otis and Newton were in danger and that she had to convince them that day. Her fate is still unknown: the village's journal ends *"We didn't listen to Lia. She tried to warn us."*
