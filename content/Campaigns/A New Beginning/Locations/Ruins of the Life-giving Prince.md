# Ruins of the Life-giving Prince

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Ερείπια του Ζωοδόχου Πρίγκιπα]]

**Role:** Ruins and dungeon where King Theron is buried

## Summary
The ruins and dungeon of the Life-giving Prince, [[King Theron]], where the party was sent by the [[The Illumians|Illumians]]. Wolves and a dire wolf guard the entrance; in the first chamber the previous search team lay dead. Inside are the tomb of two fallen heroes with a spring of magical water that removes fatigue, a chamber with an ogre, a room of riddles whose door a beholder's puzzle opens, a chamber with a pool and mimics, and a statue of the Life-giving Prince with a secret compartment in its base. The king's tombstone reads that his "blade-sharp will is inherited to his heirs".
