# Brook Hollow

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Μπρουκ Χόλοου]]

**Role:** Village in the far east where every villager became a zombie

## Summary
A village in the far east, in the desert. [[Selina]] and [[Azhyra]] were sent here by [[Lady Lavern]] to find out what had happened to [[Pastor Lia]]. When the party arrived, every villager had been turned into a strange zombie, not the usual undead but something new and dark. The party found a hideout in the basement of the Swiftfoot Inn, a tunnel dug beneath the church and a hidden room in the mausoleum, "Kor's Lodging". [[Elizium (A New Beginning)|Elizium]] blew up the church.
