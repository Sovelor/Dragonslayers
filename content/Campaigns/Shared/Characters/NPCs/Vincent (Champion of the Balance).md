## Summary
Ten years after the saga, Vincent serves his wife, the goddess Allalme, as Her Champion and Harbinger of Her teachings. He followed her to the **Great Forests Plane**, the plane of existence she created during her Ascension. He appears as an NPC in *Old Shadows*: [[Aya]] received his sword and feels obligated to him, and [[Thoriel]], for whom he is the harbinger of a gift yet to be revealed, doesn't trust him.

## Relationships

- [[Thoriel]]: harbinger of a gift yet to be revealed; she doesn't trust him
