# Organizations

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Οργανώσεις]]

The great organizations of the world of **Threads of Fate**. They are not tied to one campaign: their members, allies and enemies can turn up in any of them.

- [[Circle of the Moon]]: the druidic circle of Allalme's faithful, guardians of the ten Sacred Willows.
- [[Order of Freedom]]: Mahaya Vishan's order, defending the Material Plane from extraplanar threats.
