# Circle of the Moon

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Ο Κύκλος της Σελήνης]]

> [!warning] Spoilers
> This page reveals the fall of the guardians of the Misty Forest and the death of one of them, and how the Forest Kingdom's war against the Dragonslayers ended.

**Role:** Druidic circle, the highest order of the faithful of the goddess [[Allalme Dragonborn|Allalme]] · Seat: the Moon Clearing · Leader: Archdruid Lenor Neritoris, the Black Tulip

## Summary
The Circle of the Moon (Ο Κύκλος της Σελήνης) is the highest order of the faithful of Allalme: a druidic circle of druids, monks, priests and wardens who protect Nature, the Balance and the forests through the Sacred Willows. Its mission is to keep the balance between the light and the shadow of the Moon.

## Hierarchy
1. The Archdruid
2. The Council, the Circle of the Lunar Druids
3. The Wardens of the Sacred Trees
4. The Monks of Balance
5. The Daughters of Allalme

## Symbols and rites
- **Holy symbol:** a silver crescent moon encircling a white willow.
- **The Full Moon Rite:** at every full moon the faithful gather around a Sacred Willow and sing hymns to Allalme.
- **Silver Water:** water gathered only under the light of the full moon, used in blessings, healing and purification.
- **The Dance of the Leaves:** a traditional rite in which the faithful move in a circle around a Sacred Willow, re-enacting the eternal cycles of nature.

## The central sanctuary
The Moon Clearing (Ξέφωτο της Σελήνης), at the foothills of the Griffon's Nest (Φωλιά του Γρύπα). The Archdruid **Lenor Neritoris, the Black Tulip**, lives in this sanctuary.

## The Sacred Willows
Allalme has planted ten Sacred Willows, each in a forest she chose and each guarded by its own Wardens.

### 1. High Forest (Υψηλό Δάσος)
The Circle is trying to restore the Tree of the Old Father by imbuing it with the Blessing of the Full Moon. The Tree of the Old Father was destroyed by [[Atropos]] herself, in an attempt to take over Allalme's body. Wishing to heal and restore the Moon Tree, Allalme planted the first Sacred Willow there. It is the only Sacred Willow with a single Warden, Councillor **Alerátha Thelyn**. The willow joined with the weakened Tree of the Old Father, and the two were reborn as the **Tree of the Old Mother**.

### 2. King's Forest (Δάσος του Βασιλιά)
This forest is home to the **Forest Kingdom**, a kingdom of sun elves who refused to leave the region of Cormyr. About ten years ago, under Queen **Nabeora Lithl'Orn** and the Council of the Chromatic Dragons (who posed as the Council of the Metallic Dragons), the kingdom allied with **Tiamat** and attacked the Tower of Freedom (see [[Order of Freedom]]). After the Dragonslayers defeated Nabeora and the Chromatic Dragons, her daughter **Aryalith Lithl'Orn** became Queen. Aryalith had fought at the Dragonslayers' side as a member of their party (a player character for some sessions of the original campaign, later an NPC because her player left the table). She has made an alliance with the Silver Hand and gladly welcomed Allalme's Sacred Willow into her forest. Its Wardens have not been revealed yet.

### 3. Misty Forest (Ομιχλώδες Δάσος)
This misty forest caught Allalme's attention because it was fighting an uneven battle against the corruption and acid menace of the moors and marshes: the High Moor (Μεγάλα Χερσοτόπια) are trying to wipe it out. Allalme planted her third Sacred Willow here; it revived the forest and halted the moors' corrupting influence.

Its two Wardens, **Belak** and his companion **Irewin**, turned their backs on Allalme and tainted the Sacred Willow with the **Blood of Asmodeus**, which they took from the Blood Seer (Μάντισσα του Αίματος). After Belak's fall, Irewin gave her life to drive the corruption of the Blood out of the willow, and she is working to restore it.

The Warden today is **Liga**, a half-orc barbarian (Bear Warrior), who helped a group of heroes and [[Melda]] (a druid of Allalme) heal the corruption. Liga became one of Allalme's faithful and received the gift of transforming into a bear. The second Warden is Irewin's spirit, living on through the trees of the forest: reborn as a treant, she stands beside Liga as guardian of the Sacred Willow.

### 4. Lurkwood (Δάσος της Ελλοχείας)
One of the first great forests Allalme blessed, and the fourth to receive a Sacred Willow. Its ancient paths had been erased by time, the forest spirits had withdrawn deep into the shadows, and the people around it had forgotten the ancient pact between mortals and nature. Seeing the roots of harmony weaken, Allalme chose it as a place of rebirth. Since then its willow has been a bridge between the natural world and the world of spirits; druids and priests travel there seeking visions, guidance and the wisdom of forgotten ages.

Wardens: **Arandor Shadowleaf**, a human ranger, with his faithful panther **Nightshade**, and **Elaisa Moonweaver**, a half-elf druid and one of the youngest Wardens Allalme has ever chosen.

### 5. Moonwood (Δάσος του Φεγγαριού)
Many hold it to be Allalme's holiest forest after the Moon Clearing. On full-moon nights the silver light bathes every leaf and stone, turning the whole forest into a natural temple to the goddess. According to the Circle's legends, Allalme once walked among its trees and left behind five silver tears, from which the region's first moonlit springs were born.

Wardens: **Serana Whitemoon**, an elf druid and member of the Circle, and **Kael Mornwood**, a human scout and holy hunter of Allalme, who has fought in countless clashes against werewolves and creatures of the night that tried to defile the forest. His bow, **Silver Dawn**, is a sacred relic of the Circle, blessed by the goddess herself.

### 6. Forgotten Forest (Ξεχασμένο Δάσος)
Perhaps the most mysterious of Allalme's sacred groves. Even the oldest maps disagree about its borders, and many travellers swear its roads move when no one is looking. It was once the realm of ancient hermits, druids and sages who recorded the knowledge of the ages. During a forgotten cataclysm, erased even from most chronicles, a strange magic wrapped the region: memories began to fade, paths to vanish and the names of the dead to be forgotten. Allalme mourned the lost knowledge and ordered the Circle to plant the sixth Sacred Willow there. When it took root, its silver branches spread deep into the forest, holding on to memories that would otherwise be lost forever. Druids believe the willow keeps, in its yearly rings, stories, names and events the world has forgotten.

Wardens: **Kallirroi**, a water nymph, and her husband **Aliakmonas**, a water genasi, two creatures of the Feywild. Admiring their bond, Allalme asked them to become the forest's protectors, and they accepted with joy and honour.

### 7. Far Forest (Μακρινό Δάσος)
It lies on the border between the High Forest and the Anauroch desert, a natural frontier and bulwark that shields the region from the dry touch of the burning desert. It is wild and unexplored: the trees rise like ancient giants, the stars shine brighter than anywhere else, roads are few, and most paths exist only in the Wardens' memory. Allalme chose it for the seventh Sacred Willow because she believed the balance of nature must be protected not only near civilisation but also where the world remains untouched. When the willow was planted, a silver light covered the treetops for seven nights in a row, and the druids spoke of Allalme herself blessing the new grove.

Wardens: **Lysandra Kalilian**, a human druid and Lorekeeper of the Circle, and **Edwin Hartwood**, a human monk who had been struck by the curse of lycanthropy and was healed by Allalme.

### 8. The Reaching Woods (Δάσος του Προορισμού)
The most enigmatic of the forests Allalme blessed; the Circle's druids believe no one finds it by chance. Allalme planted the eighth Sacred Willow there at the end of the Battle of Destiny (Μάχη του Πεπρωμένου), when she sensed the world entering a new age. Knowing that mortals would face choices that would shape the future of whole kingdoms, she created a holy place where souls could seek guidance without losing their free will.

Wardens: **Alina Tevena**, a human priestess of Allalme and seer of the Lunar Omens, and **Elias Silvertongue**, a human mystic.

### 9. Wood of Sharp Teeth (Δάσος των Κοφτερών Δοντιών)
The wildest, most dangerous and most merciless of the forests the Circle protects. Here nature is not a peaceful mother but a hunter, a place where blood, the hunt and survival are part of the natural order. Many young druids struggle to understand why Allalme blessed so violent a place. The elders' answer is simple (see Quotes). When Allalme planted the ninth Sacred Willow, the forest's creatures gathered around it instead of destroying it, a sign to the druids that even nature's wildest side recognises the divine balance.

Wardens: **Rowan Grayman** and his wife **Helgata Grayman**, two human barbarians of the Stag Clan.

### 10. Wealdath (Παρθένο Δάσος)
Allalme chose the Wealdath for the tenth Sacred Willow because she wished to preserve a piece of the world exactly as it was created, untouched by mortal wars, ambitions and conflicts. No kingdom ever truly claimed it, no road ever crossed it, and no woodcutter ever dared to harm its heart. Its trees are so old that many believe they still remember the Age of the Gods, and its air is full of the scent of wildflowers that grow nowhere else.

Wardens: the twin druids **Elan** and **Eleana Silverbloom**, as ancient as the forest itself. When Allalme approached them to plant her willow, the twins tested her. She prevailed in the Three Trials; Elan and Eleana accepted her as an equal and later worshipped her as their goddess. Now they stand as steadfast Wardens of the willow.
