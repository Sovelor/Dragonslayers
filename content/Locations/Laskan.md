# Laskan

> [!warning] Spoilers
> Reveals the identity of Garpask's master (folded box near the end).

**Role / Appears in:** Home city of [[Garpask Losark]] and [[Gaston Lentinson]], and the city [[Mahaya]] left in a hurry. Described in Part Two, *The Desperate Merchant* and *The Road to Lonlywood*.
**Greek name:** Λάσκαν
**Nickname:** "the City of Pirates" (η Πόλη των Πειρατών)
**Location:** A large city south of the Spine of the World (Ραχοκοκαλιά του Κόσμου)

## Summary
Laskan is known across the North for having the largest port and one of the biggest sea trades in all of Faerûn. Gaston calls it "the most beautiful city in all of Faerûn, but at the same time the worst place to live". In practice it is run by its gangs, pirates and underworld bosses rather than its official rulers.

## Landmarks and people
- **Places** Garpask lists when remembering home: the Harbour, the river Mirar (Μίραρ), the Open Shore (Ανοιχτή Ακτή), and the Tower of Magic (Πύργος της Μαγείας). The city also has Northern Gates, through which Gaston and Mahaya leave.
- **Tower of Magic:** seat of the Arcane Brotherhood (Απόκρυφη Αδελφότητα). Garpask introduces himself as "Mage of the Arcane Brotherhood and Initiate at the Tower of Magic of Laskan", and bows in the Brotherhood's manner. See the spoiler box below for his master.
- **Rulers:** the Five Lords of Laskan (Πέντε Άρχοντες). One of them is **Lord Renthor** (Ρένθορ).
- **Law:** the Laskan Guard (Φρουρά της Λάσκαν) does not protect citizens from the gangs.
- **Pirate fleet:** the city is famous for it.
- **Sellswords:** Laskan is even better known for its adventurers and hired blades. Mercenaries and self-styled heroes arrive every day looking for fame and gold, which is how Gaston finds an escort.

## The gangs
Gaston's success as a leather and cloth merchant drew the attention of "those who really run" the city: robber chiefs, pirates and underworld leaders. They demanded a cut of his earnings in exchange for "protection" from accidents, theft and robbers. Because they were never punished, they grew bolder and harsher, until Gaston kept almost nothing for himself. The Guard would not help him. About a month before the story, he called in debts and favours from powerful citizens, hired [[Mahaya]], and left for [[Lonlywood]].

> [!info] Garpask and Laskan
> Laskan is where Garpask grew up. Mentioning it visibly upsets him: he starts to say "where I and…" and stops, close to tears. He remembers three childhood friends, **Zarifas** (Ζάριφας), **Keninstan** (Κένινσταν) and **Abareth** (Άμπαρεθ). Keninstan is the son of Lord Renthor, and the four played at fighting dragons and demons in the courtyard of Renthor's mansion.

> [!warning]- Spoiler: Garpask's master
> Garpask trained in the Tower under **Frederick Frostfire** (Φρέντερικ Φρόστφάιερ), Archmage of the School of Ανάκληση, the 5th School of Magic of the Arcane Brotherhood. Frederick is also Garpask's father. On the night everything changed for them, a few months before the story, he led Garpask secretly through the Tower's magic corridors to his study and used his Pendant of Thoughts to show him a prophetic vision.
