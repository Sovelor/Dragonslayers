# Ancient Crypt

> [!warning] Spoilers
> Reveals the end of *Fateful Meeting* and *The Elf with the Purple Hair* in Part Two.

**Role / Appears in:** Hidden complex deep inside a mountain on the [[Road to Lonlywood]]. Part Two, *Fateful Meeting* and *The Elf with the Purple Hair*.

## Summary
While sheltering from a storm in a cave, the party finds a hidden stone door. Behind it an old tunnel leads deep into the mountain to a sealed complex, where [[Badinga Alpenrok]], [[Garpask Losark]] and [[Mahaya]] find [[Allalme Dragonborn]] asleep on an altar. [[Gaston Lentinson]] stays outside in the cave to guard the goods. Garpask recognises the place from a vision his father [[Frederick Frostfire]] showed him months before.

## Layout
1. **The tunnel.** Carved stone, worn but solid, with traces of paint and carvings too faded to read. The floor is marble under the dust. Badinga, taught by Bjorn, recognises the stone.
2. **The antechamber.** Stone torch holders and lamp stands, long burnt out. A marble double gate, about 4 m high and 3 m wide, once held shut by a round marble seal carved across both leaves.
3. **The great hall.** Almost square, over 30 m a side, hot and stale, with faded murals and a damaged ceiling. It has a stone door in each of the other three walls, all smaller than the gate.
4. **The crypt.** Reached through the left-hand door and a short corridor (about 15 m). Cool, damp and lit by silver moonlight through a crack in the ceiling. It is full of sealed stone sarcophagi and clay burial jars, with a large stone altar behind them. The altar's lid lifts off, hiding a stash.

## Clues
- **The broken seal.** The seal lies shattered in front of the gate, its marble partly melted, and the gate is scorched at the centre. Garpask judges it was hit by a Lightning Bolt spell (Ριπή Κεραυνού), which only very experienced casters can cast. He cannot cast it yet.
- **Tracks.** Two pairs of footprints lead in and out, deeper going in and lighter coming out. Mahaya reads them as a heavy load carried in and left behind, about two days old. They lead to the altar.
- **Something following.** In the tunnel, Mahaya hears near-silent footsteps behind them. He insists no creature could move that quietly without him noticing, then brushes it off as his imagination, but keeps his bow in hand.
