# Abandoned Fortress

**Appears in:** Prologue

## Summary
Where [[Badinga Alpenrok]] and [[Elizium]] first meet, during a snowstorm. It is an old, half-ruined outpost of the Ten-Towns Guard (Φρουρά της Δεκάπολης), its battlements full of snow, looking "more like a burial mound than a fortress". Badinga reaches it after getting lost in the blizzard: the path is buried and the stone pillars that mark the way to Lonlywood are nowhere to be seen. A large wooden door, half-buried in snow, is still standing.
