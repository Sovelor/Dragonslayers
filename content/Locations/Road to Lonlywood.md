# Road to Lonlywood

> [!warning] Spoilers
> Reveals Elizium's death and the outcome of the goblin ambush.

**Appears in:** Part Two — *A New Beginning* onward

## Summary
The road Badinga and Garpask travel after burying Elizium. This is where they meet [[Gaston Lentinson]] and [[Mahaya]] at a deliberately felled tree, and where the 6-goblin ambush led by [[Gourd]] takes place. Later, a storm forces the group to shelter in a cave, where a hidden stone door leads to the [[Ancient Crypt]]. There they find [[Allalme Dragonborn]].

> [!question] Open thread
> The party has explored only part of the [[Ancient Crypt]]. Mahaya wants to check the rest before they return to Gaston.
