# Road to Lonlywood

**Appears in:** Part Two — *A New Beginning* onward

## Summary
The road Badinga and Garpask travel after burying Elizium. This is where they meet [[Gaston Lentinson]] and [[Mahaya]] at a deliberately felled tree, and where the 6-goblin ambush led by [[Gourd]] takes place. Later, a storm forces the group to shelter in a cave, where a hidden stone door is found deep inside (*Fateful Meeting* — manuscript continues past this point).
