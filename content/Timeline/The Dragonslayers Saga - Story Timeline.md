# The Dragonslayers Saga - Story Timeline

> [!info] Source of truth
> `The Dragonslayers Saga.docx` — this page is a navigation aid, not a copy. Update it as the DM revises Part Two onward.

Master chronological index of the manuscript "The Dragonslayers Saga."

## Untitled Opening (precedes the Prologue)
- [[Atropos]], in [[The Void]] — plotting to break free.

## Prologue
- [[Badinga Alpenrok]] meets [[Elizium]] in the [[Abandoned Fortress]] during a snowstorm.

## Part One — ΜΕΡΟΣ ΠΡΩΤΟ
1. **A Strange Acquaintance** — [[Garpask Losark]] is rescued from wild dogs on the [[Snowy Path]]; the trio forms.
2. **Loss** — Goblin/hyena night ambush at [[Forest Camp]]; [[Elizium]] dies protecting Badinga and Garpask.
3. **Badinga** *(flashback)* — Badinga's morning routine and training under Bjorn.

## Part Two — ΜΕΡΟΣ ΔΕΥΤΕΡΟ
1. **A New Beginning** — Badinga and Garpask travel on in near-silence, grieving. Meet [[Gaston Lentinson]] and [[Mahaya]] at a deliberately felled tree on the [[Road to Lonlywood]].
2. **The Desperate Merchant** — Introductions; Gaston's backstory fleeing Laskan's gangs; alliance formed (10 gold each + goods to help clear the log and escort the cart).
3. **The Goblin Ambush** — 6-goblin trap sprung; mini-boss [[Gourd]] defeated.
4. **The Road to Lonlywood** — A storm forces the group to shelter in a cave.
5. **Fateful Meeting** — A hidden stone door is found deep in the cave.
