**<u>Session 1</u>** (the group's 11th)

We are in the **Anauroch** desert.

In this session we are in the city of **Saiyaddar**, where Edric comes from. It is a small city (you can walk all of it in about an hour) with about 1,000 inhabitants, multicultural. The inhabitants are mostly: humans, half-elves, very few elves, very few dwarves, etc. It was founded in the desert because there was water.

People call the city **the Emerald of Anauroch**.

**Lilith** and **Garrick** are already in the city. They have already booked a twin room (they have been travelling together for about a year) at the city's inn.

The group, together with Edric's 3 local friends, whom they saved from a Satyr in the previous session, sees the city and heads there.

<u>News from the 3 (Ferith, Saoich, Wolly) for Edric</u>:

- The patrols have increased. Their tracks were lost 3-4 days ago; the Satyr captured them (which is rare).
- There is a presence of creatures and monsters. It had started before Edric left (he has been away about 1.5 months), but lately it has got exponentially worse.

Elizium is driving the carriage and Ezariel sits next to him; she tells him that at some point she wants the two of them to talk alone. She needs his help and asks for complete secrecy, even from the members of the group.

Selina and Azhyra are scheduled to leave the next day. Azhyra does not want to go with her, but does not say so. Elizium tells Selina that he will go with them too.

They finally reach the city.

**Edric** sees a spot in his hometown, kneels and pays his respects. His friends ask him whether he will go to the Guard or stay with them, and he chooses to give his report at once. So he heads for the Garrison.

**Selina** and **Azhyra** buy supplies for their journey and head for the Branch of the Order of Freedom to report.

**Ezariel** and **Elizium** go to the market. They look for somewhere to leave the carriage and a local sends them to the stables. Elizium says the carriage is Edric's, and so they leave it there free of charge.

Ezariel asks him "*Why will you leave?"*. He answers "*On a mission!"* and his manner changes, his voice turns serious. Elizium wants to find out what has happened to him and how he will do what he has been asked to do.

Ezariel turns serious too. They meet a resident and ask him about lodgings. He suggests some and also tells them about other well-known places in the city:

- The "Golden Camel" inn (about 15 minutes' walk from where they are)
- Sun Lake: the centre of the city
- Hero's Island: the statue of Galag stands there (a member of the Dragonslayers, a knight of the Brotherhood of Light).
- The Court of Five Wells: the city's town hall is there, where the caliph Terin Alias lives.
- The Stone Terraces: built on the ruins of an ancient empire, Netheril.
- The House of Stars: something like a planetarium. The scholar lives there.
- The Freedom Hostel: the Golden Sand Branch is there (a Branch of the Order of Freedom).

They head for the "Golden Camel" inn. Elizium says that he has no memories and tells again what he remembers (about the hourglass). He is completely sincere.

Ezariel asks for his help. She wants him to look into some things she has in her possession. She reminds him of the Dragon Egg.

He suggests that she come with them (with Selina and Azhyra) and she tells him that this cannot be. She stresses that what she wants to discuss he must never tell anyone, and if need be *take it with him to his grave*. Elizium seems to "drift away" and it is as if he does not hear her. Ezariel goes to touch him and he deftly grabs her, takes her sword and holds it to her throat. He throws it down and seems to come back to himself. He asks her "Where are we? Were we attacked?", and it seems as if a migraine has seized him. He still wonders what is going on and puts it down to tiredness. He is drenched in sweat, which makes no sense at dusk.

They try to carry on the conversation, but it does not go well. His hands are shaking (he cannot even screw the cap on his flask).

They reach the inn.

**Selina** and **Azhyra** go to the <u>Golden Sand</u>.

Entering the Branch they see a woman, a local half elf (Rogue), **Naira the Silence**, who is taking notes (left-handed), and another local, a human guard, **Iben Falouk**, who is sharpening his weapons.

In the waiting room they see two people (us):

**Garrick**: a human man, not a local, with charismatic movements, light brown hair, eyes of two colours (right green, left copper).

**Lilith**: a tiefling woman with a human skin colour, with a finely carved bow, long brown hair and golden-green eyes. Her movements are more nervous.

**Muffin**: a dog that gives off vapours, the same colours as the girl.

Azhyra is impressed to see a tiefling and asks Selina whether she knows us (she seems interested in us). Selina answers that she does not know us, and that if we are there, either we need something or we are possible allies.

Garrick and Lilith see the symbol of the Circle of the Moon on Selina's shield and also wonder who they might be.

Naira and Iben approach Selina and Azhyra (they seem to know them). Iben is impressed that they are alive and calls Azhyra "squinty". Azhyra asks them about us, and Lilith sees that they are saying something about us and coming over to introduce us.

At that moment the Branch Master, Sarena Al Nasin, comes out from behind the screen. She receives Selina, and Naira follows them.

Iben and Azhyra stay with us. Iben asks about us. Garrick tells them that he is looking for a relic and that we come from the <u>Cold Wood</u> (Ψυχρό Δάσος) and that the village he comes from is <u>Tulrun</u>. Garrick has taken over the conversation and Lilith just smiles at them ironically. At some point they ask Azhyra too to go in with the Branch Master, and before she goes in she asks us to wait for her.

**Edric**, on his way to the Garrison to give his report, meets people from his town. Everyone knows him and greets him (like a village mayor). This makes him feel rather awkward.

He reaches the Garrison and gives his report: the group has returned and everything went well. They asked him whether he is happy in the Brotherhood of Light and he spoke very highly of his group. Then they commented on his new sword, which he told them he has named Thelisis (he gives a name to all his gear).

Finally, they tell him to report to the Branch too, and to go and see his parents (Andrew and Nadia) because they will be worried.

**Ezariel** and **Elizium** go into the inn (a 3-storey building).

Ezariel goes in first and sees the innkeeper, a human over 50, with a Taliban-style beard, very thin and hyperactive. He manages everything alone. His name is Omar Bin Rashid.

Elizium comes in too; he seems to have recovered.

Ezariel goes to book the rooms and Elizium sits at a table. He looks thoughtful, troubled, and stares at the table. Ezariel goes to the innkeeper, who at once brings her salep as a welcome. At his urging she tries it hesitantly and begins to feel cooler (that is why the "Bedouins" drink it). She asks about rooms (2-3 rooms for one night, and the cost -> 4 silver coins). He also offers her dinner (salted meat, pastourma, dried fruit, salads, boiled vegetables and camel's milk). He apologises for the possible meals. Even so, she asks for some of everything. They go to Elizium, who drinks the salep they offer him in one gulp and finds it excellent.

Ezariel asks Elizium to go with her to a room. He turns her down (romantically speaking), joking. She tells him that *she wants to give him the Egg, because he is a wizard, so that he can tell her what it is and study it secretly and alchemically.* They decide to go upstairs at once for the study, without eating dinner (they will eat later). They tell the innkeeper that the other members of their group will come too, and give their names, for their own part Elizium (no surname, he does not remember it) and McAllister (she does not give her first name).

At the Golden Sand Branch, **Garrick** asks about the relic and they tell us that they will not be able to see us, because these two (Selina, Azhyra) are important. At Garrick's urging, **Lilith** agrees to wait, since they think that perhaps they could help them, and because she has the same curiosity as **Azhyra** (she does not often meet Tieflings either).

Azhyra goes in and Lilith tries to hear what they are saying. There is a second room inside too. Lilith moves closer to the counter to hear better. She manages to hear that:

**Selina** gives her report. Sarena tells her that she does not need to be formal, but Selina answers that this is how she wants it. At that moment Azhyra goes in too and the door closes. Azhyra starts giving her report and the conversation follows.

<u>I hear</u>: - Orders from High Lady Lavern for us to go to the Castle-State. She asks whether they should worry.

- Selina answers that they do not know, and Azhyra asks whether they have received any word from headquarters.

- Sarena tells them that there is activity in communications and matters are being looked into under the <u>code name</u> **<u>REPTILE</u>** (it means Dragon, but I do not know that) and not only those.

- The most serious thing. There are reports of an appearance of Shar (an old evil goddess, dead).

Azhyra questions whether it is safe and Sarena answers that she considers it safe, since it is the Branch of the Order of Freedom. Azhyra writes her report (the vision and what happened in Brook Hollow) and Selina does the same.

- Sarena asks them whether they are joking and tells them "The shadow swords were destroyed", and Azhyra answers her "Obviously not" and that they must go back, and adds that "*The temple of Shar was destroyed. The Holy sword was defeated and the six have been broken. A Shadow Dragon appeared* (a shadow dragon appeared in a dwarf colony).

- They must inform **Zafar**.

- Azhyra asks to contact **Loroves**. The other two do not know her (the Edge is not known everywhere). Sarena takes them to the cellar.

Lilith can <u>no longer hear</u>.

In the cellar there is a sending circle. While Sarena is in the circle, Azhyra tells Selina that she will stay here (she will not go with her to the Castle-State). Selina is upset and anxious; she is afraid she will fail. Azhyra tells her to go to the Order and speak to Lavern, and sends her off at first light. Selina asks her to look after the others. Sarena comes out of the circles (they glow but do not switch off) and sends them to the Golden Camel inn. Azhyra burns the reports; Sarena tries to stop her and tells her that they must be written, and she answers that Sarena should write them herself in summary and she will take the responsibility if need be.

**Edric** reaches the Branch too. He sees Selina and Azhyra coming out from behind the counter. He also sees Iben, whom he knows, and greets him.

**Ezariel** and **Elizium** (stoic and serious) go into the room. They dim the light and draw the curtains. Ezariel asks for secrecy again. He must not tell anyone. Elizium sits on the bed. Ezariel sits on the same bed facing him, takes out the box and leaves it between them. It catches his attention at once and he looks at the runes. Ezariel tells him "*In here is the Egg*", opens the box and takes it out.

Elizium takes the Egg, looks at the box and opens it with two movements. A smell of sulphur is released. *The Egg contains demonic blood,* ***Devil's blood*** *to be exact.* He shakes it, sees golden threads and closes it. He tells her *"This is not a good thing"* and asks her where she found it, as he places it carefully between them. Ezariel picks it up and puts it in her bag.

Elizium: "It is the real blood of a devilish being"

Ezariel asks him what it is used for and he answers: "If you want to kill someone, it is poison. A catalyst if you want to desecrate a Holy place. It can destroy a whole place. Where did you find it?". She does not answer him. He asks her to let him try something. He puts his hand in his bag, takes out a cloth and asks her for a drop.

She hesitates, and although he is not very convincing, he is set on doing the test, and it will take 15 minutes (to see <u>which egg of the 9 hells</u> it is). Ezariel gives the drop; Elizium sends her out of the room (so that she is safe) and takes things out to set up his alchemist's bench.

**Edric** comes and introduces himself to **Garrick** and **Lilith**. He does not look like a local to us

**Selina** takes **Edric** aside and Lilith "pricked up her ears". She tells him everything, and also that he is released from service to her, but they are two friends.

Azhyra comes and talks to us. Garrick tells her that we are looking for a relic and that we have suffered a great deal in the search for this *tool*, and also that we reached the city that morning. While this conversation was going on, Iben made a racket sharpening his swords, because he heard what we were saying (probably on purpose). Azhyra told us that if it is, she will speak to the Branch too, so that they will see us the next day and help us. Once our conversation was over, and we found that we are staying at the same inn, Garrick and Lilith left. **Muffin** followed Lilith on his own, without her needing to give him any command.

Azhyra tells Edric that she burned the reports, and that he should be brief in his own. She also tells Sarena to see us first thing, so that they can help us. She told her that we are looking for a relic, and Sarena told her that in Saiyaddar she knows of one relic. Edric said he does not think it is that one, and Azhyra asked which one they mean.

Sarena answered that they protect it and that it is the **<u>Vampire Killer (Σφαγέας των Βρικολάκων)</u>**. It was made by the weaponsmith and inventor **<u>Rinal Kant Olfi</u>**. Galag's second-to-last mission. It is a lost weapon, which the Brotherhood asked Galag to hide. It is a safeguard for facing the armies of **Carmilla.**

<u>Rumours – Legends</u> about the Vampire Killer: Lord Edgnar asked Galag to hide and secure it. He took it to his own city (Saiyaddar), and to protect it Mielikki (the city's goddess) hid it in the <u>Palm Cemetery</u>, where Nimue sent a spirit of nature to protect it.

Sarena asked Azhyra and Edric to look into it.

Edric gives his report; he is excessively brief, but Sarena does not press him.

At the inn, **Elizium**, as soon as he had finished what he was doing with the blood, called out to **Ezariel** "*Come in now!*".

When she comes in, he throws the cloth to the floor; it is burning, and he casts Rain of frost. He tells her that she has in her possession the **blood of Asmodeus** (father of Sovelor,) and *he has been dead for 10 years*. He asks her again "*Tell me now where you found it*" and Ezariel answers him " *How do you know that?*" (Elizium woke up 1 year ago). He grabs his bag of holding and trembles; he answers her "*I must have read it somewhere*" and when she asked him again where, he shouted at her "*I don't know!*".

Elizium: "*Let's take a breath. We agree that it is not good!*" and he starts making guesses. He asks her "*Do they know you have it?*"

Ezariel: "*The guys in the red cloaks know. The only thing they care about is the egg. But it is not the only thing I have."*

Elizium: "*Why, did they recognise you?* (irony) *Are we safe?*"

Ezariel: " *No*".

Elizium: "*Bring the box*" and Ezariel gives it to him. He takes it and checks it for some tracking spell. He concludes that the Illumians have disabled it. He asks her to come with them and Ezariel refuses and tells him "*As long as I am with you, you are in danger!".*

Then Elizium takes out a gold coin of exceptional minting and badly worn. He tells her: "*Guard it like the apple of your eye!".* Ezariel takes it (it belongs to none of the known cities; she would swear she has seen it before, perhaps in a book or in a history lesson, but she could not remember).

Ezariel: "Where did you find it?"

Elizium: "*I had it on me when I woke up."*

Ezariel tells him that she did not expect this about the egg, and that she would like to give him one more object to look into. She also tells him that she has a mission, through which she found the Egg, and that her time is running short. She asks him <u>to trust no one. Perhaps only Edric</u>, if he ever sees him again. She tells him: "*At the Brotherhood, look for information about the Egg in secret, and read everything about the city of **Silvermoon**, from its founding to the present day. Nothing to anyone!*".

Elizium: "*Only to you and Edric*."

Ezariel: "*Nothing to Edric or to me*"

Elizium: "*You know we will meet again*."

Ezariel: "*If Edric comes to take you somewhere, you will listen to him and go with him*."

Elizium: "*I won't let you down!*"

Ezariel: "*I don't want you to learn anything about me, that's why. Now you are in danger too.*"

Elizium, completely different from the last time they had this conversation, tells her "*Everything will be as you wish. Keep what I gave you safe.*"

Ezariel touches his shoulder and tells him "*You must care about your life from now on. There are things that weigh on us.*"

Elizium takes her hand and answers her: "*What worth and meaning does life have if there are no friends around you? Don't live like that!*".

It was a shocking and at the same time pleasant moment. Elizium goes on: "*Being alone is worse than having a blade driven into your heart.*" and thanks her for trusting him.

**Red cloaks**: they have the blood of Asmodeus in their possession (Pure blood, pure, not from a Tiefling).

**Lilith** and **Garrick** return to the inn "The Golden Camel". On the way back she confides to him everything she heard at the Branch. Garrick thinks that Azhyra and Selina could help them, and Lilith, though wary, agrees to talk to them later. They arrive and Lilith goes up to their room with **Muffin**, takes a bath and rests a little. Garrick goes to eat and picks a table out of the way, but in view. After a while Lilith comes down to join him. It is about 9 p.m. and it is quite crowded, mostly travellers.

**Azhyra** and **Edric** head for his <u>family home</u>. There Azhyra sees a man of about 45, Edric's father (Andrew), who is feeding his animals and looking out at the street. Passers-by say to him "*Welcome him home*", since the news has arrived that their son Edric has returned. Smoke comes from the house and there is light. Azhyra tells Edric that she does not want them to touch her, and Edric assures her that he will tell them. They come closer and his father looks at him. He washes and knocks on the window to call his wife Nadia. She comes out too.

They welcome them. They do not touch Azhyra, after Edric steps in and tells them that it is not done where she comes from.

Azhyra tells them that <u>Edric will be proposed for Knight.</u> His mother cries and his father feels proud.

Azhyra sees many similarities between Edric and his father.

Edric shows Andrew how his weapon (Trusty) has developed, and his father finds it excellent. Andrew also sees Edric's new sword, <u>Thelisis</u> (as he himself calls it, and he described it as "a new member of the family". Andrew examines Thelisis and asks Edric where he found it. He finds it <u>too perfect</u>, light and well balanced: "*nothing is ever perfect. Perhaps it is easier to do harm with it*", as he says. Azhyra asks him whether it could possibly be evil and he answers her "*A tool is never evil. Only the hand that uses it.'*".

<u>Thelisis</u>: the weapon of the Life-giving Prince. It was found in Theron's tomb.

Edric decides to stay with his family and asks Azhyra for them all to come together the next day. Azhyra takes the road back to the inn. She takes with her a gift for the group (dates).

At the inn, **Selina** greets **Garrick** and **Lilith** with a slight bow. Garrick returns it, Lilith does not. With Selina is **Elizium** too (a high elf, a wizard, in armour). He seems absent-minded. He looks at Lilith and says " *Interesting*". They sit with us, at Garrick's invitation. As Elizium says, he has never before seen my kind, that is, <u>a Tiefling in whom the human nature prevails</u>. Lilith answers him ironically and with annoyance: "*Maybe it prevails only on the outside*". Out of curiosity Elizium asks:

About where I come from -> I answer from **Luskan** (it is in the West)

Whether I knew my mother -> I answer "Yes" (a lie)

Whether I knew my father -> I also answer "Yes" (the truth) and irritably ask whether it matters.

During this conversation Selina seems annoyed and tries to excuse Elizium. At that moment (luckily) **Azhyra** comes in, and she too comes and sits with us.

Azhyra asks Elizium about Ezariel (I wonder how many of them there are by now!) and he answers that he has not seen her.

Azhyra tells us that she tried to find out about what we are looking for. I (Lilith) send **Muffin** to sniff her. He licks her and she strokes him and asks me whether she can give him some of the salted meat that was on the table. I allowed it.

Garrick and I do not know whom we can trust. (For me Muffin's reaction was positive).

Ezariel comes down too (a half-elf with a large part of her face covered by a mask). Selina greets her and she too comes to the table. Ezariel's instinct does not "ring" at all. She only has a <u>strange feeling about Garrick</u> (Azhyra had the same feeling as soon as she met him at the Branch).

Azhyra gives her the dates she brought from Edric's house, and there is a strange atmosphere between them (sourness). When Azhyra tells her (with attitude) *"Listen to your mum, you brat"*, Ezariel turns terribly serious.

Garrick tells them that we are looking for the weapon called the **<u>Vampire Killer</u>**, but we have no idea what it is like. Ezariel asks us whether we have got mixed up with Vampires. Selina asks whether it concerns both of us. When we confirm it, [she speaks] more humanly (she sets aside her ever-composed manner) and tells us that she would help us, but she has to leave. But she will speak to the Order so that they help us, and if we need anything we should tell Edric, and he will help us too.

Then we go to our rooms.

**Azhyra** goes to Ezariel's room (she is staying with Elizium) and throws the dates onto the table for her. She returns to her own room (she is staying with Selina). There she explains to Selina why she wants to stay and asks her to look after Elizium. She also tells her to tell Lavern to let Loroves know, and that she herself will wait for some word.

<u>Dawn</u>: Selina prays.

Elizium did not lie down to "sleep". Ezariel comes out of her meditation and sees Elizium wearing only his trousers, gazing silently out of the window.

Elizium goes to find Selina. Azhyra opens the door to him and tells him that Selina is praying, and they go together for one last smoke.

Everyone comes downstairs. (Edric is still at his family home).

We all have breakfast together and decide that we will all go to Edric's house. We make a stop on the way, so as not to go empty-handed.

<u>At Edric's house:</u> We notice the decorative weapons (swords). They tell us that Andrew was in the Guard; he never took a rank because he did not want one (he wanted to be out helping). He is very proud of his son.

! Edric's idol was Galag. When Edric was little, Galag was a trainee.

**Elizium** stays silent. He is not his usual self. He follows Selina's ways closely.

<u>News from Edric and his parents:</u> For the last two years there has been tension and a lot of movement of hostile creatures. As if something draws them. They do not understand why.

Edric says goodbye to his family and we all go together to the <u>Branch.</u> (about 5 minutes away)

There we find: **Iben Falouk** (a former caravan mercenary), **Sarena** (a veteran Ranger of the Anauroch), **Nairan** (a spy) and all the members. **Voran the Iron-born** (a Dwarf, beard down to his belly, vestments with an axe and a hammer and the symbol of Moralin, healer and blacksmith), **Elis Astrakius** (a Half elf, completely white, as rare as me, a Wizard, a scholar of the ruins of Netherin).

Selina goes into the office with Branch Master Sarena and her team. At first they talk about us (Garrick and Lilith). Selina says that we know about the object and proposes that they help us; she looks at Edric and Azhyra, who agree to do it. Sarena says that their own job is to protect this relic, but if the city agrees they can send two of their members with us to protect it, and that she will speak with the caliph so that we can make a deal. She also stresses that the Golden Sand Branch stands by us because of the agreement with Selina and Azhyra, who agree to take the responsibility for protecting and returning the Vampire Killer.

**Selina** says goodbye to her group and thanks each of them in turn. She gives Edric a scroll; it is the proposal for him to join the Brotherhood of Light and become a Knight. She tells them: "*For what is coming, I will be at your side if you need me*" (looking at Azhyra).

**Elizium** says goodbye too. Edric and Azhyra bow with great grace. Elizium returns the bow, and Ezariel is struck that he made such a bow (Elizium is a "street" elf). Elizium has presence, and it shows.

Selina and Elizium leave, and the rest call us into the office to tell us that they will help us. The Vampire Killer is in the Palm Cemetery.

**Palm Cemetery**: it is the city's old cemetery and is no longer used. It is named so because there is a palm tree three times the normal size. The dead are not buried; their ashes are scattered there. There are no gravestones, but memorials with the names of those who sleep there. It has not been used since before Galag, who confided that he had hidden the **Vampire Killer** there. Galag had told Edric never to go near it, because it is dangerous. There is thick mist in the cemetery.

The procedures begin for the city's approval for us to take the Vampire Killer. After a while the fighter finds us and tells us that we have permission to use it, as long as it does not fall into the wrong [hands]. He also threatens us indirectly that if we do anything we should not, it will end badly for us. Finally, he warns us that the way there may be dangerous (1 day's journey).

We head for <u>Sun Lake</u> (Saiyaddar's central market) to get magic items. **Ezariel** finds a silver arrow with runes, which the seller tells her never misses, but she can use it only once.

We set off with the cart, to get the weapon. We go by cart. At the front are Edric, who drives the cart, and Garrick. Inside are the rest (Azhyra, Ezariel, Lilith, Muffin).

**Muffin** enjoys Azhyra's petting and the pieces of pastourma she gives him. Until suddenly he starts to growl. Lilith goes to him and asks him what he smells. She feels as if he is asking her permission to go and see what it is, and she tells him "Go!". Muffin <u>teleports for the first time</u> in front of the group. He goes a good few metres ahead of the cart (30 feet) and growls at a dune. Lilith whistles for him to come back and he returns, again by teleporting. Garrick and Lilith, who notice Muffin's reaction, take up their weapons and suggest that the others do the same.

**Lilith** and **Ezariel** [notice] that the dune looks freshly dug. As if something big is hiding underneath. Its distance from our cart is about 90 feet.

**Garrick** and **Edric** move closer. Garrick throws a stone that way and a stench comes to us (a great stink).

A Desert Troll (Edric tells us) with one eye rises from the dune .

<u>The fight</u>:

**Azhyra** moves closer. So does **Ezariel**, drawing her bow. **Edric** takes a full defensive stance with his shield.

The **Troll** speaks, runs and leaps at Edric. It hits his shield and goes over him as if in slow motion. Edric draws his sword, holds his shield correctly, and the Troll's claws strike sparks off it, and then it falls. And Edric "sinks" a little further into the sand (he sinks a little). The Troll curses in giant, something like "*Shit*", and Edric lowers his sword and shield.

**Garrick** (activates his Aura) closes his eyes, which become more like slits (like a dragon's eyes) and brighter, and a greenish-yellow energy bursts around him, pulsing on him and spreading to Edric, Azhyra and Ezariel (who were closer to him). They see it as a frost. He raises his Morningstar, takes a breath, his symbol lights up and the veins at the back of his neck glow. Acid comes out of his mouth in a line and burns. The Troll "takes" half of it, its eyes bulge and it looks at Garrick, steps back and is in pain.

**Edric** sees the acid and knows that it is the only thing, together with fire, that can wound the Troll permanently. '

**Muffin** teleports behind the Troll. **Lilith** moves closer and prepares a spell with her focus. Energy like spectral climbing thorns wraps around her arrow.

Azhyra says in Infernal "Kneel, you stupid creature" (as she moves, the air around her darkens). Red energy swirls around her and shoots into its chest. The Troll trembles.

**Ezariel** does a "barrel roll", draws her short sword and strikes the Troll in the groin. She says in giant "Get lost, you filthy creature".

The Troll's two wounds close slightly.

**Edric** smiles and strikes its leg low at the ankle, to push it back.

The **Troll** tries to hit Garrick (it has its eye on him because of the acid) with two claws. Garrick feels a strong force. The first claw (which misses) catches his armour at a slant. The second hits him in the shoulder. It tries to bite Ezariel and only hits her armour.

**Garrick** changes Aura and something like a field comes up around us, dripping acid for a while. With Muffin's help he finds an opening, smacks it in the jaw and knocks a tooth.

**Lilith** steps forward and makes a double attack. Her first arrow with the thorns bursts on it, on its arm. It dodges the second.

**Azhyra** hits it, but does nothing to it. It just does not know where it came from.

**Ezariel** hits it and curses it, and it answers her in the same tone.

**Edric** hits it and cuts off its wrist. It shoves its arm into the sand and it sticks back on.

The **Troll** tries to hit only Edric. It hits him in the shoulder and tears it open. And then acid bursts in its face. It tries to bite Edric and he pushes it away.

**Garrick** pulls out his Morningstar and hits it in the eye.

All of **Lilith**'s and **Muffin**'s attacks fail.

**Azhyra** hits it with a ray in the jaw, and **Ezariel** with one blow cuts both its tendons. It falls onto a rock and breaks its neck.

**Edric** tells **Garrick** to burn it. Azhyra, Ezariel and Edric hit it while it tries to recover, and Garrick unleashes acid and burns it. It melts.
