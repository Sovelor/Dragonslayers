# Lorenzo von Matterhorn

> [!abstract] Ελληνικά → [[Λορέντζο]]

![[Characters - Lorenzo.jpg|300]]

**Role:** Player character in [[The Iron Hand of Progress]] (S1) · wizard, scholar, gentleman and younger brother of **Hector von Matterhorn**

**Race:** Human ("of the finest stock") · **Class:** Wizard, School of Evocation, with a minor in Illusion, purely theoretical (level: "by the Brotherhood's reckoning, considerably higher, but one does not like to boast") · **Pronouns:** he/him · **Age:** "a distinguished thirty-three", and has been for some years now · **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral · **Home:** the von Matterhorn estate, high in the mountains · **Languages:** Common, Draconic, Dwarvish

## Summary
Lorenzo von Matterhorn is a wizard, scholar, gentleman and the younger brother of **Hector von Matterhorn**. He studied at the **Arcane Brotherhood** under the personal direction of Archmage **Arklem Greeth** and was top of his class (the records were lost in a fire, a very thorough fire). Now he brings his considerable learning to the field, where it is sorely needed. He is also an author of several unpublished treatises (academic politics) and a collector of rare books, odd gems and favours owed.

## Appearance
Tall and broad-shouldered, in his thirties, built more like a duellist than a librarian: a strong jaw, dark tousled curls, a neat beard and a waxed moustache with a slight upward curl, a calm, smug half-smile and a steady, knowing gaze, usually aimed a little sideways. His look is Victorian gentleman meets sorcerer, committed to purple: a deep purple and black frock coat with gold-trimmed lapels and cuffs, a filigree brocade waistcoat with brass buttons, a violet silk cravat with an amethyst brooch, a pocket-watch chain and a purple pocket square. A small spellbook never leaves his hand.

When he casts, a glowing violet spectral hand appears above his open palm; he calls this "an advanced somatic technique". There are never ink stains on his fingers ("A tidy scholar is a good scholar"). The magic comes from his hands, never from the book. His voice is smooth, low and unhurried, refined and faintly amused, as if he were reciting the third corollary in his head.

## Personality
Charming, a bit vain and quietly dangerous. Lorenzo wins arguments with a smile, though the fireball is always an option. He loves an audience, a good wine and a well-placed academic reference (preferably one nobody can check). He always seems to be standing exactly where something remarkable is about to happen, pocket watch in hand, and looks faintly disappointed when nothing does. He is never seen studying, never prepares spells and never seems to need to: "Magic should feel natural," he says, then quickly adds, "after years of rigorous training, of course."

He is unhurried and theatrical, in a controlled way, never rushed and never worried. He straightens his cuffs and touches his cravat when he is thinking, and checks his pocket watch at every dramatic moment. Before a spell he pauses, strokes the book's cover and says "Let me consult the text," then casts at once, as if the book had told him what to do. If he opens it, he glances at the blank page and nods sagely.
- **Ideal:** knowledge must be respected. Gently. Like a sleeping cat.
- **Bonds:** his spellbook (a wizard never lets his book leave his hand: it is less a tool, more a philosophy); his dear master, Archmage Arklem Greeth ("Brilliant man. Never ate, never slept, utterly devoted to his work. Terrible at remembering my name, which in hindsight was very convenient."); his elder brother Hector ("Family is everything. Especially the family name.")
- **Flaws:** he cannot resist correcting a real scholar, even when he has no idea what they are talking about; he may, on occasion, embellish.

## Background
The younger son of **House von Matterhorn** and brother of Hector, Lorenzo showed a "remarkable aptitude for the arcane" very young: candles had a habit of lighting themselves when he entered a room, which he attributes to precocious study. He was sent to the Arcane Brotherhood and studied there under the personal direction of Archmage Arklem Greeth, who used to say, "Lorenzo, you are the finest pupil I have never had to correct." He graduated top of his class, though the records were lost in a fire. Tragic. A very thorough fire.

He doesn't like to boast about his teachers, mostly because they asked him not to. He spent many years away from home, "the Brotherhood, then the field." When he finally returned to the family, Hector was delighted, or at least polite. Hector's bodyguard, **Jo**, has been watching him closely ever since. "Years abroad change a man," Lorenzo explains. "Mostly for the better." Since then he has travelled the world testing theory against practice ("I merely favour the field-tested approach"), writing treatises he never publishes, and consulting a spellbook in a personal cipher so complex that the pages look completely blank to the untrained eye. Any rumour that he is a "Sorcerer" is, in his words, academic slander.

> [!info] Rumours and open mysteries
> - Some say no one has ever seen him prepare a spell, and that his spellbook is blank. (It is a personal cipher.)
> - Some say the Arcane Brotherhood has no record of any student called von Matterhorn. (There was a fire.)
> - Some say Hector's younger brother came home a very different man from the boy who left.
> - Some say trouble follows him; others say he arrives just before it, and checks his watch.
> - Some say candles and campfires behave strangely around him, and that he once put out a burning curtain without a word or a gesture. (The somatic component is the real art. Most people miss it.)
> - Tavern keepers find him a fine gentleman who tips generously in compliments. Real scholars tell him "We'll have to compare notes sometime" (he hopes they never do). Hector is glad to have his brother back, or too polite to say otherwise. Jo doesn't trust him, and never lets him out of sight when Hector is near.
> - Why has nobody ever seen him study? What is really written in the spellbook? How does he cast almost spontaneously? (Years of study and extreme talent.) Why does he never talk about his childhood with Hector? Why is Jo so sure something is wrong with him? How does he always know where to be when history happens?

## Goals & fears
- **Goals:** to witness every legendary event of the age with his own eyes, from a good seat, purely for research (a serious treatise demands primary sources); to complete that treatise, a chronicle of the great events of our time (unpublished; academic politics); never, under any circumstances, to be bored. Should history be slow to arrive, a scholar may... encourage it. Gently. Like a sleeping cat.
- **Fears:** being asked to lend out his spellbook, or to copy a spell from someone else's book; real scholars with good memories and a free evening; archives, records, and anyone saying "Let's look you up"; family dinners where someone says "Do you remember when we were children...?"; Jo's eyes; boredom, a quiet week, and peace treaties that actually hold.

## Relationships
- **Hector von Matterhorn**: his elder brother, "a good man, if somewhat humourless", and an ally: "my dear brother, and a very generous one".
- **Jo**: Hector's bodyguard, suspicious of everyone and of Lorenzo in particular ("a professional quirk"). An enemy, or at least a very persistent critic: she keeps asking questions only the real... only a younger brother would know.
- **Arklem Greeth**: his beloved master. "Brilliant man. A little pale."
- **The Great Fire**: an enemy; it destroyed his Brotherhood records. A very thorough fire.

## Affiliations
- **Arcane Brotherhood**: graduate, top of his class (records lost in a fire).
- **Arklem Greeth**: former personal pupil.
- **House von Matterhorn**: younger son and finest ornament.

## Notable items
- **Spellbook**: small, leather-bound, never leaves his hand; written in a personal cipher years in the making. "Don't touch it. The ink is... temperamental." (The book is empty and has no ink.)
- **Pocket watch**: checked at every dramatic moment, always "right on schedule".
- **Amethyst brooch**: holds his violet silk cravat.
- **A ledger of favours owed**: the only book he actually writes in.
- **Clothes of Gleaming**: they never get dirty.
