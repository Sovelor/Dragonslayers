# Gourd

> [!warning] Spoilers
> Reveals Gourd's death in Part Two.

**Role:** Goblin raid leader, mini-boss
**Weapon:** Curved sword
**Appearance:** Larger, battle-scarred

## Summary
Leads a 6-goblin ambush on Badinga, Garpask, Gaston, and Mahaya at the felled-tree trap: 3 archers in trees, 2 with cleavers/hatchets hidden in bushes, plus Gourd himself. Gives the attack signal — "Krassnak!" ("Attack!" in Goblin). Defeated in the resulting fight.
