# Gaston Lentinson

**Role:** Merchant
**Backstory:** Fleeing extortion/gangs tied to Laskan, heading to Lonlywood
**Travels with:** Two mules, a cart, and his hired escort [[Mahaya]]

## Summary
Nervous, middle-aged merchant first encountered when Badinga and Garpask round a bend and find the road blocked by a deliberately-felled tree. He offers 10 gold each plus goods at good prices in exchange for help clearing the log and escorting the cart — though Badinga and Garpask would have helped regardless.

## Character beats
- Anxious, hopeful the fallen tree was "just wind" (Mahaya knows better — axe marks all over it).
- Represents the ordinary-people-caught-in-danger throughline of Part Two.

> [!success] Status
> Alive, allied with the party as of the goblin ambush.
