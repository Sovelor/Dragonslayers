# Garpask Losark

**Role:** Young sorcerer — innate magic, not studied
**Origin:** Laskan
**Notable trait:** Hints of dragon blood

## Summary
Rescued from wild dogs early in Part One (*A Strange Acquaintance*), joining [[Badinga Alpenrok]] and [[Elizium]]. After Elizium's death, travels on with Badinga and becomes the group's diplomat/voice during the standoff with [[Gaston Lentinson]] and [[Mahaya]].

## Character beats
- Physically scrawny and weaponless — Mahaya is initially puzzled about what he brings to a fight, not realizing his sorcery.
- Surprisingly skilled diplomat: de-escalates the tense standoff with Gaston and Mahaya using calm, formal speech, genuinely impressing Badinga.
- Plays along with Badinga's cover story about Elizium's death to strangers.

> Alive, active party member.
