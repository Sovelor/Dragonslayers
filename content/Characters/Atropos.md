# Atropos

**Role:** One of three Fate-sisters
**First appearance:** Opening scene of the manuscript, before the Prologue — POV character
**Setting:** [[The Void]]
**Appearance:** Aged, hooded crone; crimson glowing eyes; accompanied by familiars — a wolf, a raven, and a serpent

## Summary
Opens the entire saga from her own POV, plotting to break free of the Void. A framing/mythic presence rather than a party-facing character.
